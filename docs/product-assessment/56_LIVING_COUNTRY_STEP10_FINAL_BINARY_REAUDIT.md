# 2. Blocking finding R10-01 is closed

## 2.1 Generated backstory is now temporally fenced

The repaired contract requires the conceptual order:

```text
source/configuration package
→ inherited baseline
→ generated actors/backstory
→ backstory package frozen
→ forward-generated prehistory
→ canonical opening selected
→ player era
```

A generator cannot run the political history, inspect the 2033 coalition, and then add a pre-2025 betrayal, promise, or reputation to explain it.

Changing the backstory package after forward outcomes exist creates a new candidate world and requires regeneration or an explicit owner-respecting generation migration.

The old candidate remains noncanonical developer evidence.

## 2.2 Root scope is closed

A generated-backstory root may apply only to facts whose effective origin lies at or before its declared boundary.

A fact created or materially changed after that boundary belongs to forward-generated prehistory or the player era.

Backdating the fact does not change its root class.

## 2.3 Bare roots are limited to actual background state

The repair permits bounded synthetic background where no supported consumer needs a specific origin, potentially including:

- identity and education/profession;
- general ideology or temperament;
- non-event-specific affinity or distrust;
- broad prior reputation with synthetic status;
- organization mission or culture;
- a preexisting broad interest.

These roots retain explanation limits.

A broad root can support:

> This association predates the detailed generated record.

It cannot support an invented exact event.

## 2.4 Event-dependent facts require generated history

A compressed or full occurrence/activity chain is now mandatory when a current fact semantically depends on an identifiable:

- election, appointment, office transfer, vote, law, ruling, order, payment, or program act;
- promise, offer, threat, bargain, betrayal, favor, endorsement, or commitment;
- relationship change caused by specific conduct;
- actor memory of a specific event;
- public or party reputation attributed to historical performance, evidence, communication, or recipient response;
- legal, fiscal, material, evidentiary, procedural, or organization consequence.

The chain may be compressed.

It may not be replaced by a trait.

## 2.5 Office and career legitimacy survives compression

A generated career summary may remain coarse when only broad background matters.

When prior office affects seniority, eligibility, relationships, public record, or legitimacy, its election/appointment/assignment route must be retained at the accepted resolution.

This prevents:

```text
Senator served as governor
→ exact statewide relationships and legitimacy
```

without the supporting generated route.

## 2.6 No root laundering during compression

Forward-generated history can later be summarized, but its root class and occurrence lineage remain forward-generated.

Compression cannot turn a 2029 bargain into `preexisting relationship`, or a 2030 election into `generated background`, merely because the exact sequence is no longer stored at full resolution.

# 3. Clarification C10-02 is closed: three historical clocks remain distinct

The repaired composite distinguishes:

1. **world-state/divergence seam**;
2. **external source-compilation cutoff**;
3. **in-world evidence and actor-knowledge availability**.

This resolves a difficult calibration case.

A source published after the divergence seam may improve the scenario builder's estimate of a condition as of the seam when its reference period and retrospective use are declared.

It does not become evidence available to actors before its historical publication.

It also cannot be used to import actual post-divergence events, decisions, election outcomes, or future information into the generated timeline.

Thus:

```text
later benchmark improves hidden estimate of T0
≠ historical actors knew benchmark at T0
≠ generated T0–T1 actors may condition choices on it
```

The authority can therefore support a historically closed scenario whose state reference date and source compilation date differ, without granting omniscience or future leakage.

# 4. Clarification C10-03 is closed: composite facts require full semantic coverage

The repair replaces a weak reading of:

```text
fact has at least one provenance root
```

with:

> A composite fact must reconcile across the complete contribution basis its owner requires at the accepted resolution.

For an accounting stock, that may include:

```text
opening root
+ inflows
- outflows
± transfers
± revaluations/reclassifications
± residual and uncertainty
= current state
```

For population, it may include opening population, births, deaths, immigration, emigration, and internal redistribution.

For a program, it may include authority, amendments, funding, responsible institution, obligations, participation, and active constraints.

The contract does not demand microscopic history.

It does demand a complete semantic basis, including explicit residuals and unknowns.

Step 6's contribution-lineage, overlap, transfer, gross/net, reversal, and accounting rules remain controlling.

---

# 5. Clarification C10-04 is closed: compressed history cannot masquerade as one occurrence

The repaired taxonomy distinguishes:

- **canonical occurrence**;
- **compressed process bundle**;
- **aggregate quantity**;
- **historical summary projection**.

A one-ID bundle containing forty consultations is not one consultation.

It may support a broad activity estimate or reputation assessment.

It cannot support an exact promise, receipt, meeting, legal notice, memory, or deadline unless that occurrence was separately retained.

A later feature cannot unpack a bundle into invented exact events.

When exact history was not preserved, the answer remains modeled, bounded, unsupported, or unknown.

---

# 6. Clarification C10-05 is closed: generated worlds cannot be patched editorially

A candidate world that fails reconciliation may now be:

- rejected and regenerated;
- resumed from a valid checkpoint through owner-resolved processes;
- regenerated from a newly versioned backstory/configuration package;
- explicitly migrated while preserving owners, occurrences, identities, times, and contribution lineage;
- retained as unresolved only where the abstraction genuinely permits uncertainty.

It may not be fixed by changing an office assignment, fiscal number, actor memory, legal effect, or material outcome while leaving the incompatible history in place.

A repaired candidate is a new candidate identity/version.

The abandoned candidate remains noncanonical.

This prevents reconciliation from becoming a hidden editor that makes any generated history pass after the fact.
