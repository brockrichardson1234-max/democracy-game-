# 2. Blocking finding R8-01 is closed

## 2.1 One human remains one person

The repair now requires one underlying human identity and one Step-4 population-linkage status for every effective interval, even when that human also has:

- an individualized actor overlay;
- an office assignment;
- an organization or campaign role;
- one or more platform accounts;
- an outlet subscription;
- a source relationship;
- an official or classified recipient role.

Those are contexts, relationships, accounts, or assignments. They do not create additional people, population weight, voters, or independent minds.

The invalid state is now closed:

```text
one senator
→ one Population-linked person
→ one actor object
→ one office assignment
→ one platform account
→ four independently counted recipients
```

The contexts may receive different presentations and retain different access-bounded records. They cannot duplicate the human.

## 2.2 Recipient context is explicit

Every load-bearing presentation, delivery, notice, memory, or belief transition concerning an individualized human must be traceable to:

- underlying human identity or linkage status;
- immediate recipient surface/context;
- access/classification basis;
- artifact or proposition;
- channel, account, office, or relationship;
- occurrence identity;
- support level;
- state owner being updated;
- cross-context transfer rule;
- overlap with aggregate audiences;
- effective time.

This is sufficient to distinguish:

- private personal encounter;
- office receipt;
- actor-private memory;
- organization receipt;
- campaign knowledge;
- modeled population exposure.

## 2.3 Role knowledge cannot leak automatically

A classified or privileged office delivery may update:

- office receipt;
- official records;
- actor-private role knowledge;
- authorized staff assessment.

It does not automatically update:

- public Population belief;
- household/social-network knowledge;
- platform engagement;
- public salience;
- ordinary-person exposure.

A later disclosure, leak, statement, forwarding action, or public release requires a separate canonical act and information route.

The reverse is also closed. A senator privately reading a public article does not make the Senate office, committee staff, party caucus, or agency officially aware of it. Institutional propagation requires a real communication or record.

## 2.4 Personal knowledge can still affect the actor

The repair does not impose artificial amnesia.

A public presentation may produce one exact human notice. That personally known information may later be consumed by the same human's actor decision process where the actor legitimately acts from private knowledge.

This is not a second exposure or automatic office receipt.

The valid form is:

```text
public presentation
→ exact human notice
→ personal/actor-private memory
→ later actor decision uses personally known information
```

If staff or the institution must know, another transfer occurrence is required.

## 2.5 Statistical linkage remains honest

An actor with `STATISTICALLY_REPRESENTED_WITHOUT_EXACT_IDENTITY` may no longer inherit exact personal:

- delivery;
- notice;
- memory;
- trust;
- belief;
- attribution;
- salience;
- channel use

from aggregate audience state.

A modeled result that 65 percent of a district audience received a story does not establish that a named statistically represented senator saw it.

The personal answer remains independently evidenced, modeled, bounded, unsupported, or unknown.

## 2.6 Exact and aggregate exposure reconcile

When an exact named person lies inside an aggregate audience, the design must declare whether the aggregate:

- excludes exact carve-outs;
- includes the person without resolving personal exposure;
- is reconciled through a joint allocation;
- remains modeled or bounded with uncertain membership.

The exact person cannot be added again to unique reach merely because an exact delivery is also recorded.

This binds Step 3's overlap/support rules to Step 8's information pathways.

## 2.7 One occurrence cannot be consumed through aliases

The repair establishes semantic idempotency over:

```text
recipient identity/context
+ presentation or delivery occurrence
+ proposition/content target
+ cognitive stage
```

The same delivery appearing in a briefing, dossier, media view, workstream, and historical record remains one delivery.

Those projections do not create five exposures or five belief updates.

## 2.8 Distinct presentations remain valid repetition

The repair does not overcorrect by collapsing all repeated information.

The same person may legitimately:

- see a feed headline;
- hear a television segment;
- receive a friend's message;
- later receive an office packet.

Those are distinct presentation occurrences with shared artifact or proposition lineage.

They may create familiarity, source confusion, reinforcement, contradiction, or correction exposure. They do not automatically become independent evidence.

# 4. Clarification C8-02 is closed: exposure terminology

The repair assigns closed meanings:

- **availability** — artifact can be accessed through a channel under stated conditions;
- **presentation/delivery** — channel or sender places/transmits content onto a recipient surface or scope;
- **exposure opportunity** — content is perceptible to the declared recipient under the represented context;
- **notice** — recipient minimally perceives the content;
- **attention** — recipient allocates further cognitive processing;
- **engagement** — separately owned behavior such as click, share, comment, search, or view duration.

`InformationEnvironment` may own availability, presentation/delivery, and exposure opportunity.

Recipient owners retain notice, attention, comprehension, memory, trust, belief, attribution, and salience.

A load-bearing contract may no longer use the word `exposure` without declaring the intended stage.

---

# 5. Clarification C8-03 is closed: proposition identity is not truth

A proposition identity now names the semantic target of an assertion, denial, prediction, attribution, question, or belief.

It does not own truth.

Truth remains distributed among the actual semantic owners of the underlying facts.

Evidence support remains with Step 7 processes and artifacts.

Relations such as:

- supports;
- contradicts;
- corrects;
- qualifies;
- falsifies

require an owner, evidence, method, time, and access status. They are not automatic omniscient edges.

A proposition registry may support identity, lineage, duplicate detection, and navigation. It may not become a global fact checker that reads canonical reality and labels every claim for actors or the player.

# 6. Clarification C8-04 is closed: moderation and fact-check annotations

A platform label, context panel, fact-check link, recommendation restriction, or authenticity warning remains decomposed into:

1. platform/eligible-actor decision;
2. annotation or linked artifact;
3. supporting evidence and claimed assessment;
4. distribution/ranking consequence;
5. recipient exposure opportunity;
6. recipient notice and interpretation.

The label does not:

- change underlying truth;
- prove the platform correct;
- guarantee presentation;
- guarantee notice;
- set belief;
- erase the original artifact or prior exposure.

This closes the risk that `FACT_CHECKED` becomes a magic truth or belief flag.

# 7. Clarification C8-05 is closed: disposition is not action

The repaired contract separates:

- willingness;
- intention;
- preference;
- propensity;
- readiness

from later:

- sharing;

The later act belongs to the appropriate actor, population process, organization, election, market, or institution.

This preserves Step 4's belief/objective/intention/decision/action/result distinction.

# 8. Original Step 8 gate attack

## 8.1 Reality ownership attack — PASS

A story, claim, platform label, trend, or recipient belief cannot create underlying corruption, employment, prices, law, administrative failure, or election result.

## 8.2 Duplicate-evidence attack — PASS

Derivative artifacts retain proposition, source, and evidence lineage. Repetition can affect familiarity or salience without becoming independent methodological confirmation.

## 8.3 Universal-exposure attack — PASS

Publication, availability, presentation, exposure opportunity, and notice remain separate. Audiences use exact/modeled/bounded/unsupported support.

## 8.4 Global-attention/credibility/virality attack — PASS

The design permits recipient-specific attention, relational/topic-specific trust, and derived diffusion/trending projections. No global scalar causally commands outlets and audiences.

## 8.5 Scripted-scandal attack — PASS

Journalism begins with a valid lead or observable anomaly, consumes scarce resources, obtains evidence, and may publish, delay, narrow, or abandon. It cannot generate underlying wrongdoing.

## 8.6 Omniscient-platform attack — PASS

Platforms may use held records, behavior, proxies, policies, and models. They cannot read exact hidden belief or future virality.

## 8.7 Perfect-analyst attack — PASS

Actors may misuse evidence, overstate confidence, assume false independence, cherry-pick, or deceive. Actual methodological support remains separately honest.

## 8.8 Direct-approval/election attack — PASS

Speech, publication, exposure, belief, preference, turnout disposition, ballot, count, certification, and office transfer remain separately resolved.

## 8.9 Correction-rewind attack — PASS

Corrections and retractions add artifacts and history. They do not erase prior exposure, memory, decisions, or political consequences.

## 8.10 Bot-population attack — PASS

Accounts and coordinated operations do not add citizen or voter weight without valid human/population linkage.

## 8.11 Hidden administration fan-out attack — PASS

A presidential communication choice must decompose into only the distinct instruments knowingly authorized. Media and recipient outcomes remain autonomous.

## 8.12 Recipient-alias attack — PASS

The repair closes duplicate human representation, context leakage, projection aliases, aggregate/exact overlap, and unsupported named-person exposure.

# 9. Adversarial proof review

## 9.1 Local investigation becomes national — PASS

The path requires records, journalistic initiative, evidence, publication, local exposure, institutional pickup, further reporting, and valid White House receipt. Nationalization is not guaranteed.

## 9.2 Preliminary jobs report and revision — PASS

Actual employment, preliminary estimate, claims, framing, recipient belief, later revision, correction exposure, and historical evidence-at-the-time remain distinct.

## 9.3 Viral false causal claim — PASS

The claim can use real evidence while asserting unsupported attribution. Diffusion is fragmented, correction is uneven, and belief does not alter canonical prices or directly set votes.

## 9.4 Foreign influence operation — PASS

Content creation, account coordination, platform distribution, detection, intelligence assessment, government response, media interpretation, public belief, and elections remain separately owned.

## 9.5 Quiet serious condition — PASS

A severe regional condition may remain weakly measured, underreported, locally experienced, and absent from presidential attention without a drama director forcing coverage.

## 9.6 Presidential communication bundle — PASS

The President may authorize a bounded bundle of evidence release, speech, briefing, and distribution attempts. Staff cannot silently add an agency order, congressional commitment, admission, policy change, or advertising campaign.
