# 1. Closed design question

The Living Country program asked:

> **What living United States must exist beneath the accepted presidential strategy game, and what semantic rules must let that country remain causally coherent, autonomous, historically persistent, imperfectly observed, politically contested, player-legible, and bounded by what the President can legitimately know?**

That design question is now closed at constitutional-contract level.

The final accepted answer is not one architecture, algorithm, ontology, UI, or simulation engine.

It is a set of semantic obligations that later product and runtime work must satisfy.

---

# 2. What the closed constitution establishes

The accepted composite establishes, at design-contract level:

## 2.1 Country ownership

- every load-bearing mutable fact has one semantic owner;
- occurrences retain one canonical occurrence identity;
- projections, workstreams, issues, dossiers, dashboards, histories, and UI views do not become shadow owners;
- one entity or human is not duplicated because several roles or policy lenses reference it.

## 2.2 Material/social country

- materially different domains share a causal handshake rather than one internal schema;
- fact forms, units, time, geography, population scope, provenance, and uncertainty remain explicit;
- one weighted population substrate supports household continuity, sparse domain associations, and multiple geographic frames;
- mechanism depth is assigned to owned mechanisms rather than whole policy labels;
- direct cross-domain modifier chains are prohibited;
- source owners transmit facts; receiving owners determine consequences.

## 2.3 Actors and institutions

- individuals, offices, office assignments, institutions, organizations, procedures, and aggregates remain distinct;
- actors decide from bounded information, role, authority, objectives, relationships, resources, duties, and institution-specific action spaces;
- organization actions require real governance/coordination;
- no universal utility, influence, power, staff-quality, or political-capital meter owns behavior.

## 2.4 Presidency and administration

- the country continues without the player;
- the office persists while the officeholder interprets and acts;
- the administration is a federation of persistent offices, records, queues, delegations, staff, authority and decision processes rather than one Staff actor;
- inbound country information and outbound presidential decisions each travel through explicit owners;
- presidential choices produce typed acts/instruments rather than hidden fan-out;
- agencies, Congress, states, courts, organizations and material systems resolve their own portions independently.

## 2.5 Information and measurement

- canonical reality, observation, estimate, release, interpretation and belief remain separate;
- evidence carries universe, method, support, uncertainty, vintage and dependency lineage;
- revisions do not rewrite earlier artifacts or decisions;
- forecasts cannot read future canonical state;
- polling measures rather than owns population political state;
- intelligence likelihood and analytic confidence remain distinct.

## 2.6 Media and public belief

- autonomous speakers and media actors discover/select evidence, frame claims, investigate, publish, distribute, correct or remain silent;
- reality does not originate from media need;
- availability, presentation, exposure opportunity, notice, attention, comprehension, memory, trust, belief and action remain distinct;
- one human remains one human across private, actor, officeholder, subscriber and platform contexts;
- derivative stories may multiply exposure without multiplying independent evidence.

## 2.7 Political pressure and issues

- political issues are contextual projections, not canonical world objects;
- public, media, party, congressional, state, organization and White House agendas may diverge;
- material severity, public salience, organization, media attention, pressure, urgency, feasibility, electoral consequence and presidential attention remain separate;
- pressure is grounded in actual attempts, receipts, consequences and target interpretation rather than one score;
- issue split/merge/reframe does not clone recipient cognition or underlying facts;
- political importance cannot manufacture legal authority, fiscal authority, jurisdiction or procedure.

## 2.8 Historical America

- historical completeness means causal completeness for load-bearing state, not chronological simulation of everything since 1789;
- provenance roots may be baseline-inherited, generated backstory, forward-generated prehistory or player-era;
- generated backstory freezes before forward prehistory and cannot be written backward from desired later outcomes;
- current stocks/obligations reconcile through full contribution coverage rather than one convenient ancestor;
- occurrence, compressed process bundle, aggregate quantity and historical summary remain distinct;
- historically closed baseline state hands off to live owners rather than shadow-owning future state.

## 2.9 Calibration and generated prehistory

- the accepted commercial-history seam is the Early-2025 Transition Package with primary live generated alternate-history handoff at noon ET January 20, 2025;
- January 3 congressional seating and January 6 electoral counting remain generated/frozen causal transition history rather than disappearing;
- later real-world developments do not update an existing generated run;
- the 2024 election/transition package is inside the generator proof, not a handcrafted prologue;
- generated prehistory must permit quiet, mediocre, frustrating and volatile histories;
- January 2029 is the mandatory first credibility horizon, not yet the accepted commercial start;
- January 2033 remains conditional on extra history producing meaningful inherited gameplay and on a coherent 2030 census/apportionment/redistricting/2032 House chain.

## 2.10 Cross-layer material stress

- demand intention, realized exchange, consumption, production and seller revenue remain distinct;
- prices remain independently owned;
- monetary transmission respects instrument, maturity, contract and refinancing differences;
- exact overlays conserve aggregate state;
- federal payment does not pre-resolve state or material success;
- evidence revisions do not alter material history;
- political salience does not deepen a material system or create authority;
- severe conditions can remain politically quiet and legitimately un-escalated.

## 2.11 Player legibility and bounded knowledge

- Overview → Detail → Record increases explanation, not omniscience;
- Presidential Attention, Workstreams, Country Watch/State of the Nation, Dossiers, Evidence and Historical Record have distinct semantic roles;
- source possession, White House notice, retrievability, office receipt, presidential presentation and personal memory remain separate;
- Country Watch is not a federal debug database;
- public availability does not equal Presidential knowledge;
- substantive notification previews count as presentation;
- background technical prefetch/cache does not count as semantic presentation merely because software loaded bytes;
- maps preserve support and geographic vintage;
- staff synthesis does not erase office disagreement;
- the UI can truthfully display `nothing currently requires presidential action`.

## 2.12 Live branch knowledge

- presentation history is immutable;
- unread/read status is navigation state rather than memory;
- save/load restores same-history knowledge without rerunning searches, retrievals, notifications or reads;
- rollback restores branch-bounded canonical knowledge without pretending the human forgot abandoned futures;
- abandoned branches cannot leak into AI, forecasts, search, dossiers, RNG or active history;
- same-President reelection preserves personal memory;
- a different incoming President inherits records/access/briefings rather than predecessor private memory;
- declassification/access revocation changes current access/disclosure rather than rewriting historical knowledge.

# 3. Final systemic doctrine

The closed Living Country constitution can be summarized as:

```text
WORLD TRUTH
has exact semantic owners
and persists whether or not the President knows it

ACTORS / INSTITUTIONS
observe bounded portions
and act through their own authority, resources and procedures

CAUSAL HANDOFFS
transmit typed upstream facts
but receivers own downstream transformation

EVIDENCE
is produced imperfectly, historically and with uncertainty

MEDIA / POLITICS
select, frame, distribute, interpret, organize and contest
without owning underlying truth or elections

ADMINISTRATION
receives bounded information, coordinates work,
and escalates only what legitimately needs presidential judgment

PRESIDENT
chooses typed acts, delegation, communication, negotiation,
review, delay or inaction
without owning other actors' responses or material success

HISTORY
remembers what actually happened and what was known when

PLAYER INTERFACE
shows bounded projections over that history and current information state
without becoming an omniscient dashboard
```

The product center remains:

> **The country keeps moving. You decide what deserves the President.**

# 4. Final negative doctrine

The closed constitution intentionally permits:

```text
serious condition
→ real harm
→ weak/fragmented evidence
→ little organization
→ little national coverage
→ no valid White House escalation
→ no Presidential decision
```

It also permits:

```text
politically loud controversy
→ strong coverage and organization
→ weak or narrow underlying material magnitude
→ substantial presidential political pressure
```

And:

```text
President chooses major policy
→ typed government acts
→ Congress/agencies/states/courts independently respond
→ material outcomes may succeed, fail, delay or diverge
```

These are features of the accepted design, not missing quest generation.

# 7. Post-closure evidence direction

The final Step-17 audit recommends the following broad evidence sequence, but this closure does **not** make it roadmap authority:

1. a small playable presidential information/attention prototype;
2. one end-to-end causal vertical with revisions, delegation, save/load and Historical Record;
3. a generated 2024→2029 multi-seed prehistory experiment;
4. an evidence-based January 2029 versus January 2033 decision;
5. supported-scope / Early Access selection;
6. implementation roadmap after those proofs.

The purpose is to cash out the accepted constitution into evidence.

Future work should increasingly answer questions such as:

- Can a human understand this quickly?
- Does bounded knowledge feel strategic rather than annoying?
- Can the simulation run affordably?
- Do generated histories feel coherent and non-synthetic?
- Do mixed mechanisms produce surprising but explainable outcomes?
- Does presidential attention produce compelling choices rather than administrative noise?
- Is January 2033 actually richer than January 2029 enough to justify its burden?
- What is the minimum product worth selling?
