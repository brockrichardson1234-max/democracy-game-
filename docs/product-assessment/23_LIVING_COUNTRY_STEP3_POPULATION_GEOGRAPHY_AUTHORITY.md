# 2. Accepted central answer

The Living Country population/geography substrate is accepted conceptually as:

> **One household-aware weighted population fabric with stable identity and lineage; domain-owned sparse associations; shared persistent population distinctions only when named supported processes must follow the same affected people; and typed, versioned, uncertainty-bearing relationships among residence, workplace, service, market, network, media, event, jurisdictional, and electoral geographies.**

The substrate is neither:

- 340 million individually simulated citizen objects;
- nor one global Cartesian cohort table containing every demographic, economic, Housing, health, media, belief, and geographic combination.

---

# 3. One canonical ordinary population

There is one canonical ordinary-population identity, represented-weight, residence, household/co-residential, lineage, and recipient-political continuity system.

Workers, patients, students, renters, homeowners, taxpayers, beneficiaries, audiences, voters, disaster victims, immigrants, and energy customers remain roles, states, relations, associations, or projections concerning that population.

No domain may create a separately mutable population whose identities, weights, residence, or history can diverge from the canonical population.

The one-population rule does not assign all facts about people to PopulationState.

---

# 4. PopulationState-owned facts versus domain-owned facts

PopulationState may own, where later accepted at the required resolution:

- represented person and household identity/weight;
- residence and movement continuity;
- household/co-residential membership and member roles;
- group-quarters relation;
- selected core demographic continuity facts;
- recipient-owned belief, attribution, salience, preference, memory, and turnout disposition;
- refinement/lineage state.

Facts remain with their semantic domains when that is what they are, including potential:

- employment, jobs, occupation, hours, and labor-force state;
- earnings, income, resources, assets, and debt;
- Housing stock, occupancy, tenure, rents, mortgages, and cost exposure;
- health conditions, coverage, provider use, and service state;
- education enrollment/attainment;
- program/administrative state;
- information delivery;
- ballots and election results.

A fact does not enter PopulationState merely because several systems query it or because it describes a person.

---

# 5. Household and group-quarters contract

The substrate must support a canonical co-residential household relationship sufficient for accepted cross-domain processes.

Household context may be consumed by Housing, income, taxation, benefits, employer coverage, health, education, migration, direct experience, and political processes.

Co-residential household, family/kinship, tax unit, benefit unit, insurance unit, consumption unit, and voter unit are not universal synonyms.

Non-household/group-quarters populations must remain representable where causally required.

No complete genealogy or universal legal-household model is accepted by this authority.

---

# 6. Dual person and household accounting

A household-aware weighted representation must distinguish:

- represented private-household weight;
- represented persons by member role/class;
- group-quarters person weight;
- total represented population.

Member multiplicity/distribution or another declared relation reconciles household and person weights.

Births, deaths, household formation/dissolution, member entry/exit, migration, splitting, and coarsening must identify which quantities change.

A member death does not automatically remove a household. Household formation does not create people. Movement does not change the national population total.

---

# 7. Population-scope taxonomy

The informal word `scope` may not hide several incompatible ownership patterns.

Accepted conceptual categories are:

1. **Population-owned exact scope or partition relation**
   - conserved subset identified through Population-owned identity, lineage, residence, household, or other accepted core facts;
2. **Domain-owned association allocation**
   - domain fact allocated over population references; not universal population membership;
3. **Modeled or bounded scope artifact**
   - estimate/distribution with controls, method, uncertainty, geography, and as-of time;
4. **Ephemeral derived query**
   - noncanonical read result;
5. **Occurrence-linked affected scope**
   - stable historical reference to the population affected by one occurrence, derived from one of the preceding categories.

Every load-bearing scope must declare its semantic owner/category, source lineage, weight unit, effective time, support status, allocation derivation, overlap/partition semantics, and reuse permissions.

Equal weight is never proof that two scopes contain the same people.

---

# 8. Partition, overlap, and aggregation

An exact complete partition must conserve its parent weight.

An overlapping selection is not a disjoint partition.

Overlapping scopes may not be added as unique population without resolving, modeling, bounding, or excluding their intersection.

A scope’s as-of membership and weight do not mutate in place to follow later population change. A new version/current relation is created; historical affected-population identity remains stable.

---

# 9. Domain-owned sparse associations

Material/social domains may partition or associate the same canonical population differently.

A domain association must carry, where applicable:

- population carrier/scope reference;
- represented person or household quantity and unit;
- time/interval;
- domain state;
- geography/entity relation;
- provenance and uncertainty;
- domain reconciliation rule.

A domain allocation is not a new population and cannot be imported by another owner as exact shared membership without a Population-owned exact scope, shared occurrence-linked scope, or explicit modeled/bounded mapping.

Domain-local distinctions remain local unless cross-owner identity must persist.

---

# 10. Joint-support honesty

A cross-domain or geographic population query must declare one of:

1. exactly represented;
2. modeled with provenance;
3. bounded but unresolved;
4. unsupported.

Separate marginals may not be multiplied into exact joint truth unless independence is explicitly modeled, evidenced, and classified accordingly.

Unknown, unsupported, modeled, bounded, and zero remain distinct.

Player-facing and staff projections must preserve the epistemic status.

---

# 11. Persistent-correlation admission

A population correlation becomes shared persistent structure only when a named supported process requires it.

The correlation obligation must identify:

- the process, coupling, measurement, election, eligibility rule, or player-information query;
- consuming owners;
- why domain-local state or separate marginals are insufficient;
- required support status;
- effective duration;
- source evidence or occurrence;
- uncertainty;
- retirement/coarsening condition.

UI filtering, one report, one published story, speculative realism, or “might matter someday” is insufficient.

The deletion principle remains:

> **Preserve correlations that can return through supported causal processes as different outcomes, observations, actions, interpretations, constraints, or presidential decisions. Compress the rest.**

---

# 12. Information and material exposure

Publication or distribution does not itself split the global population.

Information availability/delivery may target population scopes. Recipient incorporation may create purpose-bounded belief/memory state.

A shared persistent split is required only when supported later processes need to retain the same recipient divergence.

Likewise, a project catchment or material-exposure scope may remain domain-local unless another owner must follow the same affected people.

The current I8 pattern of exposure references inside global cohort identity remains valid for that bounded proof but is not accepted as the universal full-media representation.

---

# 13. Refinement and lineage

Every canonical population refinement must preserve:

- parent and child identities;
- represented person and/or household weights;
- cause and occurrence identity;
- effective time;
- allocation method/evidence;
- inherited and changed Population-owned state;
- valid domain associations;
- uncertainty;
- conservation and reconciliation.

Refinement itself does not create or destroy people.

Birth, death, immigration, emigration, and other actual population transitions remain separate.

---

# 14. Non-omniscient coarsening

Current refinements may be coarsened only against a declared supported-consumer closure set, including:

- active product/configuration process contracts;
- active/scheduled transitions;
- unresolved legal/program/domain/household obligations;
- current associations;
- recipient state consumed by supported political/electoral processes;
- declared measurement, election, eligibility, and query requirements;
- save/history reference requirements.

Coarsening may not inspect future random draws, actor choices, player choices, shocks, stories, or election outcomes.

Equivalent current worlds with different hidden future randomness make the same coarsening decision.

History remains preserved when current representation coarsens.

A later product feature cannot manufacture exact historical identity that was not retained; it must use prospective refinement, a modeled/bounded reconstruction, or an unsupported answer.

---

# 15. Demographic and lifecycle accounting

Population totals and household relationships change only through declared transitions with canonical occurrence identity and reconciliation.

The substrate must support, at whatever later resolution is accepted:

- aging;
- births;
- deaths;
- immigration and emigration;
- internal migration;
- household formation/dissolution;
- member entry/exit;
- group-quarters transitions.

At the supported accounting level:

```text
opening population
+ births
- deaths
+ immigration
- emigration
= closing population
```

Internal migration redistributes residence without changing national total.

Exact transition formulas are not accepted here.

---

# 16. Domain-association closure

A population transition that creates, removes, splits, merges, retires, or replaces population identity must produce lineage/replacement provenance.

Domain owners independently resolve continuation, transfer, reclassification, or termination of jobs, Housing, coverage, education, benefits, information, and other associations.

No settled current association may point to nonexistent, retired, or incompatible population identity without valid successor mapping.

Explicit pending reconciliation is permitted; silent settled contradiction is not.

Historical associations remain history.

---

# 17. Residence allocation

PopulationState owns current residence linkage/allocation.

At one residence resolution and as-of time, allocations across mutually exclusive residence supports must reconcile to the source person/household weight.

A carrier distributed over finer geographies is not fully resident in each.

Exact coarse residence and modeled fine residence can coexist when fine allocations reconcile and retain method, support status, and uncertainty.

Split/secondary residence or temporary absence may be represented when a supported process needs it, using explicit allocation/status rather than duplicate full person weight.

---

# 18. Multi-geography model

Living Country geography is a typed relationship system, not one universal tree.

Potential supported frames may include:

- legal/administrative;
- electoral;
- statistical;
- residence;
- workplace/labor market;
- Housing market;
- service/catchment;
- energy/grid/network;
- transport/supply;
- media;
- project/program;
- disaster/outage/security event.

Step 3 accepts the capability for such differences, not the final instantiated list.

Geographic entities and relationships carry effective-time/vintage semantics where they change.

Boundary change does not imply population movement or rewrite past elections.

---

# 19. Geographic and crosswalk ownership

GeographyState may own:

- spatial identity/type;
- geometry/support;
- vintage/effective time;
- exact spatial containment and geometric overlap;
- adjacency/topology;
- stable spatial references.

Relevant domains/institutions own operational relationships such as service assignment, grid membership, workplace relation, provider network, media-market operation, program catchment, and supply routing.

Population-, household-, job-, Housing-, service-, audience-, or exposure-weighted crosswalks are products of declared model/measurement/transformation processes referencing source-owner facts at an as-of time.

They are not intrinsic Geography current truth.

The receiving owner validates and owns its admitted allocation.

Area overlap does not automatically represent people, jobs, households, Housing units, voters, or service use.

---

# 20. Population query and knowledge contract

A population query may combine PopulationState, domain-owned associations, Geography, legal/program/electoral rules, and time context without moving ownership.

A load-bearing result must retain:

- source facts/owners;
- time/interval;
- geography/vintage;
- join/mapping method;
- support status;
- uncertainty;
- current/measurement/forecast/hypothetical status.

Simulation audit access does not grant the President omniscient access. Player knowledge still requires legitimate reports, measurements, records, staff analysis, or other information routes.

---

# 21. Electoral relationship

Electoral boundaries remain legal/electoral state referencing Geography.

Population residence remains Population-owned.

Electorates are derived for a contest/time from source facts and applicable law.

Eligibility is contextual, not one permanent `isVoter` field.

Redistricting changes boundaries and derived membership without moving people or rewriting historical elections.

The current state-level CVAP proxy remains accepted only as bounded I4/I5 evidence, not the final electorate model.

---

# 22. Source and calibration doctrine

Official surveys, administrative data, and geography products may inform population synthesis, controls, transitions, and relationships.

Source records do not become identifiable canonical simulated people.

PUMS may inform household/person joint structure; ACS small-area estimates may constrain modeled allocation; LODES may inform residence/workplace employment relationships; SIPP/CPS may inform transitions and labor/household dynamics.

Different sources’ universes, times, geographies, methods, and uncertainties must be reconciled or retained as residual/conflict—not forced into false exact agreement.

No one source is accepted as the complete population ontology, and no synthesis algorithm is selected here.

# 23. Application to current implementation

## Preserve

- one canonical weighted population;
- positive conserved represented weights;
- state/DC control totals;
- deterministic split/merge lineage;
- save/load persistence;
- no district-owned population;
- explicit source-artifact identity and limitations;
- bounded domain/information incorporation;
- rejection of merging causally different current state.

## Extend

- household and group-quarters relations;
- person/household dual accounting;
- demographic and migration flows;
- domain-owned sparse associations;
- scope taxonomy/support status;
- multi-frame geography and operational relations;
- weighted crosswalk provenance;
- selective refinement and non-omniscient coarsening;
- local/metro/district mapping where supported;
- lifecycle association closure.

## Reconsider before generalization

- a global cohort carrying every material exposure, information receipt, eligibility projection, and political state;
- splitting the global population for each story or project;
- requiring complete historical-reference equality for every future merge;
- state residence as sufficient for all domains;
- uniform state eligibility share across every subgroup;
- content strings as the permanent full-country population ontology.

No implementation migration is authorized by this disposition.
