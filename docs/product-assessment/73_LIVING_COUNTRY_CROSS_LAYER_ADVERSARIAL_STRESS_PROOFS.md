# 1. Executive verdict

## 1.1 Central answer

**[DI]** The assembled country is coherent on paper only if cross-layer proofs can trace every consequential step through a stable semantic mechanism/fact-family identity and its accepted owner, with no proof-only helper silently becoming a second owner.

The candidate therefore uses a bounded set of **proof aliases** for previously accepted semantic mechanism/fact families.

- may be referenced by several proofs;
- does not create another canonical mechanism registry;


No proof passes merely because its prose reaches a plausible endpoint.

## 1.2 The stress suite is perturbational

Each major proof includes at least one perturbation such as:

- remove the observation;
- change the measurement vintage;
- remove media amplification;
- change one actor's interpretation;
- remove a policy authority;
- alter a state response;
- change credit conditions;
- replace an exact overlay with aggregate state;
- remove a shock;
- change causal attribution;
- hold material state constant while political state changes;
- hold political coverage constant while material state changes.

If the proof still reaches the same endpoint for no declared reason, a hidden shortcut exists.

## 1.3 Step 14 may reveal prior-contract gaps

The purpose is adversarial.

The candidate identifies one such required clarification immediately:

> **Household-resource state and category consumption composition do not by themselves close the final-demand loop into production. A load-bearing household final-demand/expenditure-allocation mechanism must have one owner and enough structured/core behavior to convert available resources, prices, debt/credit conditions, expectations, and household composition into bounded demand inputs consumed by Production and related owners.**

This is a proposed Step-14 cross-layer repair, not yet accepted authority.

Without it, a hidden `EconomyHealth`, exogenous GDP path, or unexplained equilibrium could still choose output after household income and prices change.

# 3. Cross-layer stress principles

## 3.1 No hidden aggregate resolver

**[DI — HARD INVARIANT LC-STRESS02] A proof may not use a hidden national state such as `EconomyHealth`, `DemandStrength`, `NationalMood`, `CrisisIntensity`, or `PoliticalHeat` to resolve unrelated owner outcomes.**

Aggregates may be measurements/projections.

They may not be causal owners.

## 3.2 Final demand requires ownership

**[DI — HARD INVARIANT LC-STRESS03] Production may not infer household final demand directly from GDP, income, confidence, or a narrative recession label. Household expenditure/final-demand allocation must be owner-resolved from admitted household resources, category prices, balance-sheet/credit conditions, relevant expectations/preferences, and population structure at the supported resolution.**

The resulting demand input is transmitted to Production and relevant commodity/service owners.

This does not require household shopping simulation.

It requires a real causal bridge.

## 3.3 Price response requires ownership

**[DI — HARD INVARIANT LC-STRESS04] Category prices and producer/input prices are receiver-owned state. Cost, supply, capacity, demand, inventory, regulation, imports, and other admitted inputs may influence them, but no stress proof may insert a price change solely because a macro scenario expects inflation or recession.**

The exact price-formation algorithm remains unresolved.

## 3.4 Circular economy paths require explicit temporal semantics

A legitimate cycle may exist:

```text
production
→ labor income
→ household resources
→ final demand
→ production
```

or:

```text
rates/credit
→ Housing/investment/demand
→ production/labor/prices
→ evidence
→ Fed decision
→ rates/credit
```

The cycle must have lags, boundary ordering, periodic reconsideration, or a declared bounded solver.

No instantaneous recursive loop may settle according to handler order.

## 3.5 Nonlinearity belongs to receivers

Disjoint upstream contributions do not imply additive downstream response.

Eligibility thresholds, capacity, substitution, inventories, debt limits, household behavior, and institutional constraints may create interaction effects.

Contribution lineage supports overlap accounting; the receiver still owns the joint response.

## 3.6 No automatic politics

A material condition may remain unknown or politically quiet.

A politically salient issue may refer to a materially coarse mechanism.

Neither severity nor salience changes domain depth by itself.

**[DI — HARD INVARIANT LC-STRESS05] If an exact overlay represents state already included in an aggregate mechanism, its contribution may change the aggregate exactly once. Promotion to exact identity cannot add another copy of the plant/output/jobs/capacity, and demotion cannot erase or duplicate its contribution.**

The implementation may use:

- explicit carve-out;
- aggregate-with-component ledger;


The system may not invent an exact historical plant trajectory that contradicts or exceeds prior aggregate support.

## 5.4 Perturbations

### A2 — business investment channel removed

Hold `MECH.PROD.INVEST` constant.

- household-demand channel remains.

### A3 — preliminary measure revised upward

## 5.5 Pass conditions

- rates affect several receivers independently;
- household and business channels can differ;
- fiscal interest and private borrowing channels do not share one rate-effect modifier;
- measurement revision does not rewrite the material past;
- presidential attention requires valid administration receipt/escalation.
