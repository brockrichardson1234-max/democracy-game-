# 2. R11-01 is closed: January 3 versus January 20

## 2.1 The comparison is now explicit

The repaired composite compares both variants against the accepted criteria rather than assuming January 3 wins because it occurs first.

### B1

```text
January 3 live handoff
→ outgoing administration continues
→ new Congress begins
→ January 6 count resolves live
→ 17-day transition segment
→ January 20 executive transfer
→ first full generated presidential term
```

### B2

```text
frozen generated 2024 election/backstory
→ January 3 Congress transition retained
→ January 6 count retained
→ January 3–20 consequential activity retained
→ January 20 live handoff
→ first full generated presidential term begins immediately
```

Both preserve constitutional and institutional legitimacy.

B2 is cleaner for the presidency-centered product because forward prehistory starts with the complete first administration rather than an outgoing-administration transition fragment.

## 2.2 January 3 and January 6 are not erased

The repair does not make Congress begin at inauguration.

It requires generated occurrence history for:

- congressional election and entitlement;
- January 3 office assignment and organization;
- chamber leadership and load-bearing committee state;
- January 6 electoral-count procedure and result;
- January 3–20 consequential congressional, executive, legal, fiscal, administrative, material, and informational acts.

These remain part of the world's generated history before the primary live handoff.

## 2.3 The 17-day interval is not reduced to traits

A load-bearing January occurrence requires the same Step 10 provenance discipline as any other generated occurrence.

The package cannot replace:

- an electoral count with `winner confirmed`;
- congressional organization with a seat-total trait;
- a consequential bargain with a relationship score;
- a surviving outgoing-administration act with unexplained January 20 state;
- a transition disclosure with automatic incoming-administration knowledge.

Routine unconsumed detail may be compressed.

Consequential rights, obligations, receipts, decisions, and relationships must be retained.

## 2.4 Live prehistory begins with one coherent administration

At the January 20 handoff:

- presidential and vice-presidential entitlement has resolved;
- office assignment changes through its owner;
- the first live administration begins;
- the seated Congress already exists independently;
- institutional records, laws, obligations, cases, queues, and programs persist;
- knowledge transfer remains access-bounded;
- future appointments, legislation, implementation, material effects, public reaction, and elections remain unresolved.

This better supports two complete potential pre-player presidential terms without converting January 20 into a universal reset.

## 2.5 Named-consumer test is satisfied

The repair asks whether any accepted downstream consumer requires the January 3–20 interval to remain open-ended live simulation.

No such consumer is currently identified.

Every known consumer can be supported through retained transition history:

- office and congressional legitimacy;
- leadership and committee state;
- electoral count;
- campaign commitments;
- institutional records;
- inherited queues and obligations;
- January releases and receipts;
- consequential promises, contacts, proceedings, or crises if generated.

The selection is therefore based on required semantics rather than aesthetic convenience.

# 5. C11-03 is closed: retrospective sources are interval-specific

The repair distinguishes later-published sources whose reference interval:

- ends before the live seam;
- straddles the seam;
- begins after the seam;
- spans several years or changing universes;
- revises an earlier reference period.

## 5.1 Pre-seam reference period

A later-published source can improve hidden opening-state estimation only when:

- its reference period is genuinely pre-seam;
- its publication time remains separate;
- actor knowledge remains governed by actual release and receipt;
- transformation from period measure to seam-time state is declared where necessary;
- no post-seam political or material outcome is imported.

## 5.2 Straddling interval

A straddling source cannot be imported wholesale.

It requires:

- decomposition;
- a declared model;
- bounds;
- later validation-only use;
- or rejection.

## 5.3 Post-seam reference period

A post-seam outcome belongs to generated history or later evidence, not the opening baseline.

## 5.4 Multi-year estimate

A multi-year statistical estimate remains a period estimate under its method and geography.

It does not become an exact January 20 observation.

## 5.5 Actor knowledge

A later release or revision may improve current knowledge without becoming what an actor knew on January 20 or at an earlier generated decision.

The three-clock contract remains intact.
