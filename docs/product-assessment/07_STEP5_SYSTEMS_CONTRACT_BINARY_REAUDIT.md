# Binary verdict

## **REVISE**

The retained Step 5 thesis remains coherent and materially stronger after the addendum.

The repair now establishes credible product-level contracts for:

- resource-specific contention rather than universal Political Capital;
- information-bounded presidential attention;
- delegation, expiration, and inaction;
- separate decision discovery, option derivation, and outcome resolution;
- causal reconciliation and anti-nonsense requirements for generated prehistory;
- a multidimensional presidential record;
- autonomous-country requirements;
- locally observed cadence rather than an invented four-year quota.

Those are real advances.

However, the addendum does **not yet satisfy its own binary PASS condition**. Three load-bearing gaps remain:

1. autonomous non-player initiatives and discretionary staff escalation do not yet have a reusable causal grammar equivalent to the presidential situation-to-decision grammar;
2. several demonstrated attention stops, options, and counterfactual consequences do not contain the expiration/default or authority/route provenance the addendum requires of every demonstration;
3. the campaign feasibility envelope is established before candidate presentation but is not guaranteed to remain valid after the player makes mandate/campaign choices.

The precise conclusion is:

> **The repaired contracts are nearly coherent, but not every demonstrated presidency-level situation can yet be traced without a residual authored insertion or causal gap.**

The next work should repair only these failed contracts. No implementation, roadmap, Early Access, or Living Country work should begin yet.

# What passes cleanly

## P-01 — Resource-specific contention replaces the universal-resource thesis

The addendum names distinct owners and contested objects for:

- congressional committee, floor, amendment, confirmation, and appropriations opportunities;
- White House staff assignments and review deadlines;
- agency and OMB queues;
- presidential-calendar conflicts;
- actor commitments and remembered credibility;
- fiscal authority and obligations;
- legal authority and deadlines;
- communication channels, claims, and exposure.

The anti-spam tests correctly reject unlimited independent rolls from launching, contacting, accelerating, promising, or messaging everything.

This is sufficient to retain the thesis that overextension can emerge from contextual collisions rather than a universal Political Capital or attention pool.

## P-02 — Omniscient attention is prohibited at the product-contract level

The required chain is explicit:

```text
canonical occurrence
→ observable artifact or communication
→ administration receipt
→ bounded interpretation / escalation
→ presidential decision surface
```

The attention packet must identify its nominator, received artifact, knowledge, uncertainty, reason for escalation, deadline, and default.

The contract also correctly permits staff error and disagreement. Reality does not automatically speak to the President.

## P-03 — Deliberate inaction no longer freezes time by definition

The addendum establishes that every decision surface needs an expiration or default consequence, such as:

- standing policy continuing;
- delegated staff acting;
- an offer lapsing;
- a filing or appeal expiring;
- another actor proceeding;
- no communication being issued.

This is the correct product invariant and preserves inaction as a legitimate strategy.

## P-04 — Presidential option discovery and outcome resolution are properly separated

The situation-to-decision grammar distinguishes:

1. discovery through canonical state, observation, receipt, and escalation;
2. option derivation from authority, route, target, timing, prerequisites, constraints, uncertainty, and default;
3. autonomous outcome resolution after an administration attempt becomes canonical.

The Senate sequencing example demonstrates the intended standard well.

## P-05 — Generated prehistory has a validity contract rather than a false PASS claim

The addendum correctly:

- reclassifies Worlds A/B/C as world-shape exemplars;
- corrects World B’s duplicate 2032-winner contradiction;
- keeps January 2033 unproven;
- defines compressed ownership requirements;
- defines reconciliation across elections, offices, laws, fiscal state, implementation, courts, states, conditions, and political memories;
- requires causal provenance for every load-bearing briefing claim;
- lists explicit rejection tests.

This is enough for a future generated-prehistory proof to be falsifiable. It is not itself that proof, and the addendum no longer claims otherwise.

## P-06 — The presidency receives a legible record without one authoritative score

The proposed record separates promises, governing acts, material outcomes, implementation inheritance, fiscal inheritance, institutional effects, coalition history, elections, crises, and uncertainty of attribution.

This supports evaluation without a universal Presidency Score.

## P-07 — Cadence status is epistemically correct

The addendum withdraws the 100–160 decision assertion and uses three local paper traces instead.

The STOP / PROACTIVE / DEFAULT / BACKGROUND distinction is useful. The document correctly refuses to extrapolate those traces into a four-year count.

## P-08 — External research is substantially accurate and appropriately limited

The cited Pew and Gallup findings accurately support a modern calibration with strong partisan priors and internal coalition heterogeneity.

The bounded game comparisons are used as lessons or falsification contrasts rather than proof that this project already possesses those capabilities.

One minor source-label refinement remains below, but the research does not block the core contracts.

# Failed contracts

## R5-01 — HIGH — Non-player initiative and discretionary staff escalation still lack a causal grammar

### Finding

The addendum supplies a detailed presidential situation-to-decision grammar, but it does not supply an equivalent reusable grammar for other actors initiating major political action.

The autonomous-country section lists initiative families, and the counterfactual section provides examples such as:

- vulnerable senators creating an alternative employment package;
- a party-faction revolt replacing the cyber interruption;
- an autonomous congressional appropriations dispute replacing hurricane pressure;
- a bipartisan Senate group introducing a fiscal-restraint package;
- governors independently supporting and advancing that package.

These are desirable examples of a living country.

But their initiating step remains asserted rather than derived.

The design does not yet require the example to identify:

- what canonical condition, objective, promise, constituency exposure, faction priority, or institutional opportunity the non-player actor observed;
- what the actor believed or knew;
- what authority or position gave the actor a route;
- what alternative actions were available to that actor;
- why this actor acted now rather than earlier or not at all;
- what record captured the attempted initiative;
- how committees, leadership, members, states, or organizations independently admitted or rejected it.

Without this grammar, a designer or hidden scenario layer can still insert:

> “A bipartisan group introduces a fiscal package now.”

That insertion can then satisfy the rest of the presidential attention chain while remaining curated at its source.

### Related staff-escalation loophole

The attention contract permits **senior-staff judgment** as an escalation source. That is appropriate in principle, but the current wording can also hide a dramatic director inside a staff actor.

Naming an adviser and an artifact is not enough if the adviser may escalate any received fact because “staff judged it important.”

A discretionary staff escalation needs traceable inputs such as:

- staff role and jurisdiction;
- delegated objective or standing instruction;
- known presidential priorities;
- the adviser’s bounded interpretation and confidence;
- deadline or forecasted consequence;
- disagreement with other staff where relevant;
- the canonical escalation record.

### Why this fails the binary gate

The gate forbids curated narrative sequencing and requires autonomous actors.

The current addendum proves that other actors **may** initiate. It does not yet define how a demonstrated major initiative or discretionary escalation is generated from reusable actor state.

### Required bounded repair

Add one **Autonomous Actor Initiative and Staff Escalation Contract**:

```text
actor/institution receives or observes bounded state
→ actor interprets through role, objectives, constituency, commitments, beliefs, and current opportunities
→ actor derives legally/institutionally available attempts
→ actor chooses attempt, delay, delegation, or no action
→ attempt becomes canonical record
→ target/procedure resolves independently
→ administration learns only through valid information routes
```

For discretionary staff escalation:

```text
administration receipt
→ role/jurisdiction check
→ delegated-objective / standing-threshold / adviser-judgment basis
→ bounded assessment with confidence and known uncertainty
→ escalation or non-escalation record
```

The repair need not define implementation schema or AI algorithms. It must make the originating cause and admission rules mandatory for every non-player initiative used as product evidence.

## R5-02 — HIGH — The “exhaustive” traces and counterfactuals do not all satisfy the addendum’s own provenance/default requirements

### Finding

The addendum’s hard contract says every decision surface must have an expiration or default, and every option must expose authority, route, target, timing, prerequisites, constraints, uncertainty, and inaction.

Several demonstrations do not meet that standard.

### Trace A — August 5 Housing strategy memo

The player proactively requests a Housing review on August 1. On August 5, the resulting memo is classified as an automatic **STOP** with options to choose a route, continue preparation, or hold.

The trace does not specify:

- an expiration;
- what happens if the player ignores the memo;
- why the requested review becomes a mandatory stop rather than a completed proactive review;
- the authority/prerequisites for each offered route.

The likely default is that the prior Administration-led status continues, but the trace must say so or classify the review as PROACTIVE rather than a mandatory STOP.

### Trace C — August 25 emergency response

The storm generates a STOP for “emergency coordination posture and immediate authority/funding request.”

No expiration/default is defined. Standing emergency operations presumably continue, but the demonstration does not say what the administration or agencies do without presidential action.

### Trace C — August 26 batched crisis session

The batched session says the President resolves cross-agency conflicts and delegates thresholds, but it does not list:

- the actual conflicts;
- the available authority-grounded courses;
- the target owners;
- the default if the President does not resolve them.

This is a presidential stop by assertion rather than a complete demonstration under the addendum’s grammar.

### Trace C — August 29 supplemental-size request

Congressional leadership requests an administration position before a drafting deadline. The options are listed, but the default if no administration position is supplied is not explicit. The likely consequence is that Congress drafts without the requested position or the request lapses; the trace must identify which.

### Counterfactual Branch 14C — national rule loosening

The branch says the President loosens rules nationally, deployment accelerates, oversight risk rises, and later scandal/Inspector General risk has different provenance.

The demonstration does not identify:

- the legal authority permitting national rule change;
- procedural prerequisites;
- whether the change is guidance, waiver, rulemaking, enforcement priority, or legislation;
- which compliance records are removed or changed;
- which owner later observes possible misconduct.

The branch is plausible, but not yet provenance-complete.

### Conditional consequences without an owner

Several branches use formulations such as:

- a governor criticism or oversight outcome becomes “more likely”;
- a faction revolt “can become” the central interruption;
- vulnerable senators “can create” an alternative package;
- an autonomous appropriations dispute “can still” fill the political space.

These are appropriate hypotheses, but a demonstrated consequence cannot count as gate evidence until the initiating state, actor decision, and resolution path are named.

### Why this fails the binary gate

The audit question says **every demonstrated** situation, option, interruption, and consequence.

The generic grammar is strong, but the document’s own traces contain exceptions. Therefore the gate cannot return PASS yet.

### Required bounded repair

Add a compact provenance ledger—not another presidency vignette—for:

- every STOP in Traces A, B, and C;
- every explicit default;
- every option in the Decision 6, 14, and 19 forks;
- every claimed later consequence used to establish divergence.

Minimum columns:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Initiating owner/state | Who acted or what changed canonically |
| Observable artifact | What can reach the administration |
| Recipient/nominator | Who receives and escalates it |
| Escalation basis | Authority, delegation threshold, known window, or bounded staff judgment |
| Presidential authority/route | Why each option is attemptable |
| Target | Who or what receives the attempt |
| Expiration/default | What happens without action |
| Autonomous resolver | Who decides the non-presidential part |
| Downstream record | What later consequences consume |

The repair can be concise. It should correct or reclassify the incomplete trace entries rather than expand the narrative.

## R5-03 — HIGH — Campaign viability is checked before setup choices, but the contract does not keep the player inside the envelope afterward

### Finding

The addendum correctly requires a winning-feasibility envelope before candidates are presented.

A candidate is eligible if at least one plausible winning path exists without miraculous turnout, invented opponent collapse, contradictory state results, or a conveniently selected Congress.

The player then chooses:

- candidate history;
- running mate;
- commitments;
- campaign/mandate decisions;
- coalition emphasis.

Those choices can logically eliminate the candidate’s last plausible winning path.

For example, a marginally viable nominee could:

- choose commitments that repel a pivotal faction and a necessary regional coalition;
- select a running mate who creates an unreconciled party fracture;
- make several campaign choices that depress both base turnout and cross-party support;
- accumulate promises whose fiscal or ideological contradiction destroys the previously viable campaign history.

The addendum says a bad setup choice costs future governing position and “not necessarily” the existence of inauguration. It also says the exact victorious history resolves within the envelope.

It does not say what happens when the player’s sequence of choices leaves **no** plausible victory history inside that envelope.

Possible but currently unspecified answers include:

- options that eliminate the last victory path are not offered;
- the interface warns that the combination cannot produce the standard victorious start and requires revision;
- the player returns to candidate/commitment selection;
- an optional campaign-loss mode becomes available;
- the standard setup instead selects among already-complete victorious histories.

Without one explicit rule, the generator can still backsolve a miraculous victory after the player exits the original feasibility envelope.

### Why this fails the binary gate

The PASS condition requires campaign victory to remain inside a prior feasibility envelope.

The addendum establishes the envelope only at candidate admission. It does not maintain the invariant across subsequent player choices.

### Required bounded repair

Add a **dynamic feasibility invariant**:

> After every setup choice, at least one plausible victorious history must remain inside the previously defined world/candidate feasibility space.

Then select one transparent consequence when a proposed option would eliminate the last path:

1. the option is unavailable with a causal explanation;
2. the player must revise an earlier setup choice;
3. the setup converts to an explicitly optional loss-capable campaign mode;
4. the game uses complete pre-generated victory histories rather than open-ended mandate shaping.

House and Senate elections must remain separately owned under every option.

# Significant refinements

## R5-04 — MEDIUM — The prohibition on “aggregate capacity” is broader than the intended prohibition on a universal resource

### Finding

The addendum’s hard invariant says no aggregate `attention`, `capacity`, `overextension`, `agenda slot`, or `political capital` value may causally resolve whether initiatives advance or crowd out one another.

The intended target is clear: reject one hidden cross-domain Presidential Capacity score.

Taken literally, however, the wording could also prohibit legitimate owner-scoped quantities such as:

- a role-bounded staff office having two active negotiation assignments before a deadline;
- an agency review unit having finite throughput;
- a committee calendar containing a finite number of hearing days;
- a fiscal office tracking available budget authority;
- a communications team having a finite set of scheduled presidential appearances.

Those are contextual resources and may be aggregated inside their correct owner.

### Required refinement

Clarify the invariant:

> No **universal or cross-domain aggregate** attention/capacity/overextension value may resolve unrelated institutional outcomes. Owner-scoped, causally grounded quantities, queues, assignments, dates, dollars, and legally defined limits are permitted when their provenance and consequences remain visible.

This preserves the anti-Political-Capital rule without forcing every real capacity constraint to be represented only as prose.

## R5-05 — MEDIUM — Counterfactual reconnection is plausible but remains a contract test, not systemic evidence

### Finding

The Decision 6, 14, and 19 forks are useful. They show how branches could reconnect at later common institutions while carrying different laws, commitments, implementation state, and relationships.

But several branches still contain conditional narrative placeholders rather than fully traced outputs.

The addendum appropriately calls them paper counterfactuals. The repaired authority action should preserve that status.

### Required refinement

For each fork, distinguish:

- **guaranteed record difference** caused directly by the player choice;
- **eligible autonomous response** that may or may not occur;
- **later observation** required before the President knows the consequence;
- **reconnection institution/date** shared by branches.

Do not promote the paper forks into proof that the eventual simulation generates the same distribution of outcomes.
