# 2. Accepted central answer

Living Country participants share:

> **A decision-and-action handshake, not a shared mind, body, authority model, objective function, action vocabulary, or power meter.**

A retained actor, office, institution, organization, procedure, or aggregate participant must be able to establish, where causally applicable:

1. semantic identity and fact owner;
2. representation level and reason for that resolution;
3. actual decision locus;
4. role, standing, jurisdiction, constituency, mandate, membership, or relationship;
5. attemptability, claimed/applicable authority, capability, and independent compliance;
6. bounded information and support status;
7. purpose-bounded objectives, duties, priorities, risks, and time horizons;
8. owner-specific resources, queues, calendars, money, access, staff, and capabilities;
9. currently available institution-specific action routes;
10. action, delay, delegation, refusal, abstention, expiration, or no-action;
11. the canonical attempt, decision, communication, or rule-governed occurrence;
12. the target, procedure, domain, institution, or recipient that independently resolves the next consequence;
13. persistent relationships, commitments, memory, records, turnover, and succession.

These are semantic obligations. They are not required fields in one universal runtime actor object.

# 3. Accepted participant taxonomy

The following remain distinct:

- human person identity;
- individualized autonomous actor;
- office;
- office assignment;
- institution;
- organization;
- membership or affiliation;
- representation relationship;
- employment or contract relationship;
- delegation;
- coalition or negotiated commitment;
- procedure or rule-bound process;
- aggregate participant class.

An institution is not automatically one actor.

An organization is not every member.

An office is not its holder.

An office assignment is not permanent identity.

A procedure can resolve state without inventing a discretionary person at every transition.

An aggregate participant class is not one conscious collective actor.

---

# 4. One canonical entity across semantic roles

Participant-family labels describe roles, facets, or components. They do not authorize duplicate canonical entities.

A hospital system, university, media company, nonprofit, firm, union, party, or other real entity may possess organizational governance, institutional operations, offices, facilities, and procedures while retaining one reconciled entity graph.

Every load-bearing fact is owned once, including:

- identity;
- resources;
- contracts;
- memberships;
- offices;
- employees and relationships;
- facilities;
- actions;
- liabilities;
- records;
- commitments.

Separate parent, subsidiary, affiliate, local, plant, station, newsroom, or operating-unit identities are legitimate only when their distinct identity changes a supported action, resource, obligation, legal responsibility, or institutional outcome and the relationship is explicit.

---

# 5. Individualization and aggregation

Individualize a person or organization only when persistent identity, a single decision, office authority, resources, relationships, public role, or accountability can change a supported consequence.

Aggregate when individual identity cannot change a supported:

- outcome;
- observation;
- institutional response;
- relationship;
- political interpretation;
- presidential decision.

Aggregation must preserve relevant distributions, geography, resources, heterogeneity, uncertainty, and provenance.

Refinement cannot manufacture an exact prior identity that was never retained.

Historical identity and consequential records remain available after current processing is compressed, an actor leaves office, or an organization becomes inactive.

---

# 6. Individualized-human population linkage

Every individualized human actor must declare exactly one population-linkage status for every effective interval:

1. `EXACT_CANONICAL_POPULATION_IDENTITY`
2. `STATISTICALLY_REPRESENTED_WITHOUT_EXACT_IDENTITY`
3. `OUTSIDE_MODELED_ORDINARY_POPULATION_SCOPE`

The declaration must identify:

- effective interval;
- population universe or scope;
- support status;
- provenance;
- population-weight treatment;
- permitted and prohibited joins;
- residence/household implications;
- demographic and lifecycle implications;
- material-exposure implications;
- information-recipient and belief implications;
- electorate/eligibility implications;
- linkage-transition rules.

No individualized actor object adds a second person of population weight.

## 6.1 Exact linkage

An exact actor link references one already represented Population identity or exact one-person carrier.

Population-owned residence, household, demographic continuity, and lifecycle remain with Population.

Domain facts join only through valid exact relations.

Exact linkage does not grant epistemic access to hidden personal or population state.

Actor-private beliefs remain actor-owned and distinct from Population-owned public belief.

## 6.2 Statistical representation

A statistically represented named actor is declared to be already included once in a supported Population universe without retaining an exact person carrier.

The actor:

- adds no weight;
- may retain independently supported institutional or biographical facts;
- may not inherit an aggregate cohort’s residence, household, demographics, material exposure, recipient history, belief, eligibility, or other personal state as exact truth;
- receives modeled, bounded, unsupported, or unknown answers where exact linkage is absent;
- must reconcile any later load-bearing lifecycle transition through exact resolution or a valid occurrence-linked one-person adjustment without fabricating prior exact history.

## 6.3 Outside scope

An outside-scope actor is not included in the modeled ordinary-population universe for that interval.

Domestic Population joins remain unavailable unless an explicit population-entry and linkage transition occurs.

## 6.4 Linkage changes

Linkage changes are canonical relationship transitions with effective time, provenance, reconciliation, and no duplicate weight.

Later exact resolution identifies or partitions an already represented person. It does not add a new person because the actor became important.

Prior unsupported relationships do not become retroactively exact.

# 7. Bounded actor knowledge and support-level honesty

No actor reads canonical world truth directly.

Actor decisions may consume only information legitimately available through:

- role-accessible records;
- received artifacts and communications;
- direct personal or operational experience;
- institutional records;
- valid measurements;
- organization knowledge;
- staff, constituency, member, client, source, or counterparty reports;
- forecasts or estimates;
- retained memory.

The decision process must preserve the support level:

- exact authoritative record;
- direct experience;
- measured estimate;
- modeled estimate;
- bounded range;
- uncertain assessment;
- unsupported;
- unknown.

A lawmaker, governor, organization, firm, outlet, or administration office may not treat a modeled or unsupported population/geographic cross-tab as exact merely because its decision process requires a value.

Semantic linkage does not imply information access.

---

# 8. Authority, attemptability, capability, compliance, and result

The accepted chain remains:

```text
structurally possible input
→ role can meaningfully attempt it
→ claimed or applicable authority
→ operational capability
→ target/procedure response
→ institutional, legal, informational, or material result
→ later observation and political interpretation
```

These are not interchangeable.

A disputed or unlawful but meaningful attempt may enter history.

A lawful act may fail because capacity, procedure, target compliance, implementation, or material response differs.

A player-selected action has no privileged causal semantics over the same world attempt selected autonomously.

---

# 9. Objectives and objective change

Different participant families may use different criteria, including legal duty, constituency interest, policy preference, member interest, institutional mission, professional norms, profit/cost/liquidity, electoral ambition, evidence, procedure, relationships, commitments, reputation, and survival.

No universal utility function is accepted.

Material or social conditions may alter participant belief, objective, priority, risk posture, or choice only through a legitimate route available to that participant or institution, such as:

- direct experience;
- direct operational state;
- valid measurement or record;
- received information;
- a constituent/member/employee/client/source report;
- an observable legal duty;
- an admitted institutional input;
- a forecast or estimate with uncertainty.

Hidden material truth cannot directly rewrite actor state.

---

# 10. Owner-specific resources and contention

Participant action is constrained by real owner-specific resources and opportunities rather than one universal action currency.

Examples include:

- committee and floor time;
- legislative or organizational staff;
- agency queues;
- appropriated dollars;
- cash and credit;
- facilities and contracts;
- strike funds;
- investigative teams;
- source access;
- court dockets;
- platform distribution capability;
- legal deadlines;
- governor or presidential calendar time.

Every load-bearing resource declares owner, unit, current availability, commitments, competing uses, scope, time, and release/replenishment/expiration semantics.

Influence, credibility, reputation, legitimacy, and leverage may be derived context-specific summaries. No global scalar causes unrelated actors to comply.

# 11. Institution-specific action derivation

Available actions arise from current:

- role;
- authority or authority claim;
- procedure;
- target;
- records and information;
- relationships and commitments;
- resources;
- material or operational state;
- deadlines and opportunities.

No balanced-menu generator is accepted.

No universal action enum is required.

Senators, agencies, firms, unions, governors, journalists, courts, parties, organizations, and administration offices retain materially different action spaces and internal decision processes.

# 12. Action, delay, delegation, refusal, and no-action

A supported decision identifies the causal meaning of:

- action;
- delay;
- refusal;
- abstention;
- delegation;
- expiration;
- no-action.

No-action never freezes the world.

A delegation identifies principal, delegate, authority basis, scope, objective, resources, constraints, reserved decisions, reporting thresholds, deadlines, revocation, expiration, succession, and transferred records.

Delegation does not make the delegate a puppet or guarantee success.

---

# 13. Autonomous initiative and independent resolution

A load-bearing autonomous initiative follows:

```text
participant observes or receives bounded state
→ interprets through role, beliefs, objectives, duties,
  constituency, relationships, commitments, resources,
  risk, deadlines, and opportunity
→ derives currently available institution-specific attempts
→ selects action, delay, delegation, refusal, or no-action
→ records a canonical attempt or decision occurrence
→ target, procedure, domain, institution, or recipient resolves independently
→ consequences become owner-held current state, history,
  records, artifacts, or later opportunities
```

Actors may reasonably remain quiet.

No hidden dramatic director may cause opposition, investigation, endorsement, litigation, defection, strike, closure, leak, or escalation merely because a presidency needs pressure.

Autonomous action may materially or politically matter without reaching the President.

---

# 14. Organizations, members, and aggregate collective acts

Organizations own their identity, governance, resources, adopted positions, strategies, records, and actions.

They do not own every member’s beliefs, intentions, or later conduct.

A collective organizational action requires a declared decision locus such as leader authority, board vote, membership vote, convention, delegated staff, standing policy, or another real procedure.

An aggregate participant process ordinarily yields a distribution, count, flow, rate, price, or set of unit-level actions.

It may create one collective position, commitment, communication, or action only when a real coordination, organization, market-clearing, bargaining, legal, or procedural mechanism produces that result.

An aggregate statistical class cannot issue a press release or possess one unified intention.

---

# 15. Relationships, commitments, and records

Office assignment, membership, employment, representation, delegation, contract, coalition, and negotiated commitment remain distinct relationship families with one declared owner each.

Participants may hold private beliefs, evaluations, trust, memories, or willingness concerning those relationships. They may not maintain competing authoritative copies.

Commitments may be honored, renegotiated, breached, withdrawn, or expire when discretion remains.

The relationship influences later decisions but does not force them unless the applicable law or procedure removes discretion.

Historical occurrence, current state, actor memory, institutional record, and public information remain separate.

---

# 16. Media and journalist participation within Step 4 scope

Media organizations and journalists participate as autonomous actors under the common grammar.

They may:

- receive tips, filings, records, public statements, direct observations, and source communications;
- assess evidence, relevance, audience, legal risk, and scarce investigative resources;
- investigate, corroborate, delay, publish, correct, qualify, or abandon;
- create actions and information artifacts through the proper owners.

They may not:

- create underlying misconduct or material failure because a story is needed;
- directly own audience notice, belief, attribution, salience, or election outcomes;
- read hidden canonical truth;
- act as a global media-attention director.

Detailed media markets, outlet inventories, source algorithms, distribution, exposure, and public-belief dynamics remain unresolved for later Living Country work.

---

# 17. Time, concurrency, determinism, and succession

The actor grammar preserves:

- exact dated world time;
- participant-specific evaluation cadence;
- deadlines and opportunity windows;
- canonical ordering for same-time events;
- equivalent outcomes under equivalent dated advancement;
- save/load continuation;
- no global random cursor whose consumption depends on iteration order;
- persistent identities, commitments, institutional records, and history through turnover;
- bounded private and institutional knowledge transfer.

Leadership change does not erase institutions, reset queues, cancel obligations, or transfer every private belief.

An actor’s death, departure, appointment, election, or removal changes the appropriate assignments and processes without rewriting prior actions.

---

# 18. Presidential-attention boundary

Actor conduct does not directly create a presidential interruption merely because it is consequential.

The accepted Step 5 attention chain remains controlling:

```text
canonical occurrence
→ observable artifact or communication
→ administration receipt
→ bounded interpretation and escalation
→ presidential decision surface or review
```

The country may change while the administration remains unaware.

# 20. Accepted anti-cheat tests

Future actor and institution designs must reject:

- an individualized human with no declared Population relationship;
- an actor object adding population weight;
- a statistical actor inheriting aggregate personal facts as exact;
- exact Population linkage granting hidden knowledge;
- later individualization creating a new person instead of resolving an already represented person;
- one entity duplicated as institution and organization;
- hidden material truth directly altering actor objectives;
- aggregate classes issuing fictional collective actions;
- one generic utility or influence system resolving all participants;
- organization position automatically forcing member conduct;
- actor action directly writing another owner’s state;
- player-selected action receiving privileged success;
- autonomous conduct directly producing a presidential popup;
- no-action freezing time;
- nondeterministic results caused solely by iteration order, save/load, or time chunking.
