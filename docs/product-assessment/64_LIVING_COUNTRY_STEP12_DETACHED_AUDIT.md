# 2. R12-01 — BLOCKING: the synthetic 2024 election and January transition package is outside the generator proof

## 2.1 The gap

The candidate correctly inherits Step 11's:

```text
frozen 2024 election/backstory package
→ January 3 Congress
→ January 6 electoral count
→ January 3–20 transition history
→ January 20 live handoff
```

But most Step 12 generation, horizon-neutrality, adaptive-resolution, multi-seed, rejection, and falsification requirements begin only after January 20.

The candidate calls the Early-2025 package accepted input rather than explicitly requiring the **package generator** itself to produce multiple coherent candidates through reusable rules.

## 2.2 Exploit

A future project could:

1. manually choose one compelling incoming President and coalition;
2. manually choose House/Senate control;
3. manually write a plausible campaign and mandate;
4. manually create a few January promises, relationships, and disclosures;
5. freeze the result as the accepted transition package;
6. then run a rigorous lower-resolution generator from January 20 forward.

All 2025-forward invariants could pass.

The opening political foundation would still be authored content disguised as generation.

The same exploit could create several nominal seeds that all share one handcrafted transition package.

## 2.3 Why it is blocking

The 2024 election and transition determine load-bearing 2025 facts:

- President and Vice President;
- campaign commitments and mandate evidence;
- House and Senate composition;
- office assignments and leadership;
- January 6 count;
- transition-team relationships;
- incoming/outgoing information and access;
- vacancies, acting arrangements, and personnel choices;
- early commitments or disputes;
- public and organization expectations.

Those facts shape every later administration, congressional, fiscal, legal, media, and political process.

If they are curated, the generator's most important initial political state remains authored.

That fails the gate's no-screenplay, multi-seed, and generated-campaign requirements.

## 2.4 Required repair

The repaired contract must establish a **two-stage prehistory proof boundary**:

### Stage A — pre-seam political/transition generation

From the historically closed inherited America and frozen generated-backstory package, reusable rules generate candidate:

- 2024 presidential campaign/election;
- 2024 House and Senate elections;
- candidate eligibility and campaign commitments;
- electoral results/certification;
- January 3 congressional assignments/organization;
- January 6 electoral count;
- consequential January 3–20 transition activity;
- January 20 incoming administration and access state.

### Stage B — live forward prehistory

The accepted January 20 candidate handoff then continues through 2029 or 2033 under the Step 12 rules.

The repair must require:

- multiple transition-package seeds/control families;
- full rejection and conditioning disclosure;
- no manually authored decisive election or transition facts hidden behind generated prose;
- package-level prefix, resolution, retention, and reconciliation rules;
- quiet/routine transitions as valid;
- no required contested electoral count or dramatic January incident;
- selected package identity included in all later world provenance;
- changing the package creates a new world lineage;
- the 2025-forward proof cannot validate the transition generator by itself.

A fixed scenario-family control is permitted when disclosed before generation.

It may not be misrepresented as independently generated.

# 3. C12-02 — product-readiness exclusion could become a hidden interestingness filter

Section 16.4 permits excluding a world that lacks:

- sufficient observable state;
- at least one credible governing route or workstream opportunity;
- enough history to explain current actors and institutions.

Most of that is structurally legitimate.

But `at least one credible governing route or workstream opportunity` could be interpreted as:

```text
valid quiet world has no exciting opening dilemma
→ classify it product-unready
→ reroll until something dramatic appears
```

Required clarification:

- semantic validity, supported-scope validity, player-start operability, and scenario-family selection remain distinct;
- operability means the constitutional office, administration, information access, time advancement, and ordinary governing routes function;
- the first day need not contain a decision, crisis, workstream, or conflict;
- a quiet world may begin with briefing and automatic advancement until a valid situation emerges;
- lack of drama is never a rejection reason;
- any scenario-family conditioning that requires a particular political configuration is declared before generation;
- full valid-world distributions remain visible in proof.

This is bounded because Sections 10 and 16 already defend quiet worlds.

# 4. C12-03 — horizon and campaign-branch comparison needs a closed fork protocol

The candidate correctly notes that a 2029 player-victory branch and a 2033 autonomous continuation cannot share identical history after the 2028 campaign checkpoint.

It leaves the exact comparison protocol unresolved.

That ambiguity could permit an invalid claim such as:

```text
2029 player branch differs from 2033 autonomous branch
→ extra four years caused the difference
```

Required clarification:

- the automated spine is generated independently of a player-start target through each checkpoint;
- a 2029 product candidate branches from the 2028 campaign-entry checkpoint;
- a 2033 automated comparison continues the autonomous 2028 election branch to 2033 and branches at the 2032 campaign-entry checkpoint;
- 2033 marginal value is measured against its own January 2029 autonomous checkpoint, not against the horizon-specific 2029 player-victory branch;
- prefix invariance applies until the first differing declared intervention/control;
- campaign branches receive distinct lineage and cannot feed back into the autonomous spine.

# 5. C12-04 — ensemble denominators must prevent survivor bias across the 2033 extension

Section 19 requires at least 300 four-year identities and says the 2033 proof should continue at least the same 300 automated spines `where supported`.

`Where supported` could permit extending only the four-year worlds that looked stable, coherent, or promising.

Required clarification:

- every four-year automated attempt receives an extension disposition;
- valid four-year spines are extended unless a predeclared horizon-ineligibility rule applies;
- invalid and unsupported four-year attempts remain in the original denominator;
- failures during 2029–2033 remain in the extension denominator;
- worlds cannot be dropped because they are quiet, politically awkward, computationally expensive, or likely to fail the 2033 test;
- replacement seeds are additional attempts, not substitutes;
- reports show attempted, eligible, extended, completed, invalid, unsupported, and selected counts.

The 300-run floor remains a screening minimum rather than statistical validation.

# 6. C12-05 — endpoint obligations must not become future-aware deepening

The candidate strongly prohibits hindsight fabrication, but one phrase allows resolution to deepen when `the opening briefing or player decision needs a causal explanation`.

Read loosely, this could mean:

```text
reach 2033
→ inspect which opening facts seem important
→ go back and create finer 2027 history
```

Required clarification:

- the supported consumer closure and retention policy are frozen before each run/version;
- current-state events can trigger prospective deepening at the time they become consequential;
- known player-start fact families can reserve support without knowing their future values;
- final packaging may select among already retained facts and bounded reconstructions;
- endpoint assembly cannot create newly exact past occurrences;
- a later unmet explanation need remains bounded, unsupported, or requires a new generation package and rerun.

This is already implied by Step 10 and candidate Section 7 but should be made explicit in the final authority.
