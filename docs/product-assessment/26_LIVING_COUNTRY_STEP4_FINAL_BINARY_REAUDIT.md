# 2. Blocking finding R4-01 is closed

## 2.1 Closed status set

Every individualized human actor must now declare exactly one linkage status for every effective interval:

1. `EXACT_CANONICAL_POPULATION_IDENTITY`
2. `STATISTICALLY_REPRESENTED_WITHOUT_EXACT_IDENTITY`
3. `OUTSIDE_MODELED_ORDINARY_POPULATION_SCOPE`

An actor may no longer exist in the ambiguous state:

```text
named person
+ no population weight
+ no Population relationship
+ domestic biography and office
```

without explaining whether the person is exactly represented, statistically included, or outside the modeled population.

## 2.2 Weight reconciliation is explicit

Under every status, the actor object contributes no second person weight.

- Exact linkage references one already represented Population identity.
- Statistical linkage declares that the person is already included in a supported Population universe or scope and carries a count-neutral inclusion/reconciliation obligation.
- Outside-scope linkage declares that no ordinary-population weight is present.

Later exact resolution must identify or partition an already represented person. It cannot create a new person because an actor became politically important.

## 2.3 Support status is preserved

The linkage declaration must state support, time, provenance, permitted joins, prohibited joins, and lifecycle behavior.

A statistically represented actor cannot silently inherit exact personal facts from:

- a statewide cohort;
- a district estimate;
- a demographic cell;
- a household class;
- a media audience;
- a material-exposure allocation;
- an electorate projection.

Exact personal facts may exist only when independently supported by an actor, office, public-record, generated-history, Population, or domain source with proper provenance. They do not become an exact Population join merely because they describe the same person.

## 2.4 Actor decisions cannot launder precision

The repair binds the Step 3 support taxonomy into actor choice.

An actor may consume only information legitimately available at its actual support level:

- exact authoritative;
- directly experienced;
- measured;
- modeled;
- bounded;
- assessed with uncertainty;
- unsupported;
- unknown.

The actor model cannot convert a modeled district cross-tab into exact constituency knowledge merely because its decision policy expects a number.

This closes the future trap identified during Step 3 review.

## 2.5 Lifecycle behavior is reconciled

A statistically represented actor’s death, migration, household transition, or other population-affecting event cannot remain actor-only history while Population totals ignore it.

The repair requires:

- exact Population resolution from the relevant time;
- or an occurrence-linked one-person affected scope with reconciliation;
- or a modeled/bounded treatment only when exact population accounting is not required.

Prior uncertainty remains prior uncertainty. The transition cannot retroactively manufacture an exact household, residence, exposure, or information history.

## 2.6 Population linkage does not grant knowledge

An exact semantic link does not allow the actor, player, administration, or public to read:

- private household income;
- hidden health state;
- every artifact delivered to a population class;
- exact Population-owned beliefs;
- unsupported domain incidence.

Access remains governed by role, receipt, observation, confidentiality, and purpose-bounded knowledge.

The repaired actor therefore sits honestly inside, statistically within, or outside the ordinary population without becoming a duplicated person or an omniscient personal join.

# 3. Clarification C4-02 is closed: one entity across labels

Participant-family labels are now explicitly roles, facets, or components rather than permission to create duplicate entities.

A hospital system, university, media company, nonprofit, firm, or other entity can have:

- organization governance;
- institutional operating units;
- offices;
- facilities;
- procedures;

while retaining one canonical entity graph and one owner for every resource, contract, action, membership, office, liability, and record.

Separate identities are allowed only when parent, subsidiary, affiliate, local, plant, station, newsroom, or service-unit distinctions are genuinely load-bearing and explicitly related.

The following exploit is rejected:

```text
HospitalOrganization owns $500m and 20,000 employees
HospitalInstitution also owns $500m and 20,000 employees
```

The taxonomy no longer permits identity duplication merely because an entity fits more than one conceptual family.

---

# 4. Clarification C4-03 is closed: material truth cannot rewrite objectives

The candidate’s phrase `material condition` is now controlled by an information/experience provenance rule.

Material and social conditions may influence beliefs, objectives, priorities, risk posture, or choices only through a legitimate route such as:

- direct personal experience;
- direct operational state;
- an authoritative record;
- measurement;
- communication;
- constituent/member/client/source report;
- a legal duty triggered by an observable fact;
- an institutional process admitting the material input;
- a forecast or estimate with uncertainty.

Hidden unemployment, hidden pollution, hidden service failure, or any other unobserved canonical state cannot directly rewrite actor state.

Direct experience also remains bounded. A firm knowing its own shutdown does not thereby know national production, and a household knowing its rent increase does not thereby know the national Housing trend.

This clarification preserves the candidate’s broader bounded-information invariant rather than creating a new actor subsystem.

---

# 5. Clarification C4-04 is closed: aggregate classes cannot speak as hive minds

An aggregate participant process ordinarily produces:

- a distribution;
- count;
- flow;
- rate;
- price;
- or set of unit-level actions.

A single collective position, statement, commitment, refusal, or action requires an actual mechanism such as:

- organization governance;
- vote;
- bargaining process;
- legal rule;
- market-clearing process;
- joint signatories;
- delegated spokesperson;
- another declared coordination procedure.

Therefore:

```text
SmallFirms oppose the President
```

is not a valid collective act merely because an aggregate class exists.

The model may instead record a distribution of firm responses, or a trade association may adopt and communicate a position through its own governance.

The same rule prevents aggregate workers, hospitals, local outlets, or advocacy chapters from becoming fictional unitary minds.

# 6. Gate-by-gate disposition

## G4-01 — semantic identity and fact ownership

**PASS.**

Person, actor, office, assignment, institution, organization, relationship, procedure, and aggregate class remain distinct. The repair also prevents one real entity from being duplicated through multiple family labels.

## G4-02 — representation level and individualization

**PASS.**

Individualization requires a supported causal consumer. Aggregation retains distributions and heterogeneity. Human individualization now also requires a closed population-linkage declaration.

## G4-03 — decision locus

**PASS.**

Every consequential institutional or organizational act must identify whether it came from an officeholder, vote, board, delegated unit, procedure, market process, or aggregate response. The word `institution` is not itself a decision maker.

## G4-04 — role, standing, jurisdiction, and scope

**PASS.**

Attempts require role, relationship, office, jurisdiction, mandate, membership, contract, delegation, or another valid basis, with time and target scope.

## G4-05 — attemptability, authority, capability, compliance, and result

**PASS.**

These remain separate. A meaningful disputed action can enter history without being lawful or successful. A lawful act does not guarantee capacity or compliance.

## G4-06 — bounded information

**PASS.**

Actors consume received records, direct experience, communications, measurements, forecasts, and purpose-bounded beliefs. The repair prevents hidden material truth and unsupported population intersections from entering decisions as exact facts.

## G4-07 — objectives without universal utility

**PASS.**

Different participant families retain distinct criteria and internal processes. Objective change now requires a legitimate experience, observation, information, institutional, or historical route.

## G4-08 — owner-specific resources and contention

**PASS.**

Calendars, money, queues, staff, access, facilities, contracts, legal windows, and other resources retain their actual owners and semantics. No universal influence, power, or action currency is required.

## G4-09 — action-space derivation

**PASS.**

Actions arise from role, law, procedure, records, relationships, material or operational state, information, resources, and timing. The design does not require a balanced-menu generator or one universal action enum.

## G4-10 — action, delay, delegation, refusal, and no-action

**PASS.**

No-action has causal meaning and never freezes the world. Delegation states principal, delegate, scope, authority, resources, reserved decisions, reporting, and succession.

## G4-11 — autonomous initiative

**PASS.**

Initiative derives from bounded observation and participant-specific state. Quiet actors remain valid. No hidden narrative director is needed to create opposition, endorsement, investigation, litigation, closure, strike, or escalation.

## G4-12 — independent receiver resolution

**PASS.**

An actor’s attempt becomes a canonical action or communication. Targets, procedures, material domains, information recipients, courts, organizations, and other owners resolve their portions independently.

## G4-13 — organizations and membership

**PASS.**

Organizations own governance, resources, positions, and actions, not every member’s beliefs or choices. Collective results require an actual decision locus. Aggregate classes cannot substitute for organizations.

## G4-14 — firms, unions, advocacy, media, and journalists

**PASS.**

These families participate through the shared handshake while retaining different action vocabularies and constraints. Firms do not own aggregate material outcomes. Journalists may discover evidence but not manufacture underlying misconduct. Publication, delivery, notice, belief, and response remain separate.

## G4-15 — population and geography reconciliation

**PASS.**

Individualized people declare exact, statistical, or outside-scope population linkage. Actors cannot add person weight, inherit unsupported exact cross-tabs, or ignore load-bearing lifecycle accounting. Geographic and population support remains explicit.

## G4-16 — history, memory, turnover, and succession

**PASS.**

Occurrences, current state, actor memory, institutional records, private knowledge, office assignment, and population linkage intervals remain distinct. Leadership turnover does not erase institutions or transfer all private knowledge.

## G4-17 — deterministic continuation and concurrency

**PASS.**

The candidate requires local deterministic decision identities or equivalent causal stability, canonical same-time ordering, save/load continuity, and no global random cursor whose consumption depends on iteration order. The repair introduces no contradiction.

## G4-18 — presidential attention boundary

**PASS.**

Consequential autonomous conduct may remain unknown to the President. It reaches presidential attention only through a valid artifact, communication, administration receipt, and bounded escalation.
