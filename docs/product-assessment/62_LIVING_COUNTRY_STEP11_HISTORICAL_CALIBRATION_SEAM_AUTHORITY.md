# 2. Accepted central decision

The leading historical calibration seam for the first modern United States scenario is accepted as:

> **A historically closed Early-2025 Transition Package with its primary live alternate-history handoff at noon Eastern Time on January 20, 2025. The 119th Congress beginning January 3, the January 6 electoral count, and all load-bearing January 3–20 transition activity remain exact or bounded generated pre-seam history. The synthetic 2024 presidential and congressional election/backstory package is frozen before forward-generated prehistory begins.**

The controlling sequence is:

```text
historically closed inherited modern America
+ versioned source and transformation package
+ generated political actors and admissible backstory
+ generated 2024 presidential and congressional elections
+ January 3 congressional seating and organization
+ January 6 electoral-count procedure
+ load-bearing January 3–20 transition activity
→ January 20, 2025, noon ET reconciled current world
→ primary live forward-generated prehistory begins
→ later candidate player start
```

This selects a world-history handoff.

It does not select the eventual player inauguration.

# 3. Why early 2025 is accepted

Early 2025 provides the strongest currently defensible balance of:

- modern and recognizable constitutional, political, legal, fiscal, economic, social, technological, and media conditions;
- mature historical source availability for the relevant pre-seam periods;
- a new Congress already elected and seated before the presidential transfer;
- a clear generated 2024 election and transition history;
- enough subsequent time to produce meaningful laws, appointments, implementation, relationships, party/faction development, issue history, and political memory;
- isolation from all later real-world events once the scenario package is frozen;
- support for both a lower-burden January 2029 player-start candidate and a richer but harder January 2033 candidate.

The region is not accepted because every United States system resets in January.

No universal clean instant exists.

At the live handoff:

- fiscal year 2025 is already underway;
- courts and the Supreme Court term are underway;
- agencies, rules, contracts, grants, cases, investigations, programs, measurements, and projects may be midstream;
- Congress began before the presidential term;
- public and institutional memory already exists.

The package is accepted because those straddling states can be retained and reconciled—not because they disappear.

# 4. Why January 20 is the primary live handoff

The first Step 11 candidate selected January 3.

The detached audit correctly found that the candidate had established early 2025 as the best region but had not proved that the earliest constitutional boundary was the cleanest live handoff.

The controlling repair compared:

## January 3 live variant

```text
new Congress begins live
→ outgoing administration remains in office
→ January 6 count resolves live
→ 17-day transition segment
→ January 20 transfer
→ first complete generated presidential term
```

## January 20 live variant

```text
2024 election/backstory frozen
→ January 3 Congress occurrence history retained
→ January 6 count occurrence history retained
→ January 3–20 consequential transition history retained
→ January 20 live handoff
→ first complete generated presidential term begins immediately
```

January 20 is accepted because it:

- begins forward history with the complete first generated presidential administration;
- produces one coherent initial White House, Cabinet/acting structure, mandate, access environment, and administration control surface;
- avoids a short live outgoing-administration segment whose principal function is immediate succession;
- makes a January 2029 candidate exactly one complete generated presidential term later;
- makes a January 2033 candidate exactly two complete generated presidential terms later;
- retains every required January 3 and January 6 institutional fact rather than pretending they occur at inauguration;
- does not lose any currently identified downstream consumer by compressing routine January 3–20 detail.

January 20 is not accepted as a universal reset.

It is the cleanest primary live boundary for this presidency-centered product.

# 5. January 3 and January 6 remain canonical history

The accepted package preserves separate owner-resolved boundaries.

## 5.1 January 3

Generated pre-seam history establishes, where supported:

- House and Senate election entitlement;
- new congressional office assignments;
- chamber organization;
- leadership;
- committee or institutional organization consumed later;
- any load-bearing proceedings, contacts, commitments, or deadlines before inauguration.

## 5.2 January 6

The seated Congress owns the applicable electoral-count procedure.

The count and result are retained as generated occurrences rather than reduced to an incoming-President trait.

An ordinary generated election may produce a routine count.

A lawful dispute may exist only when generated state and procedure support it.

The design does not manufacture a contested count for drama.

## 5.3 January 3–20

Any consequential transition-period act must retain its proper:

- actor or institution;
- authority;
- target;
- procedure;
- time;
- receipt/access;
- consequence;
- historical identity;
- surviving current-state effect.

Routine detail with no supported later consumer may be compressed.

A specific bargain, promise, disclosure, legal act, fiscal act, appointment-related act, congressional action, media event, or relationship change cannot be invented later as a trait.

# 7. Political calibration controls and generated outcomes

The accepted contract distinguishes several later options.

## 7.1 Fully generated

The election and campaign resolve within declared modern political and institutional constraints without fixing the final aggregate result.

## 7.2 Conditioned generation

Selected outcomes or ranges are declared before generation, such as:

- incoming presidential party;
- broad margin range;
- divided/unified government family;
- chamber-control range;
- state-result constraints.

The generator produces compatible histories.

## 7.3 Aggregate-calibrated synthetic history

Selected actual or configured aggregate outcomes are fixed while individual political actors and their histories are synthetic.

The package is calibrated, not independently predicted.

## 7.4 Scenario families

Several separately declared election/government configurations may each generate their own valid history.

## 7.5 Required disclosure

Every fixed control identifies:

- source or design authority;
- meaning;
- time and geography;
- support/status;
- whether real aggregate, modeled range, or authored scenario constraint;
- what remains autonomous;
- what validation claims are prohibited.

A calibrated result cannot later be cited as independent evidence that the election model generated that same result without conditioning.

Aggregate controls cannot license impossible state, district, Senate, elector, candidate, or office histories.

Step 11 does not select among these approaches.

# 8. The four historical clocks remain separate

The accepted package distinguishes:

1. **historical source/reference time**;
2. **source-compilation and package-freeze time**;
3. **in-world release/receipt and actor-knowledge time**;
4. **primary live world-state handoff and later player-start time**.

The simplified rule is:

```text
source exists to developers
≠ source describes the seam exactly
≠ actors possessed it at the seam
≠ player control begins at the seam
```

## 8.1 Later-published pre-seam sources

A later-published source may improve hidden opening-state estimation when:

- its applicable reference period is pre-seam;
- reference and publication times remain distinct;
- the transformation to seam-time state is declared;
- actor knowledge remains tied to release and receipt;
- no post-seam completed outcome is imported.

## 8.2 Straddling sources

A source whose reference interval crosses January 20 cannot be imported wholesale as an opening fact.

It must be:

- decomposed;
- modeled;
- bounded;
- used only for later validation;
- or rejected.

## 8.3 Post-seam sources

A source measuring post-seam outcomes belongs to generated history or later evidence.

It cannot initialize the opening root merely because it has a convenient annual label.

## 8.4 Revisions

A later revision may improve current historical understanding.

It does not become what January actors knew and does not rewrite their earlier decisions.

# 9. Historically closed means closed

Once the scenario package is frozen and one world is generated:

- later real officeholders do not enter it;
- later real elections do not overwrite it;
- later real laws, regulations, court decisions, and appointments do not update it;
- later real statistics do not become live data feeds;
- later real maps do not replace generated geography;
- later real crises do not become the run's history;
- source corrections adopted by the project create a new package version rather than mutating existing runs.

The first modern scenario remains a stable alternate-history product rather than a perpetual current-events service.

# 13. Player-start candidates remain separate

## 13.1 January 2029

Using the January 20, 2025 seam, a January 2029 player start would require approximately:

- one generated presidential term;
- one midterm;
- a generated 2028 presidential and congressional election;
- enough law, implementation, condition, relationship, organization, court, media, and political history to create a meaningful inherited country.

It avoids completing the 2030 census/redistricting cycle before active play.

It remains the lower-burden fallback.

## 13.2 January 2033

A January 2033 player start would require approximately:

- two complete generated presidential terms;
- two midterms;
- the 2028 and 2032 presidential/congressional elections;
- the 2030 census/apportionment/redistricting chain;
- richer careers, laws, appointments, implementation, court state, political memory, and policy feedback.

It remains the more ambitious leading hypothesis only if later proof succeeds.

## 13.3 No automatic preference

Step 11 does not declare eight years better merely because it creates more history.

The later generated-prehistory proof must determine whether the richer history is coherent, varied, maintainable, and worth its burden.

# 16. Accepted anti-cheat requirements

Step 11 rejects:

- one universal clean reset date;
- treating January 3, January 6, and January 20 as one event;
- treating January 20 as the beginning of Congress;
- treating inauguration as a fiscal, legal, judicial, administrative, material, or information reset;
- omitting the January 3–20 interval;
- importing real post-seam January events after the package is frozen;
- reducing the electoral count or congressional transition to actor traits;
- assigning real persons' acts to generated politicians;
- using later-published sources as earlier actor knowledge;
- importing straddling/post-seam realized outcomes as opening facts;
- treating a multi-year estimate as an exact seam-time measurement;
- presenting calibrated outcomes as independent generator validation;
- backsolving impossible individual elections from aggregate targets;
- making February 2027 a design authority deadline;
- selecting August 2026 because it is already implemented;
- labeling January 2027 or 2029 a seam while hiding earlier generated history;
- rolling real-world updates into existing runs;
- treating 119th-district geography as current through 2032 without explanation;
- retaining individual Representatives without district/electorate provenance;
- accepting January 2033 because the calibration seam passes;
- selecting a generated world through hidden dramatic value;
- hiding contradictions through opening prose;
- converting source availability into player omniscience.
