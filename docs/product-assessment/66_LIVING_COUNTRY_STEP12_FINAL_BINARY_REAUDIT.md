# 3. Clarification C12-02 is closed: quiet worlds cannot be filtered as invalid

The repair separates:

1. semantic validity;
2. supported-scope validity;
3. player-start operability;
4. scenario-family eligibility;
5. subjective product interest.

A valid quiet world can begin without:

- an immediate decision;
- a crisis;
- a scandal;
- an active major bill;
- an inherited White House workstream;
- a high-pressure political issue.

Operability requires a lawful President, functioning administration and access, deterministic time, ordinary institutions and country processes, and the ability for later situations to emerge.

The first briefing may be informational and time may advance.

Human reviewers may find the output boring or commercially weak, but that result remains in the generator's validity and product-quality evidence.

A later product may decline to ship a generator mode whose valid outputs are often poor. It may not claim the generator naturally produces only strong histories by silently rejecting the weak ones.

The contract does not prove that adaptive resolution, retained history, and multi-seed generation are computationally affordable.
