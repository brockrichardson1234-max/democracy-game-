# 1. Repair disposition

The central Step 10 thesis is retained:

> **Causal completeness rather than chronological completeness: every load-bearing fact must terminate at an honest root or trace through retained owner-resolved history, while missing detail remains explicitly modeled, bounded, disputed, unsupported, or unknown.**

The repair adds five controlling contracts:

1. generated-backstory roots receive a closed admissibility, temporal-scope, and generation-order rule;
2. the world seam, source-compilation cutoff, and actor-knowledge chronology remain separate;
3. composite current facts require complete contribution/root coverage at the accepted resolution;

# 2. Closed generated-backstory contract

## 2.1 Core invariant

**[HARD INVARIANT LC-HISTR01] `Generated backstory` is a temporally bounded initialization root class, not a general-purpose repair layer. It may not absorb a fact created or materially changed during forward-generated prehistory or player-era history, and it may not be added, redated, or reclassified after downstream outcomes are known in order to make the selected opening world coherent or dramatic.**

## 2.2 Generation order

A generated-history package must preserve the conceptual order:

```text
historical source/configuration package fixed
→ baseline-inherited state constructed
→ generated actors/backstory constructed and reconciled
→ generated-backstory package versioned and frozen
→ forward-generated prehistory begins
→ one valid opening history selected
→ player-era play begins
```

The implementation may combine phases operationally while preserving the same causal information barrier.

The generator may not:

```text
run forward history
→ inspect 2033 coalition
→ add a 2023 betrayal to explain it
```

unless it discards that candidate world and regenerates a new candidate from a newly versioned pre-forward package.

The abandoned candidate remains noncanonical developer evidence.

## 2.3 Temporal scope

A generated-backstory root may cover only state whose declared effective origin is at or before the configured boundary assigned to generated backstory.

Any fact created, materially changed, adopted, received, or resolved after that boundary belongs to:

- forward-generated prehistory;
- player-era history;
- or an explicit later migration/correction outside ordinary generation.

A fact's timestamp cannot be backdated merely to qualify it as backstory.

## 2.4 Admissible root categories

A generated-backstory root may terminate causal recursion for a synthetic background fact when all of the following hold:

1. the fact's synthetic status is explicit;
2. its effective origin lies within the permitted backstory interval;
3. no supported consumer requires a specific causal occurrence history;
4. it does not create a current legal, fiscal, procedural, material, evidentiary, or institutional consequence that itself requires an owner-resolved route;
5. it does not claim that a real historical person performed the act;
6. its current owner, support, player-knowledge status, and permissible explanations are declared;
7. later deepening cannot fabricate an exact prior occurrence that was not retained.

Potentially admissible examples include, at deliberately bounded resolution:

- synthetic personal identity and background;
- age, birthplace, education, profession, or career family sufficient for eligibility/context;
- general ideological disposition or governing temperament;
- longstanding non-event-specific familiarity, affinity, distrust, kinship, or professional connection;
- broad synthetic reputation state at the seam when no precise historical cause is asserted;
- a generated organization's background mission, constituency, and internal culture;
- a declared preexisting actor interest whose origin is outside supported detail.

Admissibility does not imply player access or exactness.

## 2.5 Occurrence-required categories

**[HARD INVARIANT LC-HISTR02] A load-bearing fact may not terminate as a bare generated-backstory root when its semantics assert or depend on an identifiable act, transaction, decision, communication, receipt, institutional procedure, or material consequence.**

At minimum, a compressed or full generated occurrence/activity chain is required for a load-bearing:

- election result, certification, office entitlement, appointment, confirmation, vacancy, succession, or office transfer;
- vote, sponsorship, legislative bargain, enactment, veto, ruling, order, filing, regulation, enforcement action, or administrative determination;
- appropriation, obligation, payment, tax change, debt change, contract, grant, or other fiscal/legal commitment;
- promise, commitment, offer, threat, endorsement, withdrawal, betrayal, favor, quid-like expectation, or negotiated concession;
- relationship change explicitly caused by historical conduct;
- actor memory of a specific event;
- public reputation, party association, blame, credit, or competence judgment explicitly attributed to historical performance, evidence, communication, or recipient response;
- organization right, resource, liability, obligation, or expectation produced by prior action;
- program, material, legal, fiscal, evidentiary, or procedural state whose current operation depends on the prior act;
- post-divergence political issue, agenda, pressure, or policy-feedback history.

The chain may be compressed.

It may not be absent.

## 2.6 General relationship versus event-specific obligation

A generated senator may begin with:

```text
general longstanding distrust of Governor K
```

as a bounded synthetic backstory relation when no specific cause is claimed or needed.

The senator may not begin with:

```text
Governor K owes Senator E a vote
because Senator E protected K during a 2028 investigation
```

without the relevant generated investigation, protective act, knowledge, relationship transition, and current commitment/expectation lineage.

The first is background state.

The second is an historical claim and obligation.

## 2.7 Broad reputation versus causal reputation claim

A scenario may initialize a bounded synthetic distribution such as:

```text
Party A is generally associated with fiscal restraint
among some recipient groups at the backstory boundary
```

when the state is explicitly generated, population-specific, time-specific, and not explained through invented real events.

It may not tell the player:

> Party A gained that reputation after defeating the 2027 budget package.

unless that package, defeat, evidence, exposure, and recipient response exist in generated history.

A broad root permits a broad explanation:

> This association predates the detailed generated record.

It does not permit a fictional exact story.

## 2.8 Office and career routes

A synthetic career description may summarize earlier occupations or offices.

When a prior office affects:

- current legal legitimacy;
- seniority;
- eligibility;
- a relationship;
- a public record;
- an institutional right;
- a specific current memory;

its route must include enough generated provenance to support those effects.

An elected office needs a generated election/entitlement/assignment route at the accepted compression.

An appointed office needs a vacancy/selection/procedure/assignment route where applicable.

`Previously served as governor` cannot be a consequence-free flavor line if the game later uses it to grant exact relationships, records, or authority.

## 2.9 Root-admission ledger

Every load-bearing generated-backstory fact must be able to declare:

- fact identity/family and current owner;
- root-admission category;
- effective interval;
- backstory package version/generation identity;
- generation order relative to forward prehistory;
- institutional, geographic, and population constraints;
- support and uncertainty;
- actor/player-knowledge status;
- consumers justifying retention;
- exact historical statements permitted;
- exact historical statements prohibited;
- deepening policy;
- whether a later specific occurrence is required before stronger claims may be made.

## 2.10 No root laundering

**[HARD INVARIANT LC-HISTR03] A forward-generated, player-era, or otherwise occurrence-dependent fact may not be reclassified, summarized, compressed, or migrated into a generated-backstory root merely to remove missing provenance.**

Compression may replace detail with a supported bundle or summary while preserving the fact's root class and occurrence lineage.

It may not change when or how the fact entered the world.

# 3. Three historical clocks

## 3.1 Core distinction

**[HARD INVARIANT LC-HISTR04] The following times are distinct and must be declared when they differ:**

1. **world-state/divergence seam**;
2. **external source-compilation cutoff**;
3. **in-world evidence/actor-knowledge availability time**.

## 3.2 World-state/divergence seam

The world-state seam identifies when inherited state hands off to generated continuation for the applicable fact or process.

It governs which future transitions must be resolved by live owners.

## 3.3 Source-compilation cutoff

The source-compilation cutoff identifies which external publications, corrections, legal materials, maps, statistical revisions, and archival records the scenario-building process was permitted to use.

It may be later than the world-state seam when retrospective evidence improves estimation of conditions at the seam.

## 3.4 Actor-knowledge availability

Actor-knowledge time records when an artifact, record, observation, or communication actually existed and became accessible in-world.

A later source used by the developer does not become earlier actor knowledge.

## 3.5 Retrospective baseline evidence

A scenario builder may use a later-published source to estimate hidden state as of an earlier seam only when:

- the source's later publication time is retained;
- the target/reference period is explicit;
- post-seam occurrences are not imported as if they had already happened;
- the source is not provided to earlier actors;
- decisions between the seam and source publication do not consume the later evidence;
- uncertainty and transformation are declared;
- the calibration package is historically closed at its declared compilation cutoff.

Example:

```text
world seam: T0
retrospective benchmark published: T1 > T0
benchmark describes state near T0
→ scenario builder may improve hidden T0 estimate
→ no actor at T0 receives T1 artifact
→ T0–T1 generated choices cannot use T1 knowledge
```

## 3.6 Future-outcome prohibition

Retrospective evidence may improve an estimate of state at the seam.

It may not be used to condition hidden T0 traits or generated T0–T1 decisions on actual post-seam outcomes in a way that leaks the future.

For example, later observed election results cannot be used to set a fictional actor's T0 private strategy in a world that diverges at T0.

## 3.7 Legal and geographic vintages

Current codification, enacted source law, regulation versions, court decisions, and geographic boundaries may each have separate effective and publication vintages.

The baseline must state which version initializes operative state and which later materials were used only as retrospective source evidence.

# 4. Complete contribution and root coverage

## 4.1 Core invariant

**[HARD INVARIANT LC-HISTR05] A composite current fact must reconcile across the complete semantic contribution basis required by its owner at the accepted resolution. Referencing one convenient ancestor does not establish causal completeness.**

## 4.2 Stock-flow form

Where accounting applies, a current stock may require a relationship such as:

```text
opening stock / inherited root
+ admitted inflows
- admitted outflows
± transfers
± revaluation
± reclassification
± modeled residual and uncertainty
= closing/current stock
```

Not every domain uses this exact arithmetic.

Every domain must still cover the semantic contributions its own process requires.

## 4.3 Population form

Current population and distribution may require:

```text
opening represented population
+ births
- deaths
+ immigration
- emigration
+ internal geographic redistribution
± modeled allocation/reconciliation residual
= current represented population and residence distribution
```

Actor overlays add no additional population weight.

## 4.4 Legal/program form

A current program may require coverage of:

- inherited or enacted authority;
- amendments and effective dates;
- funding/appropriation state;
- implementing institution;
- current rules;
- outstanding obligations/contracts;
- participation and cases;
- supersession, expiry, or court constraints.

A reference to the original law alone is not complete current program provenance.

## 4.5 Composite support

A composite current fact may contain:

- exact components;
- modeled components;
- bounded residuals;
- disputed classifications;
- unknown portions.

The resulting support cannot be stronger than the contribution basis permits.

## 4.6 Step 6 lineage remains controlling

Different IDs are not automatically additive.

Shared roots are not automatically duplicates.

Contribution overlap, transfers, gross/net semantics, reversals, and residuals remain governed by accepted Step 6 coupling/accounting authority.

# 5. Occurrence, bundle, aggregate, and summary taxonomy

## 5.1 Core invariant

**[HARD INVARIANT LC-HISTR06] One canonical occurrence, a compressed process bundle, an aggregate quantity, and a historical summary are distinct objects/claims and may not substitute for one another where identity or sequence matters.**

## 5.2 Canonical occurrence

A canonical occurrence identifies one retained act, transition, or event under its occurrence-family owner.

It can support exact references such as:

- this vote;
- this payment;
- this promise;
- this filing;
- this order;
- this meeting;
- this publication;
- this project milestone.

## 5.3 Compressed process bundle

A bundle records a bounded set or interval of activity under a declared compression method.

It may preserve:

- participating owners/classes;
- interval;
- counts and flows;
- distributions;
- inputs and outputs;
- selected retained occurrences;
- unresolved obligations;
- uncertainty and omissions.

The bundle is not one occurrence merely because it has one ID.

## 5.4 Aggregate quantity

An aggregate quantity records a count, flow, stock, rate, or distribution under its units, universe, geography, and period.

It does not establish the identity or sequence of every contributing occurrence.

## 5.5 Historical summary projection

A summary is a read-only interpretation or presentation over occurrences, bundles, aggregates, current state, evidence, and memory.

It cannot become the source of an exact event.

## 5.6 Specific-reference rule

A load-bearing exact:

- actor memory;
- commitment;
- legal notice;
- receipt;
- relationship change;
- causal attribution;
- accountability claim;
- deadline;
- procedural consequence

must reference a retained occurrence or another semantic target capable of supporting that exact relationship.

A broad bundle may support a broad belief or estimate.

It cannot be unpacked into fictional individual events.

## 5.7 Example

```text
bundle:
Administration A conducted an estimated 40–55 state consultations in 2029.
```

This may support:

- a general organizational-capacity assessment;
- an aggregate activity history;
- a broad governor-network reputation with modeled support.

It does not support:

- Governor B remembers the exact promise made at consultation 17;
- a court received notice at consultation 22;
- Senator C personally attended consultation 31;

unless those occurrences were separately retained.

# 6. Owner-respecting preselection repair

## 6.1 Core invariant

**[HARD INVARIANT LC-HISTR07] A generated candidate world that fails reconciliation may not be made valid by editorially changing a consequential owner output while leaving its causal history unchanged.**

## 6.2 Permitted responses to failure

Before canonical selection, a failed candidate may be:

1. rejected and regenerated;
2. resumed from a valid checkpoint through owner-resolved transitions;
3. regenerated from a modified, versioned backstory/configuration package;
4. migrated through an explicit generation repair that preserves owner authority, occurrence identities, contribution lineage, times, and downstream consistency;
5. left unresolved where the accepted abstraction legitimately permits uncertainty rather than contradiction.

## 6.3 Invalid patching

Forbidden:

```text
Congress elected 51–49
current Senate assignment says 49–51
→ edit assignment totals only
```

```text
program outlays do not reconcile
→ change one outlay field without changing payment history
```

```text
actor memory has no supporting event
→ add a dated backstory note after generation
```

```text
court order conflicts with legal state
→ suppress the order from opening presentation
```

## 6.4 Generation repair activity

A permitted preselection generation repair must declare:

- failed invariant;
- affected owners/facts;
- source candidate/version;
- repair method;
- regenerated or migrated occurrences/state;
- identities preserved or replaced;
- contribution and evidence consequences;
- resulting new candidate identity;
- validation result.

The repaired candidate is a new candidate world/version.

The abandoned candidate is noncanonical.

## 6.5 Conditioning and rejection

A generator may condition or reject based on declared:

- consistency;
- product feasibility;
- configured starting constraints;
- variation requirements;
- player-selected valid setup bounds.

It may not edit resolved owner state after the fact merely to satisfy those constraints.

It may not use a hidden drama score to choose among valid worlds.

# 7. Controlling application to generated actor history

## 7.1 Example: senator career

A generated senator may have:

```text
background root:
former attorney and state legislator
```

at compressed support when exact intermediate events are not consumed.

If the game uses:

```text
won a close 2030 Senate race
```

for current electoral vulnerability, that election requires generated contest/result/entitlement/assignment provenance.

If the game uses:

```text
owes Governor R for decisive endorsement
```

the endorsement, receipt, campaign consequence, relationship transition, and current expectation require retained occurrence/bundle support.

## 7.2 Example: party reputation

A broad synthetic pre-forward reputation may be initialized as a bounded recipient distribution.

Post-divergence change in that reputation must arise through:

- actor/party conduct;
- evidence and communication;
- recipient exposure and cognition;
- polling or assessment where observed.

The generator cannot overwrite the reputation at the opening to match the desired election result.

## 7.3 Example: organization commitment

A generated organization may begin with a general ideological relationship to a party.

An exact commitment such as:

```text
organization will withhold field staff unless Promise P is honored
```

requires the promise, organization receipt/adoption, resources, and current decision state.

## 7.4 Example: Cabinet rivalry

Two generated officials may begin with a bounded longstanding interpersonal rivalry.

If a decision package explains that rivalry through a specific earlier policy fight, the fight and relevant memories require generated history.

If no such occurrence is retained, the player-facing explanation must remain broad:

> Staff records indicate a longstanding rivalry; its detailed origin is outside retained history.
