## R4-01 — individualized actor/population linkage

The original candidate prohibited double-counting named actors but still allowed a named human to exist without a closed relationship to the canonical Population substrate.

The repaired contract therefore required every individualized human actor to declare exactly one effective population-linkage status:

1. `EXACT_CANONICAL_POPULATION_IDENTITY`
2. `STATISTICALLY_REPRESENTED_WITHOUT_EXACT_IDENTITY`
3. `OUTSIDE_MODELED_ORDINARY_POPULATION_SCOPE`

The linkage must preserve support status, permitted joins, population-weight reconciliation, lifecycle behavior, and the rule that a statistically represented actor may not inherit exact household, residence, demographic, material-exposure, recipient-history, eligibility, belief, or historical facts from an aggregate.

## C4-02 — one canonical entity across semantic roles

Participant-family labels such as `institution`, `organization`, `firm`, `hospital`, `university`, `media organization`, or similar semantic roles do not authorize duplicate canonical identities for one real entity.

Resources, contracts, members, offices, actions, liabilities, facilities, and records must remain owned once.

## C4-03 — material truth cannot directly rewrite actor state

A hidden material condition cannot directly alter an actor's beliefs, objectives, priorities, or choices.

Material state may matter through legitimate routes such as direct experience, operational state, received evidence, records, communications, forecasts, duties, or other bounded observation.

## C4-04 — aggregate participants are not hive minds

An aggregate participant ordinarily produces a distribution, count, flow, or set of unit-level actions.

A single collective position, commitment, or act requires an actual coordination, organization, market-clearing, legal, governance, bargaining, or procedural mechanism that produces that collective result.
