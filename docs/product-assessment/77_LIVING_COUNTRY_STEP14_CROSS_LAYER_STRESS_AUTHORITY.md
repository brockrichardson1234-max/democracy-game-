# 4. The economic core now has a closed final-use relationship

The first Step-14 audit found the most important remaining gap in the material spine.

Step 13 had already established:

```text
Production
↔ Labor
↔ Household Resources
↔ Prices
```

but had not fully specified how household resources become **desired purchases**, and how desired purchases become **actual exchange** when seller supply, capacity, inventory, imports and prices differ.

Without that closure, a hidden `EconomyHealth`, GDP path, or invisible equilibrium routine could still choose output, sales, consumption and prices.

Step 14 therefore accepts the following distinction.

## 4.1 Household demand intention

Households/household-resource-consumption state own desired/planned demand at the supported category/population resolution.

Proof alias:

```text
MECH.HH.DEMAND_INTENT
```

Minimum depth floor for load-bearing categories:

> **CORE CAUSAL at bounded category/population resolution.**

This is a narrow supplement to Step 13's depth matrix.

Core does not mean individual shopping simulation.

The mechanism may compactly represent how admitted state such as:

- disposable resources;
- taxes/transfers;
- category prices;
- debt service;
- borrowing conditions;
- savings/buffer state;
- household composition;
- major Housing/health/transport obligations;
- prior demand state;
- expectations where supported

changes desired category expenditure or quantity.

The exact behavioral model is unaccepted.

## 4.2 Demand intention is not a transaction

The following is forbidden:

```text
households want $100B of goods
→ firms sold $100B of goods
```

Desired demand does not itself create:

- seller output;
- realized purchase;
- actual household consumption;
- government procurement completion;
- inventory draw;
- imports;
- price movement;
- seller revenue.

# 5. Realized exchange/final use has one semantic relationship owner

For every load-bearing buyer/seller interaction, the design must have one declared category/market/service exchange or realization process.

Proof-family alias:

```text
MECH.EXCHANGE.<supported-category-or-market>
```

The exact technical implementation may live inside a material mechanism, service system, market process, procurement process, or another appropriate architecture.

Semantically, however:

> **One process/relationship owns the realized exchange/final-use flow at its supported resolution.**

There is no universal `MarketSystem` owning all demand, supply, prices, output and welfare.

## 5.1 Separately owned inputs

The exchange process may consume:

### Buyer intentions

- household category demand;
- business investment demand;
- intermediate-input demand;
- lawful government procurement/purchase intent;
- external/export demand.

### Seller/supply state

- Production output/offers;
- service-provider capacity;
- inventories;
- imports;
- infrastructure/logistics constraints;
- energy/material constraints.

### Price state

- applicable canonical price from the Price owner;
- administered/contract/regulatory price constraints.

### Institutional constraints

- eligibility;
- queues;
- contracts;
- procurement procedure;
- rationing;
- legal restrictions;
- geography/access;
- timing.

Consuming these facts does not transfer their ownership to the exchange process.

## 5.2 Realized exchange identity

At the accepted resolution, the realized exchange may preserve relevant:

- buyer scope;
- seller scope;
- category/product/service;
- quantity;
- expenditure/revenue amount;
- applied price;
- time/period;
- geography/market;
- unmet desired demand/backorder/rationing;
- inventory/import contribution;
- transaction lineage;
- support/uncertainty.

An aggregate flow is permitted.

The design does not require every retail transaction to become an object.

## 5.3 One transaction, multiple owner consequences

The same realized exchange can create receiver-owned consequences for:

- Household Resources actual expenditure;
- household actual consumption/use where retained;
- Production seller revenue/sales/inventory;
- Trade import/export flows;
- Public Finance transaction-linked taxes/customs where supported.

Those records reference one semantic exchange.

They are not independent injections.

# 6. Five economic concepts may not collapse

The following are now explicitly distinct:

```text
desired demand
≠ realized transaction
≠ actual household consumption/use
≠ seller production/output
≠ seller sales/revenue
```

This supports cases such as:

- households desire more than suppliers can deliver;
- inventories satisfy purchases while current production falls;
- production enters inventory without being sold;
- imports satisfy domestic purchases without equivalent domestic output;
- service capacity limits use;
- administered prices limit/lag price adjustment;
- government obligates money before goods/services are delivered.

This distinction is mandatory wherever supported gameplay depends on it.

# 8. Circular economic causality requires explicit time

The material economy can contain cycles.

Example:

```text
Production
→ Labor income
→ Household Resources
→ household demand intention
→ realized exchange
→ Production sales/inventory/output response
→ Labor
```

Another:

```text
Federal Reserve policy
→ monetary/credit conditions
→ mortgages / household debt / business investment / federal refinancing
→ Housing / demand / Production / fiscal state
→ measurements and forecasts
→ later Federal Reserve decision
```

These loops do not authorize uncontrolled same-time recursion.

A later implementation must use:

- lags;
- interval semantics;
- explicit reconsideration boundaries;
- or a bounded solver with deterministic convergence/failure semantics.

The result cannot depend on callback, map, subscription, or registration order.

# 9. Nonlinear receiver response remains sovereign

Cross-domain contribution lineage solves overlap and double-count questions.

It does not make downstream response additive.

A receiving mechanism may legitimately have:

- eligibility thresholds;
- capacity constraints;
- inventories;
- substitution;
- household composition effects;
- debt limits;
- nonlinear price response;
- legal/procedural gates;
- queue congestion;
- geographic bottlenecks.

Thus two disjoint upstream changes can interact nonlinearly inside a receiver.

The receiver owns that joint transformation.

# 10. Monetary/credit state is structurally sufficient only if exposures stay distinct

The Federal Reserve/FOMC remains autonomous from the President.

A Fed policy act may influence multiple monetary/credit conditions.

The design explicitly rejects one universal effective interest rate copied into every receiver.

## 10.1 Applicable condition families

A later model may distinguish supported state such as:

- short-term policy/market rates;
- yield-curve/maturity conditions;
- mortgage origination/refinancing conditions;
- consumer-credit rates/spreads;
- business-credit rates/spreads;
- risk/liquidity conditions;
- Treasury financing conditions.

The exact representation is deferred.

## 10.2 Contract and maturity matter

Receivers consume only applicable state.

Therefore:

- an existing fixed-rate mortgage does not reprice automatically;
- adjustable debt may reset according to its terms;
- a new mortgage reacts through current origination conditions;
- business investment reacts through applicable financing and demand state;
- Federal Public Finance reacts to debt stock, maturity, refinancing and new borrowing;
- a private credit-spread shock may differ from a Treasury-rate movement.

The Fed decision does not assign each downstream result.

# 12. Federal payments do not own state or material outcomes

The accepted federalism stress path is:

```text
law / fiscal authority / program
→ exact federal award / obligation / payment
→ state/local fiscal receipt
→ state-owned allocation, substitution,
  matching, delay, refusal or administration
→ exact project/admin process where supported
→ structured/core material receiver
→ owner-resolved material result after latency
```

The federal government can know exactly that it paid State-S.

That does not mean it knows or owns exactly what material outcome occurs next.

Identical federal transfers may produce different state/material outcomes because:

- state priorities differ;
- matching/flexibility differs;
- procurement/permitting differs;
- existing network bottlenecks differ;
- costs differ;
- state funds are substituted or supplemented;
- implementation fails or delays.

A grant payment cannot directly create jobs, traffic improvement, Housing units, or political credit.

# 14. Measurement revision changes knowledge, not history

An underlying recession/shortage/expansion is owned by material mechanisms.

Official statistics measure that state imperfectly and with vintages.

The accepted stress relationship is:

```text
material state at T1
→ preliminary estimate at T2
→ actors decide with T2 evidence
→ revised/benchmarked estimate at T3
→ later reinterpretation
```

T3 may alter current belief and political argument.

It does not alter:

- T1 material truth;
- T2 publication;
- who received T2;
- the decision made with T2 information.

A revision is not time travel.


# 17. Investigations do not create scandals or misconduct

The accepted investigative stress path begins with an underlying occurrence or record owned elsewhere.

Possible discovery routes include:

- inspector general;
- congressional staff;
- journalist/source;
- whistleblower/organization;
- court/filing records.

An investigator or journalist can discover evidence that did not previously exist as public knowledge.

They cannot manufacture the underlying misconduct because the political simulation needs conflict.

Removing one discovery path does not automatically remove all others.

A later audit/court/investigation that clears the allegation creates new evidence/history without erasing prior allegations, coverage, beliefs, hearings, or White House decisions.

- weaker preliminary labor evidence;
- a governor request;

- authority;
- information;
- administration queues;
- deadlines;
- Congress;
- state requests;
- evidence;
- fiscal/legal routes.

```text

→ show Crisis Response Menu
```

Changing state fiscal capacity should affect assistance needs without changing the port event.

This perturbational separability is central to Step 14 acceptance.

# 26. Step 14 verdict

The Step-14 question is answered at design-contract level:
