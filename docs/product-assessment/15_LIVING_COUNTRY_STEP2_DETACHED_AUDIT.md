# What passes cleanly

## P-01 — Interface rather than universal schema

The candidate explicitly states that the grammar is a semantic participation contract and prohibits interpreting its headings as a required runtime class, base interface, table, or event payload.

Housing, employment, healthcare, energy, public safety, and public finance are shown with different:

- native entities;
- stocks and flows;
- temporal cadence;
- geography;
- observations;
- constraints;
- outputs.

This is sufficient to prevent the common grammar from becoming a mislabeled Democracy-style collection of interchangeable meters.

## P-02 — Fact forms remain semantically distinct

The candidate distinguishes:

- stocks;
- flows;
- events;
- rates/ratios;
- prices/indices;
- distributions;
- queues/pipelines;
- capacity/constraints;
- network state;
- statuses;
- latent conditions.

It requires the domain to declare only the forms it actually needs.

This supports materially different models while preventing common stock-flow and rate-aggregation errors.

## P-03 — Units and denominators are load-bearing

Cross-owner quantities require sufficient unit semantics to distinguish:

- counts and rates;
- percentages and percentage points;
- nominal and real dollars;
- price indices and money balances;
- capacity and output;
- physical units with different dimensions;
- unknown and zero.

Conversions require an explicit owner, method, time/geographic scope, and uncertainty where relevant.

A unitless `ECONOMY_WORSE = 4` payload is not sufficient.

## P-04 — Time is plural but reconciled

The candidate does not confuse daily world continuity with daily mutation.

It permits exact-event, daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual, irregular, and mixed domain processes while requiring dated order and separating:

- as-of/effective time;
- flow interval;
- decision time;
- execution/completion;
- release/receipt;
- deadlines/reconsideration.

That is compatible with the accepted Step 5 time model.

## P-05 — Geography supports different causal frames

Residence, workplace, service area, jurisdiction, market, network region, project site, and exposure footprint may differ.

The candidate requires explicit aggregation and prevents ungrounded disaggregation.

It does not force all domains to use state/DC or congressional-district resolution merely because those structures already exist.

## P-06 — One population remains intact

Domains may associate facts with the canonical population while keeping employment, income, Housing, health, and other facts with their semantic owners.

The candidate explicitly rejects cloned economic people, patients, renters, audiences, beneficiaries, and voters.

Cohort refinement is justified only when a process consumes the distinction, and represented population must reconcile through split/merge.

## P-07 — Cross-domain handoff preserves receiver ownership

The handshake is:

```text
source-owned fact/output
→ typed unit/time/geography/entity/provenance
→ receiver validates and transforms
→ receiver-owned state changes
```

The source does not dictate the downstream result.

The candidate also distinguishes direct transfer from shared cause, correlation, and political interpretation.

This is enough for Step 6 coupling design to proceed later without permitting direct cross-owner mutation now.

## P-08 — Material truth and observation remain separate

Every domain must expose what can be observed and by whom, but the domain does not own external measurement artifacts.

Direct records, operations, surveys, administrative reports, private estimates, investigations, forecasts, and claims remain distinguishable.

The President cannot receive canonical truth merely because a domain stores it.

## P-09 — Calibration and history remain non-shadow owners

Initialization requires source vintage, coverage, transformation, units, geography, population/entity mapping, date, uncertainty, and reconciliation.

Calibration stops being live authority after initialization.

The candidate preserves one canonical occurrence identity and permits causal chains to terminate later at legitimate baseline roots without pretending the calibration package remains current world state.

## P-10 — Quiet material reality is valid

The explicit quiet path is one of the candidate’s strongest safeguards:

```text
serious condition
→ delayed or ambiguous measurement
→ local experience
→ weak organization
→ limited reporting
→ no valid White House receipt
→ no presidential interruption yet
```

This rules out a hidden issue selector whose job is to turn every severe condition into drama.

The candidate supplies concrete rejection tests for:

- stock-flow confusion;

# Nonblocking clarifications for the authority receipt

## C-01 — A domain declaration may name multiple fact owners, but no declaration may defer ownership indefinitely

The candidate correctly says a conceptual or player-facing domain may contain several semantic subowners.

The authority receipt should clarify:

> A domain declaration may group several owners for design/navigation, but every load-bearing mutable fact must still identify one owner before the domain is accepted for coupling or depth assignment. “The economy owns it” is not sufficient when the relevant fact is actually owned by Labor, Prices, Income, Industry, Public Finance, or another declared subowner.

This prevents flexible grouping from becoming an ownership escape hatch.

## C-02 — Market/transaction conditions, modeled aggregates, and published indices must remain distinct

The candidate discusses prices and indices correctly but should bind one clarification:

> A domain may own underlying transaction/market price conditions or modeled canonical aggregate price state at its selected abstraction. A statistical agency’s published price index remains a measurement artifact. A staff or private estimate remains another artifact/projection. Those objects may share a referent but do not become alternate owners of one value.

This avoids later ambiguity between “actual inflation/price conditions” and the CPI/PCE release players see.

## C-03 — “Admissible consumers” limits semantic interpretation; it does not authorize the source to prescribe receiver behavior

The candidate lets outputs declare admissible consumers or general meaning.

The authority receipt should clarify:

> A source may declare what its output semantically represents and which receiver prerequisites are required. It may not prescribe the receiving domain’s state change. The receiver independently determines admissibility, conversion, delay, and response through its own accepted process.

This preserves receiver ownership and prevents a typed output from becoming a sophisticated direct modifier.

# Attempted falsification

## Test A — Create one generic domain engine

**Exploit:** Implement every domain as level/pressure/capacity/trend.

**Result:** rejected by LC-G01/LC-G02 and the domain contrast demonstrations.

## Test B — Send a unitless economic shock

**Exploit:** Energy emits `INFLATION +5`; Prices and voters apply it directly.

**Result:** rejected. The handoff must name the actual energy quantity/price/supply event, time, geography, and affected entities. The Prices owner determines its response; voters require observation and recipient-state processes.

## Test C — Hide gross labor churn

**Exploit:** Retain only unemployment’s net monthly change.

**Result:** rejected when hires/separations create different institutional workload, eligibility, geography, or political consequences.

## Test D — Infer local truth from national average

**Exploit:** Spread a national Housing or employment rate proportionally across every state/metro.

**Result:** rejected unless an explicit modeled disaggregation with uncertainty and aggregate reconciliation is declared.

## Test E — Clone population for each domain

**Exploit:** Labor has workers, Health has patients, Elections have voters, and Media has audiences with independent weights.

**Result:** rejected. Domain facts associate with one canonical population identity.

## Test F — Let a policy input dictate its result

**Exploit:** A law emits `jobsCreated = 500000` into Labor.

**Result:** rejected. Law/program/fiscal facts create typed inputs; Labor owns the material response.

## Test G — Make a measurement current truth

**Exploit:** Published unemployment or CPI overwrites canonical material state.

**Result:** rejected. Measurements/artifacts remain observations.

## Test H — Force monthly updates everywhere

**Exploit:** All domains change on the first of the month because the UI uses months.

**Result:** rejected. Domain cadence follows causal need.

## Test I — Generate drama from severity

**Exploit:** Any condition above a severity threshold becomes a national issue and presidential alert.

**Result:** rejected by the quiet-state contract and Step 5 attention provenance.

## Test J — Let the sender own the receiver

**Exploit:** Labor emits a typed `householdIncomeLoss` object that directly sets Income state.

**Result:** rejected under clarification C-03. Labor may emit employment/wage facts; Income owns its transformation.

No attempted exploit requires a structural Step 2 rewrite.
