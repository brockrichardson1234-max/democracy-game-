# 1. Executive design verdict

## 1.1 Central answer

**[DI]** Living Country should represent observation and measurement as:

> **Versioned evidence-production processes between canonical reality and actor knowledge. Reality is owned by its semantic domain; observation processes acquire bounded evidence; methods transform evidence into estimates or assessments; information artifacts preserve methodology, uncertainty, access, and vintage; actors receive only artifacts or direct experience available to them; later revisions create new evidence states without rewriting what anyone knew earlier.**

The accepted conceptual chain should be:

```text
canonical reality or occurrence
→ eligible observation/data-generating route
→ source records, sample, administrative data, direct observation,
  investigation evidence, sensor data, or intelligence reporting
→ measurement/analytic process with declared method and universe
→ estimate, count, assessment, forecast, or evidence product
→ artifact creation with vintage, methodology, uncertainty, and access
→ release / authorized availability / delivery
→ actor or institution receipt
→ actor-owned interpretation, belief, forecast use, or decision
→ later source data or method changes may produce a new revision/vintage
→ prior published artifacts and prior actor knowledge remain historical facts
```

The system must prevent:

- canonical truth automatically becoming official statistics;
- one current estimate overwriting all prior vintages;
- a measurement artifact owning the underlying material condition;
- a forecast reading future simulation state;
- polling reading exact hidden population belief;
- an intelligence assessment exposing debug truth;
- an administration or President silently altering an independent statistical release;
- missing, suppressed, unobserved, zero, and not-applicable values collapsing together;
- methodology changes rewriting what actors knew at an earlier date;
- one generic confidence number pretending to cover sampling error, model error, source credibility, forecast probability, and analytic confidence.

## 1.2 Five things that must stay distinct

**[DI — HARD INVARIANT LC-OBS01]** The following are never aliases merely because they refer to the same phenomenon:

1. **canonical condition** — what the relevant world owner says is materially/institutionally true;
2. **observation/source record** — what a valid collection route actually records;
3. **measurement/estimate/assessment** — what a method infers from available evidence;
4. **information artifact/release** — what is recorded or published for some audience/access class;
5. **recipient interpretation** — what an actor, institution, organization, or population concludes after receipt.

Example:

```text
Labor-owned employment state
≠ establishment-survey sample records
≠ estimated payroll employment
≠ published Employment Situation release
≠ White House interpretation of the release
≠ public belief about jobs
```

## 1.3 Product reason

The presidential game requires a country where:

- bad conditions may exist before government measures them;
- preliminary data may be wrong;
- estimates may be revised after a political decision has already been made;
- two legitimate sources may disagree because they measure different universes or use different methods;
- private estimates may move before official releases;
- intelligence may be high-confidence but still probabilistic;
- polling may suggest political movement while remaining an imperfect measurement of population political state;
- a President may make a defensible decision from evidence that later turns out to be incomplete;
- opposition claims may exploit real revisions without those claims rewriting material history.

Without this layer, Step 5 attention becomes omniscient and Step 6 coupling eventually collapses back into a dashboard reading the world root.

# 3. Observation-family taxonomy

The game should not call every informational artifact a `measurement`.

## 3.1 Direct authoritative record

A record may be authoritative about a specific institutional act or transaction because its owner directly records that fact.

Examples:

- a signed law;
- an agency payment ledger entry;
- a filed court order;
- an office assignment;
- a recorded grant award;
- a contract signed by the organization that owns it.

This can be exact about the record while remaining incomplete evidence about a broader material phenomenon.

A tax filing may be exact about what was filed. It is not automatically exact household economic truth.

## 3.2 Administrative-data observation

Administrative systems may observe portions of reality through program operations, claims, tax records, applications, permits, payroll reports, hospital records, inspections, or other operational processes.

Administrative data must declare:

- legal/program universe;
- participation or filing coverage;
- effective/reference period;
- units;
- known exclusions;
- processing and correction status;
- whether values are claims, adjudicated facts, payments, reported facts, or derived administrative measures.

Administrative coverage is not automatically the target population universe.

## 3.3 Census or near-complete enumeration

A collection may approach complete coverage of a defined universe.

It still requires:

- a target universe;
- reference time;
- coverage rules;
- missing/late records;
- editing and classification;
- revisions where supported;
- methodology and confidentiality treatment.

Near-complete is not metaphysically perfect.

## 3.4 Sample survey

A survey observes a sample and estimates characteristics of a target universe.

The process may need:

- sample frame;
- selection design;
- weights;
- response/nonresponse treatment;
- imputation;
- estimation method;
- standard error or margin of error;
- design effect where relevant;
- coverage and nonsampling limitations;
- release/vintage schedule.

## 3.5 Sensor or operational telemetry

Physical systems may produce sensor, network, operational, or telemetry observations.

The observation remains distinct from the underlying condition because sensors can have:

- coverage gaps;
- calibration error;
- latency;
- outages;
- aggregation rules;
- thresholding;
- false positive/negative behavior.

## 3.6 Investigation and evidentiary discovery

An investigation may discover evidence previously unavailable to decision-makers.

Potential owners include:

- Inspector General;
- auditor;
- legislative committee;
- law-enforcement/investigative institution where later supported;
- journalist/outlet under later media depth;
- internal compliance office;
- court/litigant discovery process.

Investigation can create new evidence artifacts.

It may not create the underlying misconduct or failure being investigated.

## 3.7 Private estimate / nowcast

A private organization, bank, research group, trade association, pollster, campaign, market-data service, or analyst may estimate current conditions using its own sources and model.

The estimate must retain:

- owner;
- as-of/vintage;
- methodology or methodology class;
- accessible source base;
- target estimand;
- uncertainty/model limitations;
- publication/access rules.

Different private estimates may disagree legitimately.

## 3.8 Forecast

A forecast concerns future state or future measured outcomes.

**[DI — HARD INVARIANT LC-OBS02] A forecast may never read future canonical simulation state.**

It must be generated from information available at the forecast's as-of time plus declared assumptions/model/scenarios.

A forecast must preserve, where applicable:

- forecast origin time;
- horizon;
- scenario or baseline assumptions;
- probability distribution or interval;
- model/version;
- conditioning policy/law assumptions;
- source-vintage references;
- forecast uncertainty;
- later forecast-error evaluation without retroactive correction of the original forecast.

## 3.9 Intelligence assessment

An intelligence assessment is an analytic product over source reporting, collection, known facts, assumptions, and analytic judgment.

It must separate:

- underlying source reports/evidence;
- source credibility/reliability where represented;
- analyst assumptions;
- judgments;
- likelihood/probability;
- analytic confidence;
- alternative hypotheses where load-bearing;
- classification/access;
- dissent or differing analytic judgments where supported.

Intelligence assessment is not a direct window into foreign or hidden canonical truth.

## 3.10 Poll

A poll measures a defined political/opinion population imperfectly.

It may use probability or nonprobability designs later accepted by the project.

A poll must declare enough semantics to distinguish:

- target population;
- sample/recruitment method;
- field dates;
- sample size;
- question wording/order where load-bearing;
- weighting/calibration basis;
- mode;
- sampling error where meaningful;
- model-based precision if used;
- nonresponse/coverage/model limitations;
- sponsor/client/access;
- release/vintage.

A poll does not own population belief, candidate preference, approval, salience, or turnout state.

# 5. Estimand and universe integrity

## 5.1 Similar labels do not imply same concept

Two measures called `employment`, `income`, `inflation`, `coverage`, `crime`, or `approval` may target different universes or definitions.

The game must preserve distinctions when they can change interpretation or action.

Examples:

- household versus establishment employment;
- resident versus workplace employment;
- persons versus jobs;
- cash income versus disposable income;
- headline versus core price indices;
- insured at interview versus enrolled at administrative cutoff;
- reported crime versus victimization estimate;
- registered voters versus likely voters.

## 5.2 No silent universe conversion

A measure for one universe may not become exact truth for another universe through a label match.

A statewide estimate cannot become exact district state without an explicit geographic/population transformation and support classification under Steps 2–3.

## 5.3 Exact record is not exact phenomenon

An authoritative record can be exact about what the record says while remaining incomplete for the phenomenon.

Example:

```text
UI tax record exactly reports covered payroll employment filed by employer
≠ exact total labor-market employment in all work arrangements
```

# 6. Time and evidence chronology

## 6.1 Required temporal distinctions

A measurement/assessment may need distinct:

- condition effective time;
- reference/observation interval;
- collection/acquisition time;
- processing time;
- artifact creation time;
- internal availability time;
- public release time;
- recipient receipt time;
- revision time;
- benchmark/rebase time.

These may be different.

## 6.2 No retrocausal knowledge

**[DI — HARD INVARIANT LC-OBS04]** Actors may not use a measurement, revision, report, source, or estimate before its valid availability/receipt time.

A June benchmark revision can change the current historical statistical series.

It cannot become something the President knew in March.

## 6.3 Same-time ordering

When collection, release, legal indexation, receipt, or decisions share a timestamp, ordering must be deterministic through explicit boundary semantics rather than handler registration order.

# 7. Uncertainty taxonomy

One `confidence = 0.72` field cannot carry every kind of uncertainty.

The design must distinguish, where relevant:

## 7.1 Sampling uncertainty

Uncertainty due to observing a sample rather than the full target universe.

Possible representations include standard error, margin of error, confidence interval, design-based variance, or another accepted method.

## 7.2 Coverage uncertainty/error

The observation frame may omit or misclassify parts of the target universe.

## 7.3 Nonresponse and missing-data uncertainty

Some selected or expected records may be absent, late, incomplete, or imputed.

## 7.4 Measurement and reporting error

Respondents, sensors, administrative reports, or classification processes may record inaccurate values.

## 7.5 Model uncertainty

A modeled estimate depends on assumptions, functional form, priors, calibration, or other modeling choices.

## 7.6 Revision uncertainty

A preliminary estimate may predictably change as more complete source data arrive or benchmarking occurs.

## 7.7 Forecast uncertainty

Future outcomes are uncertain even when current state is measured well.

A forecast probability is not the same thing as measurement confidence.

## 7.8 Intelligence source/analytic uncertainty

Analytic confidence, source credibility, information gaps, contrary reporting, and likelihood judgments remain separate where represented.

## 7.9 Abstraction limitation

The simulation may not retain enough detail to answer a desired query exactly. This is not statistical sampling error; it is a model-support limitation under Steps 2–3.

## 7.10 Actor epistemic uncertainty

An actor can be uncertain because it lacks access, has not received an artifact, distrusts a source, or possesses conflicting evidence.

That uncertainty belongs to the actor, not the measurement process itself.

# 8. Release, access, and statistical independence

## 8.1 Artifact existence and release differ

A product may be:

- collected but incomplete;
- internally estimated;
- under quality review;
- finalized but embargoed;
- available to a restricted lawful audience;
- publicly released;
- superseded by a revision.

Those are different states.

## 8.2 Statistical independence

**[DI]** Where the scenario includes an official statistical agency/unit with accepted statistical independence, policy officials may not silently choose its estimate, suppress an unfavorable release, alter methodology for one political result, or rewrite a statistical artifact through ordinary policy preference.

If later extreme-power gameplay supports attempted interference, that attempt must be explicit actor/institutional history with applicable legal, organizational, personnel, public, and credibility consequences.

It still does not convert the preferred political number into canonical material truth.

## 8.3 Confidentiality and exclusive statistical use

Confidential respondent-level statistical data may be inaccessible to policy offices even when a statistical agency sits inside the same executive department.

The President's executive authority does not automatically mean exact access to every confidential response, protected microrecord, source identity, or raw statistical file.

Access must follow applicable law, institution, classification, statistical confidentiality, and accepted scenario rules.

## 8.4 Embargo and prerelease

If the game later supports legitimate prerelease access, the recipient, access rule, time, embargo, and permitted use must be explicit.

No generic `WhiteHouseKnowsOfficialDataEarly = true` is accepted.

# 9. Vintage and revision contract

## 9.1 Immutable publication occurrence

**[DI — HARD INVARIANT LC-OBS05]** A released artifact/vintage is a historical occurrence and may not be silently mutated into its later revised values.

If an estimate changes, create a new revision/vintage linked to the prior one.

## 9.2 Current statistical series versus as-known history

The game may expose two different legitimate views:

1. **current/revised series** — the latest accepted statistical estimate for historical periods;
2. **as-known-at-the-time series** — the artifacts actually available to actors on a historical date.

They are not interchangeable.

A retrospective presidency record should be able to show:

```text
March decision used preliminary employment estimate +120k
June revised March estimate to -20k
```

without pretending the President had June's revision in March.

## 9.3 Revision-cause taxonomy

A revision may arise from:

- late or additional source records;
- updated administrative universe counts;
- benchmark/reconciliation;
- seasonal adjustment recalculation;
- corrected processing error;
- updated population controls;
- revised weights or imputations;
- methodological change;
- classification change;
- rebase/rebenchmark;
- source-agency revision.

The revision should preserve the reason class where it matters.

## 9.4 Method revision versus data revision

A new value from new source data is not semantically identical to a new value from a changed method.

Both may be legitimate.

The artifact lineage should let later users distinguish them where politically or analytically relevant.

## 9.5 Corrections

A correction to a mistaken publication creates corrected lineage.

It does not erase that recipients may already have seen and acted on the erroneous artifact.

---

# 10. Forecast contract

## 10.1 Forecast information set

A forecast must freeze its input information set or input-vintage references at forecast origin.

Later data revisions do not automatically alter the old forecast.

## 10.2 Baseline versus scenario

A baseline projection, conditional scenario, stress case, and prediction may imply different conditioning assumptions.

The game must not present one as another.

## 10.3 Probability and confidence

A forecast may state probability distributions or intervals where supported.

Model confidence/quality remains a separate concept from probability of the future outcome.

## 10.4 Forecast evaluation

Later outcomes may produce forecast-error records:

```text
forecast vintage
→ later observed/revised outcome
→ forecast error under declared comparison basis
```

The error record does not rewrite the original forecast.

## 10.5 Policy conditioning

Forecasts may be conditional on current law, current policy, announced proposals, assumed enacted policy, or scenarios.

The conditioning basis must be declared where it changes interpretation.

---

# 11. Intelligence analytic contract

## 11.1 Source and judgment separation

An intelligence product must not blur source report and analyst judgment.

The system may represent:

- source/report identity;
- collection date;
- source credibility/reliability assessment;
- information gaps;
- contrary reporting;
- analyst assumptions;
- analytic judgment;
- probability/likelihood;
- confidence;
- alternatives;
- dissent;
- classification and access.

## 11.2 Probability is not confidence

**[DI — HARD INVARIANT LC-OBS06]** Likelihood of an assessed proposition and confidence in the analytic basis are distinct.

Example:

```text
60% likelihood actor will escalate
with low analytic confidence
```

is semantically different from:

```text
60% analyst confidence that escalation will occur
```

The game may later choose a player-facing vocabulary, but the underlying distinction must survive.

## 11.3 Intelligence does not dictate policy

An intelligence office may assess risks and implications.

The policy decision remains with the appropriate political/institutional actor under Step 4–5 authority.

## 11.4 Change in assessment

A later assessment should be able to explain whether judgment changed because of:

- new evidence;
- source reassessment;
- changed assumptions;
- resolved uncertainty;
- actual world change;
- methodological/analytic correction.

Prior assessments remain historical artifacts.

# 12. Polling contract

## 12.1 Poll is a measurement product

Polls measure population-owned political/opinion state imperfectly.

They do not directly modify that state.

## 12.2 Target population matters

Adults, citizens, registered voters, likely voters, party-primary electorates, and issue-specific populations are distinct universes.

A poll cannot silently convert among them.

## 12.3 Probability versus nonprobability designs

If a probability sample is used, sampling-error semantics may be available.

If a nonprobability sample is used, a model-based precision estimate must be labeled as such; conventional margin-of-sampling-error language may not be invented without the required basis.

## 12.4 House effects and methodological differences

Different pollsters may produce differing results from:

- target universe;
- sampling/recruitment;
- weighting;
- likely-voter model;
- mode;
- question wording/order;
- field dates;
- nonresponse;
- modeling assumptions.

The game should not need a canonical `PollsterBias` scalar unless a later model justifies a context-specific error process.

## 12.5 Internal and public polling

An administration or campaign may possess private polling not available to the public.

Public poll release is a separate information artifact/action.

Succession does not automatically transfer private campaign polling to a new administration.

# 13. Administrative records and official statistics

Administrative data and statistical products may interact but do not collapse.

Example:

```text
UI payroll reports
→ administrative records
→ BLS/QCEW-like universe counts
→ benchmark input to CES-like estimates
→ revised statistical series
```

The administrative records retain their legal/operational meaning.

The statistical agency owns the statistical transformation and estimate.

A policy office consuming the statistical release does not gain ownership of the underlying respondent records.

# 14. Measurement and Step 6 coupling

A measurement product can be consumed by another world owner only when a valid coupling exists.

Examples:

- statute explicitly indexes benefits to published CPI-like release;
- formula grant uses an officially defined population estimate;
- agency trigger depends on a legally defined measured threshold;
- contract references a published index.

In those cases:

```text
material reality
→ statistical measurement/release
→ separate law/program/contract consumes release
→ receiving owner calculates legal/administrative consequence
```

The measurement still does not become material truth.

## 14.1 Measurement feedback must not self-authenticate

If policy changes because of a measurement and then changes the measured phenomenon, later measurements observe the resulting world through their normal methods.

The previous estimate does not become a causal shortcut into the material domain except through the explicit law/policy coupling.

# 15. Measurement and Step 5 attention

A presidential interruption may arise from a measurement only after valid administration receipt and bounded interpretation.

Example:

```text
Labor condition deteriorates
→ monthly survey/reference period
→ preliminary release
→ economic-policy office receives release
→ staff evaluates revision risk and regional composition
→ matter may or may not cross an accepted escalation threshold
→ President may be briefed
```

The scheduler may know canonical unemployment internally.

It may not interrupt the President before a valid observational/administrative route exists.

# 16. Competing measures and honest disagreement

Two products may disagree without one being fraudulent.

Examples:

- household and establishment employment;
- survey estimate and administrative count;
- GDP and GDI;
- official measure and private nowcast;
- two polls with different universes/methods;
- two intelligence elements with different sources or judgments.

A player-facing explanation should preserve:

- what each product measures;
- its vintage;
- its source/method differences;
- uncertainty;
- whether disagreement is expected, material, or unresolved.

No hidden system should collapse competing estimates into one `TrueReportedValue` visible to the President.

# 20. Historical and generated-prehistory obligations

Generated prehistory may create statistical and informational history at lower resolution, but it must preserve enough evidence chronology to support load-bearing inherited facts.

For a 2033 opening statement such as:

> Manufacturing employment weakened through 2032, but the final pre-election estimate understated the deterioration and was revised later.

prehistory must preserve at least:

- underlying generated labor trajectory;
- the relevant measurement family;
- release/vintage available before election;
- later revision if politically remembered;
- actors' possible receipt/response based on what existed then.

Prehistory need not synthesize every monthly release if no later supported process consumes it.

The deletion/compression principle still applies.

# 22. Adversarial proof A — preliminary employment data and later benchmark revision

## Initial world

- Labor owns actual employment and gross job transitions.
- A CES-like official measurement uses employer sample records for a reference month.
- The preliminary estimate shows weak but positive payroll growth.
- The administration receives that release.
- Staff sees deteriorating unemployment-insurance claims and a private nowcast suggesting worse conditions.

## Valid information state

The President can legitimately receive:

- preliminary official payroll estimate;
- separate administrative claims data;
- private nowcast;
- staff assessment that signals conflict.

The President cannot receive exact canonical Labor state.

## Later revision

Additional administrative universe data produce a benchmark revision showing the earlier month was materially weaker.

The revised series changes the current statistical estimate.

It does not rewrite:

- the preliminary release;
- the date it was received;
- the President's original information set;
- the decision made under that information.

## Pass condition

The historical record can say both:

```text
At decision time, official payroll growth appeared positive.
Later benchmark data revised that estimate downward.
```

without contradiction.

# 23. Adversarial proof B — ACS-like local estimate with large uncertainty

A state has rising Housing stress concentrated in one smaller region.

An ACS-like sample estimate suggests severe renter cost burden but carries a large margin of error.

Valid consequences:

- local officials may treat it as warning evidence;
- the White House may request more data;
- journalists may later investigate;
- a state program may produce administrative records that improve knowledge;
- a staff memo may say evidence is suggestive but uncertain.

Invalid:

```text
estimate = 48%
→ canonical local renter burden becomes exactly 48%
```

The measured estimate remains evidence about the Housing/Population state.

- a law indexed to a specific legally defined release consumes the correct vintage under its rule;
- no later revision changes canonical historical actions already taken.

# 25. Adversarial proof D — intelligence assessment with probability and confidence

An external actor may disrupt a shipping route.

Available intelligence includes:

# 28. Anti-cheat and anti-ontology tests

A Step 7 implementation/design must reject:

1. **Truth alias test:** official statistic is direct alias to canonical condition.
2. **Future leak test:** forecast reads future world state/random draws.
3. **Vintage overwrite test:** later revision mutates earlier published artifact.
4. **Historical omniscience test:** historical UI uses latest revised data as if known earlier.
5. **Universe mismatch test:** adult poll becomes exact likely-voter state.
6. **MOE deletion test:** sample estimate with high uncertainty is displayed as exact.
7. **Methodless estimate test:** private estimate exists without target concept/method/source provenance.
8. **Administrative-universe test:** program administrative data silently represent nonparticipants.
9. **Confidence collapse test:** probability, sampling error, source confidence, and model uncertainty share one scalar with identical meaning.
10. **Confidentiality bypass test:** President gets raw confidential statistical records merely because agency is executive-branch.
11. **Political-edit test:** policy preference silently changes independent official estimate.
12. **Correction rewind test:** correction erases prior receipt/belief/action history.
13. **Measurement-cause test:** release directly changes material reality absent a separate legal/program coupling.
14. **Poll-cause test:** poll result writes population preference/approval.
15. **Intelligence-god test:** classified assessment reads hidden canonical foreign intention.
16. **Missing-zero test:** unavailable/unmeasured/unsupported becomes zero.
17. **Small-area fabrication test:** coarse/sample evidence is presented as exact local/person-level truth.
18. **Release-before-processing test:** artifact is available before its valid production/release boundary.
19. **Revision-without-lineage test:** new estimate replaces prior value with no reason/vintage relation.
20. **Drama-release test:** unscheduled report appears because presidency needs a development.

# 29. Step 7 detached audit gate

The detached audit should answer:

> **Can canonical conditions and occurrences become official statistics, administrative records, private estimates, forecasts, intelligence assessments, investigations, and polls through explicit bounded evidence-production processes with declared universe, method, time, support, access, uncertainty, vintage, and revision lineage—without artifacts becoming aliases for truth, actors gaining omniscient access, forecasts leaking future state, revisions rewriting prior knowledge, or political actors directly manufacturing the evidence they want?**

PASS requires at minimum:

1. truth, observation, estimate, artifact, release, receipt, and interpretation remain separate;
2. measurement universes/estimands cannot silently convert;
3. preliminary and revised vintages coexist historically;
4. actor knowledge respects release/receipt chronology;
5. uncertainty types remain semantically distinct;
6. administrative data retains its actual coverage and legal meaning;
7. forecasts use only information available at forecast origin;
8. intelligence separates sources, assumptions, likelihood, and confidence;
9. polls remain measurements rather than political truth;
10. statistical independence/confidentiality can exist inside executive government;
11. measurement-driven legal formulas use explicit coupling rather than truth aliasing;

A PASS establishes the Step 7 semantic evidence-production contract only.
