# 2. R13-01 is closed: cross-lens aliases no longer duplicate mechanisms

## 2.1 One mechanism identity controls across all policy views

The repair directly states:

> Every load-bearing mechanism or mutable fact family has one semantic identity, one owner, and one minimum depth floor.

Policy labels such as:

- Education;
- Cost of Living;
- Energy;
- Trade;
- Healthcare;
- Immigration;
- Disaster Recovery;
- Public Safety

are allowed to reference multiple mechanisms.

They do not create current-state owners.

## 2.2 Student-loan hostile case

Closed.

Federal credit/program/fiscal state owns:

- balances;
- obligations;
- collections/payments;
- cancellations/write-offs;
- fiscal subsidy/cost state.

Household Resources owns borrower cash-flow consequences.

Population owns person/household identity.

The Education lens merely references the relevant mechanism because it is politically/administratively connected to education.

Therefore:

```text
Education.StudentLoanBalance
PublicFinance.StudentLoanBalance
```

cannot become separate mutable truths.

## 2.3 Energy-price hostile case

Closed.

Energy owns canonical energy market/retail price and availability facts at the accepted resolution.

Prices owns broader price-category state/aggregation consuming those inputs.

Household Resources owns actual household expenditure/resource effects.

Production owns sector input-cost effects.

Different values in those owners represent different facts rather than copied source truth.

## 2.4 Immigration hostile case

Closed.

Immigration legal/admin process owns status/admission/asylum/enforcement case facts.

Population owns canonical immigration/emigration person-flow and residence transitions.

Labor, Housing, state/local finance, and other domains consume those facts.

A net-migration release can remain a measurement without becoming a second population owner.

## 2.5 Disaster hostile case

Closed.

One policy lens may group hazard, Housing damage, infrastructure damage, health effects, displacement, fiscal acts, and recovery programs while each fact remains with its proper owner.

No `DisasterRecoveryState` needs to shadow-own the country.

# 3. Tier semantics survive the repair

The repair does not turn the tier system into an implementation taxonomy.

The accepted candidate meanings remain coherent:

- **Core causal** — load-bearing endogenous state/path dependence and repeated couplings;
- **Structured condition** — canonical evolving state with bounded internal response;
- **Contextual** — bounded condition/context sufficient for supported constraints, evidence, and politics without repeated deep transformations;
- **Bounded external/shock** — externally initiated state with explicit eligibility and typed domestic handoffs.

A high tier does not require:

- more runtime entities;
- person-level simulation;
- daily updates;
- exact forecasting;
- more player controls.

A lower tier does not mean politically unimportant.

# 4. Exact institutional overlays are separated from material depth

The repaired contract rejects two opposite errors.

## 4.1 Exact upstream record does not make receiver exact

Example:

```text
exact federal bridge grant
→ exact obligation/payment
→ structured bridge/project state
→ structured capacity/network consequence
```

The exact grant cannot justify invented exact traffic, employment, output, or voter effects.

## 4.2 Coarse material receiver does not make upstream record approximate

A structured infrastructure model can still inherit an exact:

- statute;
- grant;
- contract;
- court order;
- program deadline;
- project identity.

The records retain their own support.

This preserves the Step 1–7 ownership/information contracts.

# 6. Receiver-depth closure passes

The candidate plus repair now requires every accepted coupling to land in a mechanism with sufficient state to resolve it.


Rejected.

```text

```

Rejected.

```text

→ Education wellbeing +4
```

Rejected.

```text

→ state popularity -3
```

Rejected.

Where the receiver is too shallow, the policy/shock content is narrowed or outside supported scope.

# 7. Supported-package activation rule passes

Generated history cannot use salience or narrative importance as permission to deepen state.

If a generated event suddenly makes a shallow mechanism relevant, the active package has only four honest routes:

1. prospective future-blind deepening already declared;
2. modeled/bounded response at the current floor;
3. narrowed claim/consequence;
4. out-of-scope classification.

The generator cannot invent the missing historical state after learning the event became important.

This preserves Step 12's no-hindsight-history rule and prevents mechanism depth from becoming adaptive storytelling.

# 8. Measurement/source granularity is independent of runtime depth

The repair closes a subtle ontology pressure.

The presence of:

- detailed BEA input-output tables;
- incident-level NIBRS data;
- hundreds of thousands of FHWA bridge records;
- detailed CMS expenditure accounts;
- detailed NCES education data

cannot force the game to simulate at identical resolution.

Conversely, a headline statistical release does not mean the hidden material domain may be equally shallow.

Examples:

- Labor may retain gross transitions beneath one unemployment-rate release;
- Production may compress hundreds of industries into a smaller causal graph;
- Crime may remain structured despite incident-level calibration sources;
- Infrastructure may use regional asset classes plus exact consequential bridges/projects;
- Healthcare may retain mixed depth despite detailed payer/service data.

The source/calibration transform carries what was aggregated or modeled.

Source detail never becomes current-state ownership.

- core stocks/flows remain stable and reconciled;
- structured mechanisms do not freeze unrealistically;

- slow feedback does not require retrospective deepening.

If a mechanism cannot survive eight years at its accepted floor, that is evidence against the floor or the 2033 horizon—not permission for endpoint patching.

# 12. Nonblocking watchpoints

These do not prevent Step 13 authority.

## 12.1 Core economic spine could still be expensive

`Core` is semantic, not implementation scope, but Production/Labor/Prices/Households/Energy/Trade feedback may still become technically costly or difficult to calibrate.

A later technical proof may demonstrate that a proposed compact representation is inadequate.

That would require a design/runtime revisit rather than silently demoting the mechanism.

## 12.2 Monetary and finance boundaries

The exact boundary among monetary policy, market rates, mortgage credit, household balance sheets, business credit, and federal debt service remains unresolved.

The accepted floor requires distinct facts and valid handoffs; it does not pick the implementation model.

## 12.3 Health and education breadth

Mixed depth can still become broad in aggregate.

Later Early Access and implementation work must avoid interpreting every structured mechanism here as required at launch.

## 12.4 External affairs

Bounded external state may eventually prove insufficient for a richer national-security presidency.

Step 13 establishes a first-product floor, not a permanent ceiling.

## 12.5 Measurement/calibration cost

Official source availability does not guarantee that reliable transformations to the game's compressed mechanism state are easy.

That burden remains future calibration work.
