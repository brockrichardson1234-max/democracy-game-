> **A horizon-neutral, owner-respecting continuation of the accepted Living Country from the synthetic 2024 election and January transition package through a candidate player start, using adaptive process resolution, exact occurrence retention where later gameplay requires identity or accountability, compressed bundles and aggregates where it does not, disclosed scenario conditioning, complete rejection accounting, and multi-seed falsification—without one historical narrator, mandatory event quotas, future-aware deepening, hidden rerolling, post-hoc biography repair, dramatic world selection, or direct assignment of a desired opening world.**

```text
historically closed inherited modern America

```

The January 20 handoff is a checkpoint inside one lineage, not the beginning of a separate story.

# 3. No handcrafted political prologue

The synthetic 2024 election and January transition package is part of the same generator proof.

It may not be one manually curated prologue shared across seeds while only the later years are generated.

Where later consumed, Stage A must generate through reusable owner-respecting rules:

- candidate eligibility and histories;
- presidential campaign/election state;
- campaign commitments, coalition, and mandate evidence;
- state popular-vote and elector processes;
- separate House and Senate elections;
- results, certification, and entitlement;
- January 3 congressional office assignments, organization, and leadership;
- January 6 electoral count;
- transition-team state, access, relationships, vacancies, and acting arrangements;
- consequential January 3–20 promises, contacts, disclosures, legal/fiscal acts, proceedings, and surviving outgoing-administration acts;
- January 20 incoming administration and current world.

Routine detail may be compressed.

Consequential facts require the accepted occurrence, evidence-at-time, relationship, and historical-provenance semantics.

A disclosed political outcome or range may be conditioned before generation.

It remains a scenario/calibration control and cannot be presented as an independently generated discovery.

A routine election, ordinary electoral count, and quiet transition are valid.

No transition crisis is required.

## 6.2 January 2029 player-start branch

Branches from the declared 2028 campaign-entry checkpoint and incorporates the player's bounded viable campaign history.

## 6.3 January 2033 player-start branch

## 6.4 Comparison rule

The 2033 horizon is compared with its own autonomous January 2029 checkpoint and subsequent continuation.

It is not compared to a different 2029 player-victory branch as though the histories shared the same intervention.

Campaign branches have separate lineage and cannot write back into the autonomous spine.

# 7. Lower resolution preserves full ownership

Lower resolution permits fewer represented intermediate details.

It does not permit a summary to own the result.

A process may use a mix of:

1. **exact canonical occurrences**;
2. **compressed owner-resolved process bundles**;
3. **aggregate stock/flow/distribution transitions**;
4. **bounded contextual/external initiating state**;
5. **explicit deferred/unsupported detail**.

There is no universal prehistory tick, year roll, or fidelity level for the whole country.

Different processes may operate on exact dates, daily/weekly activity, monthly or quarterly flows, annual boundaries, conditional transitions, or irregular events.

Every load-bearing result still preserves its owner, time, units, authority, population/geography, support, and causal inputs.

# 8. Exact occurrence versus bundle versus aggregate

Exact occurrence identity is required when supported later play needs a specific:

- office assignment, vacancy, appointment, resignation, removal, or succession;
- election/certification;
- consequential vote, law, veto, filing, decision, order, or stay;
- obligation, payment, transfer, reversal, contract, or award;
- promise, bargain, commitment, threat, betrayal, endorsement, or relationship-changing act;
- report, receipt, disclosure, leak, correction, or evidence-at-time record;
- organization action or major resource commitment;
- player-start program/project milestone;
- campaign commitment or mandate-forming act.

A compressed bundle may preserve interval, owners, counts/flows, selected occurrences, outputs, obligations, uncertainty, and omissions.

A bundle cannot later be unpacked into invented exact events.

An aggregate fact does not establish each constituent identity or sequence.

Unsupported detail remains unsupported rather than receiving a synthetic zero or convenient event.

# 9. Adaptive deepening

Resolution may deepen prospectively when current generated state and the predeclared consumer closure require finer identity or chronology.

Valid triggers include:

- a named actor/institution/firm/outlet/organization/project/case becomes causally consequential;
- an election, vote, appointment, case, or negotiation becomes close or contested;
- a right, obligation, deadline, order, contract, or accounting chain requires identity;
- cross-domain coupling needs an affected scope or contribution lineage;
- actor memory, evidence-at-time, responsibility, or accountability requires an occurrence;
- geography/population distribution changes later effects;
- a process will remain active at player start;
- a supported institution's procedure requires more detail.

Resolution may not deepen because:

- an event would make a good story;
- a later outcome makes an earlier event convenient;
- the player-start briefing needs vivid prose;
- the generator is looking for an interesting seed.

The consumer closure, resolution, deepening, and retention policies are frozen before a generation attempt.

They may know supported process families.

They may not know future outcomes or player choices.

If discarded detail later matters, the system must use retained latent support, model/bound it honestly, leave it unsupported, or regenerate under a new package.

It may not invent an exact past.

# 10. Quiet, mediocre, frustrating, and volatile histories

No year, term, transition, or player-start package is required to contain:

- a crisis;
- scandal;
- major reform;
- realignment;
- court confrontation;
- recession;
- war;
- natural disaster;
- dramatic betrayal.

A quiet world still contains functioning budgets, appointments, Congress, agencies, states, material change, evidence, media, organizations, and elections.

A mediocre administration may produce narrow laws, failed priorities, mixed implementation, and ambiguous material outcomes.

A frustrating world may contain divided government, blocked appointments, state resistance, weak evidence, missed opportunities, and diffuse accountability.

A volatile history is valid only when supported conditions, shocks, actors, and institutions create it.

The generator may not compensate for a quiet seed with a late crisis.

The player is not owed an emergency on inauguration day.

# 11. Validity, operability, and product interest remain separate

The accepted judgments are:

1. **semantic validity** — ownership, authority, causality, accounting, history, identity, information, and determinism hold;
2. **supported-scope validity** — the world remains within declared supported process families;
3. **player-start operability** — the Presidency, administration, time, information, and ordinary country processes function;
4. **scenario-family eligibility** — the world satisfies controls declared before generation;
5. **subjective product interest** — human judgment concerning political richness, repetition, comprehensibility, and fun.

A quiet world may be semantically valid and operable even when reviewers find it dull.

It must remain in proof evidence.

Operability does not require an immediate decision, crisis, bill, controversy, or existing White House workstream.

The first briefing may be informational, followed by time advancement.

A product may later reject an entire generator mode if its valid distribution is poor.

It may not silently reroll individual worlds until an exciting one appears and then claim unconditional generation quality.

- source or actor;
- eligibility;

- geography/population/entity scope;
- severity and uncertainty;
- dependence with other shocks;

A shock does not carry pre-resolved domestic politics.

```text

→ material/institutional effects
→ government and private response

```

Unsupported shock families should not be admitted casually and then narratively contained.

A post-hoc out-of-scope result must be logged under a predeclared rule.

No compensating shock may be added for drama.

# 18. Multi-seed credibility floor

The later formal/implemented proof must include at least:

## 18.1 Full-lineage machine screen

At least **300 distinct generation identities** beginning before the synthetic 2024 election and continuing through the four-year autonomous horizon.

All attempts remain in the denominator.

The figure is a minimum defect-screening floor, not proof of political statistical validity.

## 18.2 Eight-year continuation

Every four-year spine receives an extension disposition, with the eligible cohort continued and all extension failures reported.

## 18.3 Scenario-control coverage

Every supported conditioned/unconditioned family receives multiple attempts rather than one showcase seed.

## 18.4 Human deep audit

At least **24 reproducibly selected histories per horizon** receive contiguous review, including quiet, mediocre, frustrating, volatile, continuity, transfer, unified, and divided cases where supported.

The Stage A transition packages are included in that review.

## 18.5 Perturbation and metamorphic tests

The proof includes:

- same-seed input perturbation;
- time-chunk invariance;
- save/load continuation;
- iteration/handler-order invariance;
- horizon-prefix invariance;
- no future-aware deepening;
- resolution conservation;
- rejected-world isolation;
- no duplicate population, occurrence, contribution, evidence, or pressure;
- no retroactive actor knowledge.

## 18.6 Blind opening review

Reviewers inspect generated player-start packages without seed-quality labels and assess coherence, political plausibility inside the generated world, distinguishability, actionable inheritance, bounded information, repetitive/synthetic content, explanation quality, and whether history changes governing choices.

One polished history is never generator proof.
