# 1. Autonomous non-player initiative — PASS

## 1.1 Reusable initiative grammar exists

`08` requires every evidentiary non-player initiative to follow:

```text
actor/institution receives or observes bounded state
→ interprets through role, jurisdiction, objectives,
  constituency, beliefs, relationships, commitments,
  political risk, and current opportunities
→ derives institutionally available attempts
→ chooses attempt, delay, delegation, or no action
→ records the attempt canonically
→ target/procedure resolves independently
→ administration learns through a valid information route
```

This closes the largest residual narrative-insertion loophole from `07`.

The actor's initiative is not generated because the presidency needs pressure. It must have:

- an initiating owner;
- an observed/received basis;
- role and jurisdiction;
- objectives and constraints;
- available attempts, including no action;
- timing basis;
- a canonical attempt record;
- an independent admitting/resolving owner;
- a valid administration-discovery channel.

If those fields are absent, `08` explicitly removes the example from evidentiary status and leaves it as a design hypothesis.

## 1.2 No hidden political-story selector remains necessary

`08` forbids a global selector from deciding that a term needs congressional conflict and inserting the highest-scoring eligible initiative.

It also allows:

- multiple actors acting on the same date;
- actors delaying;
- actors taking no action;
- initiatives failing admission;
- coalitions failing to form;
- introduction without passage.

That is sufficient at product-contract level to avoid a RimWorld-style dramatic director or a Democracy-style global event selector becoming the source of political history.

## 1.3 Fully traced domestic example closes the prior gap

The autonomous fiscal-restraint package now identifies:

- official fiscal projections;
- senators with deficit commitments;
- jurisdiction and an available vehicle;
- available alternatives;
- canonical introduction and sponsorship records;
- independent committee, leadership, House, and governor choices;
- the channels through which Legislative Affairs learns;
- the distinction between introduction and passage.

The example is still a paper test, which the repair states correctly. It now demonstrates the grammar rather than substituting for it.

## 1.4 Existing untraced examples are not silently grandfathered as proof

The repair's rule is explicit: any older background phrase such as “a faction revolt occurs” or “senators create another package” remains non-evidentiary unless it can populate the initiative-provenance fields.

This resolves the concern that examples left in `04` or `06` could continue carrying proof weight merely because those files remain preserved.

# 2. Discretionary staff escalation — PASS

## 2.1 Adviser identity is no longer sufficient

`08` requires staff escalation to include:

- received artifact or communication;
- staff role and jurisdiction;
- delegated objective, standing instruction, presidential priority, deadline, or authority basis;
- a bounded assessment;
- confidence and uncertainty;
- forecasted consequence;
- an escalation or non-escalation record;
- intended recipient.

“Senior staff judged it important” is no longer a complete causal explanation.

## 2.2 Staff remains fallible

The contract permits staff to:

- over-escalate;
- under-escalate;
- disagree;
- revise an assessment;
- route a matter elsewhere;
- leave a matter in background summaries.

The President therefore receives an administration's interpretation of accessible information, not objective simulation importance.

## 2.3 Non-escalation is also causal

A matter remains background because of delegated tolerance, lack of presidential authority need, no near deadline, low confidence, subordinate resolution, or the player's reporting preferences—not because a hidden director decided it was insufficiently dramatic.

This closes the residual omniscience/drama loophole identified in `07`.

# 3. Attention, option, default, and consequence provenance — PASS

## 3.1 Every cited automatic STOP now has a bounded source

The final provenance ledger identifies for each automatic stop:

- initiating owner/state;
- observable artifact;
- administration recipient;
- escalation basis;
- presidential route and target;
- expiration/default;
- autonomous resolver;
- downstream record.

The ledger covers the ordinary, divided-government, and campaign/crisis traces.

## 3.2 Previously incomplete trace entries are repaired rather than hand-waved

The repair makes the following explicit corrections:

- The August Housing memo is reclassified as completion of a **PROACTIVE** review, not a second automatic stop.
- Existing Housing preparation continues if the player does nothing.
- Standing FEMA/state emergency operations continue without expanded presidential action.
- The batched crisis session names the actual cross-agency conflicts and standing default.
- Congress drafts a supplemental without a new administration position if the response window passes.
- The program-wide flexibility branch is available only after agency counsel identifies existing discretion and required procedure is followed.
- The election result is a **REVIEW**, not a presidential choice over the result.

These changes satisfy the absolute gate rather than asking the audit to infer likely defaults.

## 3.3 Deliberate inaction is preserved

Every decision surface in the ledger has an expiration/default or is explicitly classified as a non-decision review.

Defaults include:

- an offer lapsing;
- standing policy continuing;
- delegated staff or agencies proceeding;
- Congress acting without an administration position;
- a filing/appeal period expiring;
- standing emergency plans remaining operative;
- no new public attribution being issued.

No demonstrated decision requires the player to clear an inbox before time can continue.

## 3.4 Options are grounded rather than balanced by authorship

The ledger ties options to routes such as:

- political negotiation;
- legislative outreach;
- constitutional presentment;
- agency direction within validated authority;
- legal appeal/compliance;
- emergency declaration/coordination where law permits;
- fiscal proposal to Congress;
- communication or deliberate non-response;
- delegation to staff, agencies, the Vice President, or Cabinet actors.

Conditional options are unavailable when their authority or prerequisites are absent. The design does not guarantee a fixed number of attractive buttons.

## 3.5 Later consequences consume records rather than narrative flags

The counterfactual ledgers identify:

- immediate guaranteed record differences;
- merely eligible autonomous reactions;
- later observations needed before the President learns outcomes;
- shared reconnection institutions/dates.

A possible scandal is no longer a direct consequence of “looser rules.” It requires changed guidance/control records, an observed failure, and an independently initiated IG/oversight process.

This is the appropriate product-level standard.

# 4. Counterfactual term evidence — PASS at its stated status

## 4.1 Epistemic status is correct

Decisions 6, 14, and 19 remain paper contract tests. They are not promoted into evidence that an implemented simulation produces particular outcome distributions.

## 4.2 Same-state branches have causal divergence

The repaired forks distinguish:

- acceptance, rejection, counteroffer, House-route, and lapse for Senate sequencing;
- implementation rescue, Housing-first, and legally validated program-wide flexibility;
- personal governing, personal campaigning, and surrogate campaigning.

Each branch records a different administration action while leaving Congress, states, agencies, actors, and elections autonomous.

## 4.3 Reconnection does not erase history

Branches can later reach the same budget cycle, election, or congressional calendar while carrying different:

- commitments;
- proposals/laws;
- staff assignments;
- implementation records;
- relationship memories;
- observed consequences.

This is sufficient to demonstrate a coherent branching grammar without requiring a replacement authored story for every option.

# 5. Resource-specific contention — PASS

## 5.1 Universal resource is prohibited precisely

`08` corrects the overbroad wording from `06`.

It bans universal or cross-domain aggregate attention/capacity/overextension values from resolving unrelated outcomes.

It permits owner-scoped:

- calendars;
- queues;
- assignments;
- personnel/throughput;
- dollars;
- deadlines;
- legal limits.

## 5.2 The anti-Political-Capital distinction is enforceable

A valid explanation must identify the concrete collision.

The design fails when its complete explanation is merely:

> The administration did too much, so efficiency fell.

It passes when the explanation names the Senate window, staff assignment, agency queue, legal deadline, fiscal authority, incompatible meeting, or commitment conflict that produced the consequence.

No hidden universal resource is required by any repaired trace or fork.

# 6. Dynamic campaign feasibility — PASS

## 6.1 Feasibility is maintained after every choice

The final repair requires at least one plausible victorious history to remain after:

- nominee selection;
- running mate;
- every commitment;
- every coalition/message choice;
- every consequential campaign response used in setup.

Candidate admission is no longer the only feasibility check.

## 6.2 Miraculous backsolving is explicitly rejected

When a combination would eliminate the last plausible winning history, the standard setup:

- explains the contradiction;
- identifies the conflicting choices;
- requires the player to revise one;
- preserves the opponent, electorate, and prehistory.

It cannot rescue the setup through invented opponent collapse or impossible turnout.

## 6.3 Difficult choices remain possible

The design does not hide every costly option. A choice remains available when at least one plausible win survives, even if it produces a narrow margin, contradictory coalition, dependence on a faction, or difficult governing commitments.

## 6.4 Congress remains separate

House and Senate elections remain separately owned. Shared turnout/enthusiasm effects are allowed; selecting a convenient Congress is not.

The campaign contract now satisfies the binary gate while remaining transparently a victory-conditioned setup for the governing game.

**Attempted exploit:** make a bipartisan bill appear because the term needs pressure.

**Result:** rejected. The initiating actors need bounded information, jurisdiction, objectives, alternatives, timing, canonical attempt records, and independent admission.

## Test C — Hide a dramatic director inside staff

**Attempted exploit:** an adviser escalates whatever would create the best gameplay.

**Result:** rejected. Staff role, receipt, delegated basis, assessment, confidence, uncertainty, and record are mandatory.

## Test D — Reveal hidden truth through attention

**Attempted exploit:** stop time when a material condition is objectively severe but unmeasured.

**Result:** rejected. The chain requires an observable artifact, administration receipt, and bounded escalation.

## Test E — Freeze time until the player clicks

**Attempted exploit:** make every prompt mandatory indefinitely.

**Result:** rejected. Every decision surface has an expiration/default; non-decision reviews are classified separately.

## Test F — Author a balanced option menu

**Attempted exploit:** show three options because three choices feel game-like.

**Result:** rejected. Each option must derive from authority, route, target, timing, prerequisites, and current state.

## Test G — Guarantee victory after impossible campaign choices

**Attempted exploit:** backsolve a lucky election after the final viable path disappears.

**Result:** rejected. The choice cannot be finalized in the standard mode; prior selections must be revised.

## Test H — Call eligible reactions guaranteed consequences

**Attempted exploit:** assert that a governor attacks, a senator defects, or an investigation begins merely because a presidential choice was made.

**Result:** rejected. The counterfactual ledgers separate guaranteed records, eligible autonomous reactions, and required later observations.
