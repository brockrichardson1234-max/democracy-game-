# Binary verdict

## **PASS**

The repaired Step 3 composite satisfies the semantic population/geography gate.

The accepted candidate no longer requires:

- separately mutable populations for workers, patients, renters, audiences, beneficiaries, or voters;
- one global cross-product cohort table;
- equal-weight scopes being treated as the same people;
- GeographyState owning population/job/Housing/service weights;
- future-seed knowledge to decide when current carriers may merge;
- every story or project producing a permanent global split;
- one geographic hierarchy or one universal weighting basis;
- unsupported local or cross-domain correlations being presented as exact.

The PASS means only that the design contracts are coherent enough for later Living Country work. It does not prove that the substrate can be implemented efficiently or calibrated well.

No additional Step 3 repair is required before a separate authority action.

# 1. One canonical population — PASS

The composite retains one Population-owned identity, represented-weight, residence, household/co-residential, lineage, and recipient-political continuity system.

Domain states refer to that population through declared associations or scopes.

No accepted path permits Labor, Housing, Health, Education, Information, programs, or elections to create a second authoritative people store.

The one-population rule does not turn PopulationState into a god object. Employment, income, Housing, health, education, information delivery, and ballots remain with their semantic owners.

---

# 2. Scope identity and overlap — PASS

## 2.1 Closed scope taxonomy

The repair distinguishes:

- Population-owned exact scopes/partitions;
- domain-owned association allocations;
- modeled or bounded scope artifacts;
- ephemeral derived queries;
- occurrence-linked affected scopes.

Those categories have different ownership and reuse semantics rather than being one generic mutable `PopulationScope` object.

## 2.2 Equal weight does not imply equal people

A Labor allocation of 10,000 workers and a Healthcare allocation of 10,000 covered persons cannot be treated as the same population solely because their weights match.

Cross-domain identity requires:

- one shared Population-owned exact scope;
- one shared occurrence-linked affected scope;
- or an explicit modeled/bounded mapping.

## 2.3 Scope weights cannot drift independently

Persisted scope weights and membership semantics are immutable as-of records. Later population change creates a new current scope/version or owner transition; it does not rewrite the historical affected population.

## 2.4 Overlap is explicit

Exact partition children reconcile to their parent. Overlapping scopes cannot be summed as unique people without intersection, exclusion, or modeled/bounded overlap semantics.

This closes the most direct duplicate-population loophole.

---

# 3. Domain-owned sparse associations — PASS

A domain may partition the one population differently from another domain while owning only its domain-specific facts and allocations.

A domain allocation:

- references population identity;
- carries unit, time, geography, provenance, uncertainty, and reconciliation;
- does not become universal population membership;
- is not imported by another domain without a shared scope or mapping.

This permits Labor, Housing, and Health to retain materially different internal state without requiring a global joint table.

The design also preserves a route to shared identity when a real coupling must follow the same affected people.

---

# 4. Persistent correlation without Cartesian explosion — PASS

## 4.1 Named causal consumers are required

A correlation becomes shared persistent structure only when a named supported process, coupling, measurement, election, eligibility rule, or player-information query requires the same people over time.

“Could matter politically someday” is insufficient.

## 4.2 Domain-local distinctions remain local by default

If only Housing needs a project catchment or only Labor needs an occupation partition, that distinction can remain domain-owned.

UI filtering, one report, one transient forecast, or one story being available does not promote the distinction into global PopulationState.

## 4.3 Information and project portfolios do not force global splits

Publication and delivery may use sparse scope records. Direct recipient-state divergence is retained only where later political or institutional processes consume it.

Project catchments can remain material-domain associations unless cross-domain identity must persist.

Therefore hundreds of stories and overlapping projects do not inherently create a Cartesian population history.

---

# 5. Household/person continuity — PASS

The repair now requires separate accounting for:

- represented private households;
- represented persons by member role/class;
- group-quarters persons;
- total population.

Member multiplicity or another declared distribution reconciles household weight to person weight.

The design correctly distinguishes:

- a birth increasing person weight without automatically creating a household;
- a death reducing person weight without necessarily removing a household;
- household formation/dissolution changing relationships/counts without creating or destroying persons;
- movement changing residence without changing national population;
- group-quarters transitions changing household relation while preserving person continuity.

Household, family, tax, benefit, insurance, consumption, and voter units remain distinct.

No exact implementation structure is required by the contract.

---

# 6. Demographic, lineage, and association closure — PASS

Population transitions produce canonical occurrence identity and lineage/replacement information.

Domain owners independently resolve what birth, death, movement, household change, split, merge, or group-quarters transition means for their state.

The repair prohibits a settled current association from pointing to retired or nonexistent population identity without successor mapping.

It permits explicit pending reconciliation, which avoids imposing one atomic transaction across all domains while still preventing silent contradiction.

Historical job, Housing, health, benefit, information, or electoral relationships remain historical records rather than being rewritten after transition.

---

# 7. Non-omniscient coarsening — PASS

Coarsening is evaluated against a declared supported-consumer closure set containing current product/process contracts, active and scheduled obligations, current associations, recipient state, and declared query/election/measurement requirements.

It may not inspect:

- future random draws;
- future actor decisions;
- future player choices;
- future shocks;
- eventual election outcomes;
- which stories happen to be published later.

Equivalent current worlds with different hidden future randomness therefore make the same coarsening decision.

A later product feature cannot reconstruct exact historical identity that was not retained. It must use prospective refinement, modeled/bounded reconstruction, or an unsupported answer.

This preserves performance flexibility without turning representation management into an omniscient story system.

---

# 8. Residence and multi-geography — PASS

## 8.1 Residence is conserved

At a selected residence resolution, allocations across mutually exclusive supports reconcile to the source person/household weight.

A carrier modeled across three districts is not counted fully in all three.

Exact coarse residence and modeled fine residence can coexist when fine allocations reconcile and retain support/uncertainty status.

## 8.2 Other geographic relationships remain separate

Workplace, service, market, network, media, project, event, jurisdictional, and electoral relationships do not add residence weight or become synonyms for residence.

## 8.3 Geographic time remains real

Boundary and classification changes carry effective time and do not retroactively move people or rewrite elections.

Redistricting changes electoral geography; derived electorate membership changes without population residence mutation.

---

# 9. Crosswalk ownership — PASS

The repair cleanly separates:

1. Geography-owned spatial identities, geometry, containment, overlap, adjacency, and topology;
2. domain/institution-owned operational assignments such as networks, service areas, catchments, and workplace relations;
3. weight-specific crosswalk/model products derived from Population, Labor, Housing, service, or Information facts at an as-of time;
4. receiver-owned allocations admitted from those products.

A 2025 population-weighted crosswalk remains a 2025 artifact after population moves. Geography does not own or silently update the weights.

Exact area overlap is not presented as exact population, job, household, voter, or service overlap.

The crosswalk contract requires unit, denominator, vintage, weighting owner, method, uncertainty, residual handling, and reconciliation.

---

# 10. Query honesty and bounded knowledge — PASS

A load-bearing query must expose:

- source facts and owners;
- time and geographic vintage;
- mapping method;
- exact/modeled/bounded/unsupported status;
- uncertainty;
- whether it is current state, a measurement, forecast, or hypothetical.

Unsupported renter × manufacturing × insurance intersections do not become exact via marginal multiplication.

The simulation’s ability to derive an audit-truth answer does not automatically grant the White House that information. Player access still requires measurement, records, staff analysis, or another valid route.

The composite preserves the correct parts of I4:

- deterministic refinement identity and lineage;
- save/load consistency;
- no district-owned people;

# 12. External-research fit — PASS

Official Census materials support the limited design lessons used:

- ACS PUMS provides person and housing-unit microdata, household organization, and group-quarters persons with disclosure protection, while public-use geography is nation/state/PUMA rather than exact local assignment.
- LODES distinguishes residence, workplace, and origin-destination employment at fine geography and is tabulated/modeled administrative data subject to nonsampling error.
- Census relationship files distinguish geography types and changes across time.

The repaired design uses those facts to justify household-aware synthesis, typed geographic relations, and explicit modeled local allocation. It does not claim that any source supplies the final canonical population or mandates a particular synthesis algorithm.

# 13. Attempted falsification

## Test A — duplicate equal-weight scopes

**Exploit:** treat unrelated 10,000-person domain allocations as the same people.

**Result:** rejected by scope category, owner, lineage, and mapping requirements.

## Test B — sum overlapping audiences

**Exploit:** report two overlapping 100,000-person audiences as 200,000 unique reach.

**Result:** rejected unless intersection/overlap is represented or modeled.

## Test C — global story fragmentation

**Exploit:** append every story receipt to global cohort identity.

**Result:** not required; sparse delivery and purpose-bounded recipient state are permitted.

## Test D — global project fragmentation

**Exploit:** permanently split all population by every project-catchment overlap.

**Result:** rejected unless a named cross-owner consumer needs the same affected subset.

## Test E — Geography owns people through crosswalks

**Exploit:** GeographyState stores live population weights for district overlap.

**Result:** rejected; weight-specific product references Population at an as-of time.

## Test F — future-aware merge

**Exploit:** merge because a hidden future event will not use the distinction.

**Result:** rejected; future realized history is not a coarsening input.

## Test G — household/person accounting collapse

**Exploit:** one member death automatically deletes a household.

**Result:** rejected by separate person and household reconciliation.

## Test H — repeated residence membership

**Exploit:** count one state carrier fully in several districts.

**Result:** rejected by conserved residence allocation and separate overlap relations.

## Test I — dead carrier remains employed

**Exploit:** leave settled current Labor state attached to retired identity.

**Result:** rejected by lifecycle closure/pending-reconciliation contract.

## Test J — silent independence

**Exploit:** infer an exact joint count from unrelated marginals.

**Result:** rejected by joint-support status and query honesty.

## Test K — later feature fabricates lost history

**Exploit:** recover exact historical renter-worker identity after coarsening discarded it.

**Result:** rejected; answer must be modeled/bounded/unsupported.

No remaining exploit requires another Step 3 semantic repair.
