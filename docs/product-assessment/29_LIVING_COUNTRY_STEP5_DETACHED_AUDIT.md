# 1. Binary verdict

## **REVISE — 1 BLOCKING FINDING, 4 BOUNDED CLARIFICATIONS**

The candidate is substantially sound and answers most of the Step 5 question.

It correctly establishes:

- a functional closure set rather than one frozen White House organization chart;
- the controlling distinction that offices persist while officeholders interpret and act;
- persistent White House offices, Cabinet/agency leadership, and career institutions as different semantic structures;
- bounded receipt, access, consultation, dissent, delegation, queueing, and escalation;
- task-specific capability rather than one `StaffQuality` score;
- acting, vacancy, nomination, confirmation, and office assignment as separate states;
- routine delegated work below presidential attention;
- player-facing administration strategy rather than daily memo clicking.

However, the candidate closes the path **into** presidential attention more rigorously than the path **out of** a presidential decision.

After the President selects an option, issues an instruction, delegates, appoints, or requests work, the text often moves directly to “offices and agencies resolve their portions.” It does not yet require one closed dispatch chain proving:

- what exact presidential record exists;
- who receives it;
- when and how it is delivered;
- whether the recipient treats it as an order, delegation, request, objective, appointment, public statement, or political preference;
- whether the recipient has authority and capability to act;
- which recipient-owned assignment or queue state changes;
- what happens when the recipient refuses, delays, requests clarification, or cannot comply;
- how supersession, revocation, and partial distribution work.

That omission leaves a route for a hidden `Administration` orchestrator to fan one presidential choice directly into every office and agency queue.

The candidate therefore does not yet satisfy the gate completely.

# 2. What passes cleanly

## P5-01 — The administration is not one actor

The candidate explicitly defines the administration as a time-bounded governing configuration rather than one autonomous mind.

The President, Vice President, White House offices, officeholders, Cabinet officials, agencies, career units, procedures, councils, records, and queues remain distinct.

This prevents:

```text
Administration decides
→ every subordinate institution obeys
```

as a valid explanation.

## P5-02 — Office continuity and holder interpretation are separated

The candidate’s strongest rule is:

> **The office persists; the officeholder interprets and acts.**

Mandates, records, assignments, queues, delegations, deadlines, and institutional capabilities may persist through turnover. Private beliefs, relationships, judgment, and unrecorded knowledge do not automatically transfer.

This is compatible with Step 4’s distinction among person, office, assignment, institution, and relationship.

## P5-03 — Functional closure avoids a mandatory org chart

The candidate identifies functions that a playable administration must close while permitting different administrations to combine or split offices.

This is preferable to hardcoding the current real White House structure as universal ontology.

The design also requires a function to have a current owner or coordination rule, information route, authority/delegation semantics, queue or process where necessary, and succession behavior.

## P5-04 — The Chief of Staff is constrained

The operations function may route, schedule, bundle, assign leads, and enforce process. It does not own every substantive issue or objective importance.

Mandatory notices, deadlines, and external actions cannot disappear through an invisible filter. Improper suppression must be a canonical actor/office act with history.

That is sufficient to prevent the Chief of Staff from becoming a hidden storyteller at the intake side.

## P5-05 — Staff Secretary and decision packages remain projections

The candidate correctly makes the Staff Secretary/executive secretariat responsible for assembly, versioning, routing, clearance status, dissent attachment, deadlines, and presidential disposition receipt.

The packet does not own legal authority, fiscal state, agency capability, underlying truth, actor positions, or the downstream result.

Options require state and authority provenance rather than balanced-menu authorship.

## P5-06 — Policy councils coordinate rather than own the country

Domestic, economic, national-security, science, technology, cyber, climate, and other policy functions may coordinate analysis and interagency work.

They cannot absorb:

- material-domain truth;
- agency authority;
- congressional decisions;
- public finance;
- courts;
- foreign actors;
- media or public belief.

This preserves Steps 1–4.

## P5-07 — OMB and legal functions stay within their roles

OMB may own executive budget-development, review, management, legislative-reference, and regulatory-review processes without owning congressional appropriations, Treasury operations, agency execution, macroeconomic truth, or material outcomes.

White House Counsel, agency counsel, DOJ/OLC, law, and courts remain distinct. Advice does not become legal truth.

## P5-08 — Liaison offices do not own their counterparties

Legislative Affairs can own administration contacts, outreach assignments, whip assessments, and commitment records without owning votes or congressional scheduling.

Intergovernmental Affairs can own federal-side contact and coordination without owning governors or state decisions.

Public Engagement can own White House relationships without owning unions, firms, advocacy organizations, or their support.

Communications can own administration speech/publication attempts without owning media distribution or population belief.

## P5-09 — Official and campaign resources are separated

The candidate establishes a mandatory seam between official political/coalition assessment and campaign/party operations.

Government resources, agency information, public money, and official authority cannot become campaign assets merely because the President is a candidate.

Exact compliance mechanics remain properly deferred.

## P5-10 — Appointments and vacancies have real stages

Candidate identification, vetting, presidential selection, nomination, confirmation, commissioning/assignment, acting service, resignation, removal, vacancy, and replacement remain distinct where applicable.

The Senate owns confirmation. The agency persists during vacancy. Acting leadership is not a generic lower-quality version of permanent leadership.

## P5-11 — Cabinet and career institutions are not hive minds

Cabinet membership does not produce one adopted position by default.

A secretary’s advice, agency action, personal belief, department authority, and interagency role remain different facts.

Career operations, records, statutory duties, queues, and capability persist when political leaders change.

## P5-12 — Assignments and queues are owner-specific

The assignment contract is strong. It requires initiating owner, lead, supports, objective, authority, source records, target, time, deadline, dependencies, resources, status, threshold, uncertainty, and closure.

Queues belong to specific owners. Collisions cause concrete delay, reduced verification, missed deadlines, reassignment, or escalation rather than one generic overload penalty.

## P5-13 — Delegation remains bounded

Delegation identifies principal, delegate, authority, scope, permitted and reserved decisions, resources, access, thresholds, deadlines, revocation, succession, records, and default.

The delegate remains autonomous and cannot receive authority the principal does not possess.

Delegated success may remain below presidential attention.

## P5-14 — Information receipt and support status remain honest

No office or official reads the world root.

Public, office-internal, agency, presidential, privileged, classified, source-protected, private, and campaign information can remain differently accessible.

Access does not prove truth, reading, comprehension, or completeness.

The candidate also respects Step 3 and Step 4 support levels for population and domain information.

## P5-15 — Disagreement is task-grounded

Internal disagreement can arise from evidence, legal duty, institutional mission, fiscal assumptions, implementation knowledge, risk, timing, constituency information, professional norms, and policy objective.

It is not generated because every decision needs a red adviser and a blue adviser.

## P5-16 — Competence is task-specific

The candidate rejects one `StaffQuality`, `CabinetCompetence`, `AdministrationCapacity`, or `Loyalty` value.

Performance depends on relevant experience, evidence, procedures, assigned resources, queue state, access, cooperation, relationships, deadlines, complexity, and management choices.

The consequences are concrete: delay, incomplete consultation, higher uncertainty, missed conflict, flawed advice, poor follow-up, or deadline expiration.

## P5-17 — Escalation and quiet administration work are coherent

A matter reaches the President only through a valid receipt and office process.

Delegated owners can resolve work below the President. Significant problems may remain unknown. Staff can miss, delay, suppress, overstate, or revise through canonical conduct.

The world is not obligated to generate a presidential choice for every administrative process.

## P5-18 — Turnover and transition preserve institutions

Presidential, agency, office, privileged, classified, private, and campaign records do not collapse into one administration memory.

Agency duties, programs, queues, court orders, budgets, and operational obligations persist. Incoming officials receive only legitimate records and access.

Transition risks are specific rather than a universal debuff.

## P5-19 — Temporary bodies do not multiply authority

Task forces and crisis cells require mandate, chair, membership, scope, resources, records, reporting, and sunset.

They coordinate but do not absorb member agency authority or domain ownership.

## P5-20 — Player gameplay is strategic rather than clerical

The player makes decisions about people, portfolios, delegations, leads, reporting thresholds, conflicts, appointments, risk, personal intervention, and requests for further work.

The player does not manually route every memo, clear every document, manage every career employee, or acknowledge every successful routine action.

# 3. Blocking finding

## R5-01 — BLOCKING: presidential disposition lacks a closed dispatch-to-execution contract

### Finding

The candidate rigorously defines:

```text
world occurrence
→ administration receipt
→ office analysis/coordination
→ presidential decision package
→ presidential disposition
```

But the downstream side is usually summarized as:

```text
President acts
→ offices/agencies/other owners resolve their portions
```

That is directionally correct but semantically incomplete.

A presidential decision can have very different forms:

- a presidential order or memorandum;
- a delegation;
- a request for analysis;
- a direction to White House staff;
- a direction to an agency under claimed authority;
- a nomination or removal decision;
- an offer to Congress or a governor;
- a public statement;
- a political objective without operative instruction;
- approval of an agency recommendation;
- return for revision;
- deliberate inaction.

Those forms do not create the same recipient obligations or queue changes.

The candidate does not yet require a canonical process that distinguishes:

1. the President’s decision or attempted act;
2. the authoritative or advisory instrument carrying it;
3. recipients and scope;
4. delivery/receipt;
5. recipient interpretation of authority and applicability;
6. recipient-owned assignment, queue, instruction, or response;
7. acceptance, partial acceptance, delay, clarification, refusal, or inability;
8. supersession, revocation, expiration, or amendment;
9. downstream reporting and escalation.

### Exploit

```text
President selects “prioritize Housing implementation”
→ AdministrationPriority becomes HIGH
→ every White House office and HUD queue automatically reorder
→ no directive identity
→ no separate recipient receipts
→ no authority or delegation check
→ no resource-specific displacement
→ no agency interpretation or resistance
```

The design could claim the President merely “set a priority,” while a hidden administration orchestrator directly mutates unrelated owners.

Another exploit:

```text
President approves option in decision packet
→ all consultation offices are treated as informed
→ agency acts as though ordered
→ Legislative Affairs treats a policy preference as a bargaining commitment
→ Press treats it as publicly announced
```

One click has become multiple unowned actions.

### Why blocking

The Step 5 administration is supposed to be the causal bridge between presidential intent and executive-government action.

The intake half is closed. The dispatch half is not.

Without this contract, the model can still use a god `Administration` owner to fan one presidential choice into every subordinate state while technically preserving separate office and agency objects.

### Required repair

Add a **Presidential Disposition, Instrument, Dispatch, and Recipient Resolution Contract**.

At minimum it must require:

```text
presidential choice or default
→ canonical presidential decision/attempt occurrence
→ one or more explicit instruments or communications
→ declared addressees, scope, authority claim, effective time,
  requested/required action, deadlines, and classification
→ separate delivery/receipt records
→ each recipient independently determines applicability,
  authority, capability, queue/assignment consequence,
  need for clarification, and response
→ only recipient-owned state changes
→ completion, noncompliance, refusal, delay, partial action,
  supersession, or escalation records
```

It must distinguish:

- objective from instruction;
- instruction from delegation;
- delegation from transfer of office authority;
- request from command;
- approval from execution;
- private direction from public communication;
- nomination from office assignment;
- White House process decision from agency action;
- presidential statement from legal order;
- distribution from receipt;
- receipt from compliance.

One canonical presidential occurrence may have multiple delivery receipts, but the receipts may not duplicate the act.

A recipient may not be treated as informed without a valid delivery/access route.

The President may revoke, supersede, narrow, amend, or allow expiration only through another canonical act with effective-time and relationship to the prior instrument.

The repair should also require that an administration objective or workstream priority changes another owner’s queue only through a specific instruction, delegation, assignment, or standing rule admitted by that owner.

### Falsification test

Given one presidential decision addressed to HUD and OMB but not Legislative Affairs or Press:

- HUD and OMB may receive and respond according to their roles;
- Legislative Affairs and Press must not automatically know or act;
- the public must not receive a statement;
- Congress must not treat the decision as an offer;
- no unrelated agency queue may change;
- later distribution can inform additional offices through separate records.

If the same decision is later superseded, the old instruction remains historical while current recipient obligations change only after valid receipt and effective time.

# 4. Bounded clarifications

## C5-02 — Review, clearance, concurrence, certification, and veto must remain distinct

### Ambiguity

The candidate defines a `Clear` role as an office that must review due to legal, fiscal, security, records, or procedural requirements.

It does not explicitly distinguish:

- review requested;
- review completed;
- advice given;
- substantive concurrence;
- legal certification;
- budget clearance;
- procedural completeness;
- authority to block;
- final presidential decision.

A future implementation could turn every “clearance” office into a hidden veto or, conversely, treat mandatory legal/fiscal review as optional advice.

### Required clarification

A clearance process must declare:

- source of the review requirement;
- reviewer;
- scope;
- whether the result is advice, factual validation, procedural completeness, concurrence, certification, approval, or a legally operative condition;
- whether nonconcurrence blocks, delays, escalates, or merely accompanies the packet;
- who can waive or overrule it, if anyone;
- records and deadline/default.

No office gains veto power from the word `clearance` alone.

## C5-03 — Combining offices or assigning one person multiple roles cannot merge resources, access, or duties invisibly

### Ambiguity

The functional-closure approach properly allows administrations to combine functions or place one person in several roles.

The candidate should explicitly bind:

- one person holding multiple office assignments does not duplicate that person’s time, relationships, or population identity;
- each office mandate, queue, record regime, delegation, and access basis remains separately traceable;
- information learned in one role crosses into another role only when access/use is legitimate;
- conflicting duties and simultaneous demands remain real;
- removing one assignment does not silently end another;
- combined-office topology cannot erase mandatory independent review or statutory authority.

A flexible org chart cannot become one all-purpose super-office.

## C5-04 — Reorganization requires migration and reconciliation

### Ambiguity

The candidate allows offices to be combined or split and temporary bodies to sunset, but does not fully state what happens to live work during reorganization.

### Required clarification

Any organization-topology change must identify:

- offices/functions created, combined, split, renamed, transferred, or ended;
- effective time;
- legal/organizational authority;
- officeholders and assignments;
- queues and live assignments;
- delegations and standing instructions;
- resources and personnel;
- access and records;
- deadlines and obligations;
- coordination memberships;
- unresolved dissent;
- successor owners and reconciliation.

Reorganization may not drop, duplicate, or retroactively rewrite live work.

## C5-05 — Presidential decision-package readiness must not become a permanent time lock

### Ambiguity

The candidate generally requires deadlines and defaults, but a future packet could remain “awaiting clearance” forever and prevent the presidential process from advancing.

### Required clarification

Every presidential package or candidate package must have one of:

- a known deadline/default;
- a continuing delegated path while analysis proceeds;
- a return-for-revision disposition;
- withdrawal/cancellation;
- a rule that it remains available without blocking time;
- a valid mandatory hold owned by law/procedure with its own expiry or consequence.

Packet incompleteness alone cannot freeze the country or force a presidential click.
