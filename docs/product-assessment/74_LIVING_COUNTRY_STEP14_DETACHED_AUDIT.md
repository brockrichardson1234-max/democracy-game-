# 2. R14-01 — BLOCKING: desired final demand, realized transactions, prices, and consumption are not closed

## 2.1 Candidate path

The candidate proposes:

```text
Household Resources / Balance Sheet / Prices
→ MECH.HH.FINAL_DEMAND
→ Production
```

and separately requires Prices to own category prices.

This is better than using GDP or `EconomyHealth`.

It is still incomplete.

## 2.2 Hidden resolver exploit

Suppose households desire $100 billion of a category at current prices while domestic and imported supply can deliver only $85 billion-equivalent at those prices.

What owns:

- quantity actually purchased;
- money actually spent;
- inventories drawn down;
- unmet demand/rationing/backorders;
- import substitution;
- seller revenue;
- final-use quantity consumed;
- price adjustment timing;
- sales-tax or other transaction-linked receipts where modeled?

If `MECH.HH.FINAL_DEMAND` directly becomes Production's realized sales, the household owner has effectively assigned the seller's result.

If Production simply accepts the demand, shortages/capacity constraints can be bypassed.

If Prices simultaneously adjust to make supply equal demand, an undeclared equilibrium routine owns the outcome.

If household actual spending is later copied from the cleared result without a transaction identity, money/quantity can double count.

## 2.3 Why it is blocking

Step 14 explicitly promises to attack final-demand and price formation without a hidden equilibrium.

The current proof cannot satisfy that promise until it distinguishes:

1. buyer-side desired/planned demand;
2. seller-side available supply/capacity/inventory/import channels;
3. category/market price state;
4. realized exchange/final-use transaction or clearing result;
5. actual household expenditure/consumption consequence;
6. producer revenue/output/inventory consequence;
7. transaction-linked fiscal consequences where supported.

Without those stages, the assembled economic spine still has an unowned joint transformation at its center.

## 2.4 Required repair

The repair should not create one universal `MarketSystem` god object.

It should instead state:

> **Households own desired/planned category demand; sellers/production owners own supply, capacity and offered output; Prices owns canonical category price state; and a declared category/market exchange process or relationship resolves realized transactions/final use from admitted buyer demand, seller supply, price, inventories/imports/capacity, institutional constraints and timing. The realized exchange is one canonical economic occurrence/flow referenced by buyer and seller consequences rather than independently recreated by each side.**

The market/exchange process may be implemented inside or alongside the relevant commodity/service mechanism later.

Semantically it must have one owner.

It may be:

- quantity-constrained;
- price-adjusting;
- inventory-buffered;
- import-adjusting;
- contract/admin-price constrained;
- rationed;
- another bounded category-specific process.

There is no required universal equilibrium algorithm.

The repair must also distinguish:

```text
desired demand
≠ realized transaction
≠ actual consumption/use
≠ seller output
≠ seller revenue
```

Those may coincide only when the owning processes make them coincide.

## 2.5 Stress consequence

Proof A and Proof C must then be read as:

```text
resources/prices/credit
→ desired household category demand
→ category exchange/realization process
→ realized final-use transactions
→ Production/Labor/household consequences
```

Price adjustment and quantity adjustment may interact through explicit time/solver semantics rather than one invisible macro closure.

# 3. C14-02 — exact-overlay conservation needs a scope-transition receipt

The candidate correctly requires an exact plant/bridge/port overlay to affect its aggregate once.

Clarify the transition itself.

When an entity is promoted from an aggregate representation to exact identity, retain a reconciliation receipt establishing:

- source aggregate/mechanism;
- entity identity;
- effective time;
- represented quantity/weight/capacity/jobs and uncertainty;
- residual aggregate before/after;
- population/geography association;
- whether the exact overlay is a partition, modeled allocation or unresolved overlap;
- historical support available after promotion.

Demotion/merging later requires the inverse conservation semantics.

This prevents a future implementation from saying “reconciled” while silently changing totals during resolution changes.

This is bounded because Step 3/6/13 already require conservation and lineage.

# 4. C14-03 — controlled perturbations are audit interventions, not in-world powers

Proofs use operations such as:

- hold household final demand fixed;
- remove media amplification;
- hold investment constant;
- reverse a measurement revision;
- change only debt maturity structure.

Those are useful metamorphic tests.

The authority should explicitly distinguish:

> **A proof perturbation is a developer/audit intervention on controlled inputs or one process rule for falsification. It is not automatically a canonical actor action or a player capability.**

Each perturbation should declare:

- changed input/process;
- facts intentionally held constant;
- earliest allowed divergence time;
- which upstream history must remain identical;
- expected affected owners;
- which facts must not change.

This prevents a test fixture from quietly violating conservation or actor autonomy and then claiming the model failed.

# 5. C14-04 — monetary transmission must preserve product/term exposure instead of one derived rate vector

The candidate already rejects one universal rate modifier and cites term/risk/product-specific conditions.

The final authority should lock the implication:

- Fed policy act is one institutional occurrence;
- `MECH.MONETARY.CREDIT` may derive several market/product/term conditions;
- mortgage, consumer debt, business investment and Treasury debt each consume only their applicable conditions;
- existing fixed-rate contracts/loans do not reprice automatically unless their terms permit it;
- refinancing/maturity timing remains receiver-owned;
- a credit-spread shock may affect private borrowing without identically changing federal refinancing;
- a Treasury-rate shift may affect federal interest exposure differently from a household fixed mortgage.

This is necessary for the proof to show structured monetary depth is genuinely sufficient rather than hiding one rate vector behind a new name.
