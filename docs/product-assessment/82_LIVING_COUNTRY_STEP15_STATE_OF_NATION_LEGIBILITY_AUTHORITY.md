# 2. Accepted central answer

The Presidency should understand the country using the hierarchy:

```text
WHAT CHANGED?
→ WHAT DO WE ACTUALLY KNOW?
→ WHY MIGHT IT MATTER?
→ WHO IS ACTING?
→ DOES ANYTHING REQUIRE THE PRESIDENT?
→ SHOW THE EVIDENCE / RECEIPT / HISTORY
```

The accepted principle is:

> **Overview → Detail → Record increases explanation, not omniscience.**

The interface is a family of bounded projections over the Living Country and administration information state.

It does not own the country and cannot create political importance merely through presentation.

---

# 3. Five layers may not collapse

Every load-bearing view preserves the distinction among:

1. **subject/referent** — the material, legal, fiscal, institutional, political, actor, or historical matter being discussed;
2. **available evidence / operational record** — what has actually been measured, recorded, retrieved, received, or directly known;
3. **staff/institutional interpretation** — what a specific office/team concludes or forecasts;
4. **political activity** — what actors, organizations, media, institutions, or populations are doing or treating as salient;
5. **administration/presidential state** — what the White House has adopted and whether the President actually needs to act.

Thus:

```text
material severity
≠ evidence strength
≠ staff risk assessment
≠ media/public salience
≠ White House priority
≠ presidential decision requirement
```

No interface summary may quietly turn those dimensions into one score.

# 4. Six semantic information surfaces

These are semantic contracts, not six mandatory tabs.

## 4.1 Presidential Attention

Contains only matters legitimately escalated for presidential review/action through the accepted administration route.

Every item identifies:

- receiving/escalating office;
- artifact/duty basis;
- what is known;
- uncertainty;
- why the President is needed;
- authority/routes;
- deadline/expiration;
- default/inaction;
- independent downstream resolvers.

Attention may be empty.

No event quota or severity threshold fills it.

## 4.2 Administration Workstreams

Shows objectives/matters the administration has deliberately adopted, delegated, monitored formally, pushed, or held.

Workstream state remains separate from:

- material severity;
- national importance;
- media attention;
- congressional agenda;
- public salience.

## 4.3 Country Watch / State of the Nation

**Country Watch** is the continuing bounded monitoring/discovery layer built from legitimate administration information processes.

**State of the Nation** is a dated synthesis/orientation projection over available Country Watch material, administration information, and clearly declared political lenses.

They do not show the entire canonical country.

They do not show only White House priorities.

Observable received conditions may remain visible while politically quiet and unadopted.

## 4.4 Actor/Institution Dossiers

Shows bounded identity, office, public action, known commitments, relationships, communications, constituency/context, and staff assessment.

It does not expose hidden motives, private beliefs, future choices, or unsupported exact constituency truth.

## 4.5 Evidence / Measurement

Shows where a number, estimate, claim, forecast, poll, or assessment came from, with appropriate:

- producer;
- reference period;
- release/receipt time;
- vintage/status;
- methodology/source family;
- geography/population;
- uncertainty/support;
- dependency;
- revision;
- access limitation.

## 4.6 Historical Record

Shows what happened and preserves separately:

- what was known then;
- what was decided then;
- what later happened;
- what is known now.

A later correction/revision cannot rewrite an earlier actor's information set.

The live Record remains player-access-bounded rather than exposing developer provenance automatically.

# 5. Information-access ladder

The first Step-15 audit established a controlling distinction:

> **The executive branch is not one epistemic actor.**

The accepted ladder is:

```text
source/institution possession
≠ White House index/notice
≠ retrievable-on-request
≠ specific administration office receipt
≠ presidential receipt
≠ restricted/inaccessible/unknown
```

## 5.1 Source possession

An agency/institution may possess a record that the White House has never received.

Its exact content is not thereby player-visible.

## 5.2 Index/notice

A valid administration process may know that a report/product exists without knowing its substance.

The interface may show metadata such as:

> Report available — not yet retrieved/reviewed.

## 5.3 Retrievable

If a designated office has a legitimate retrieval route, the player may request it.

Retrieval creates a new information/receipt occurrence.

It does not backdate knowledge.

## 5.4 Office receipt

One White House office receiving content does not automatically mean every office or the President possesses it.

## 5.5 Presidential receipt

A presidential briefing may convey a bounded summary without conveying every underlying source detail.

Opening additional source content requires that it was already received/attached or that a valid retrieval occurs.

# 6. Country Watch cannot become a federal debug database

Country Watch may display substantive content only when:

- a designated monitoring/retrieval process received it; or
- a direct legitimate player retrieval creates receipt at the current time.

It may display metadata/availability when an authorized index knows a source exists.

It may not perform:

```text
search all hidden agency state
→ return canonical truth
```

Search is limited to received/indexed/retrievable information spaces.

A question about unknown motives, corruption, actual hidden economic state, or future behavior must be answered through evidence/analysis/investigation—or remain unknown.

---

# 7. Monitoring coverage and silence

Country Watch must distinguish, where known:

- current monitoring;
- stale data;
- delayed product;
- partial coverage;
- failed feed;
- known but unretrieved product;
- no standing monitoring;
- access restriction;
- source not producing information;
- unknown state.

Thus:

```text
no warning
≠ no problem
```

and:

```text
no recent data
≠ current data showing stability
```

The design does not require the White House to monitor all American mechanisms continuously.

Institutional blind spots are allowed.

---

# 8. Proactive investigation

The President/player may investigate rather than wait for escalation.

A legitimate request creates an administration assignment with:

- question/scope;
- authority/requesting office;
- lead;
- source/access basis;
- resources/queue;
- deadline/priority;
- expected product;
- uncertainty/confidentiality constraints.

Possible results include:

- stronger evidence;
- narrower estimate;
- contradiction;
- insufficient evidence;
- access denied/restricted;
- delay;
- referral;
- no meaningful finding.

Investigation does not reveal debug truth.

---

# 9. Summary-selection contract

An overview may select a subset for usability using declared lenses such as:

- presidential decision/deadline;
- recent received condition/institutional change;
- scheduled milestone;
- active administration workstream;
- significant newly received evidence;
- player Watch instruction;
- declared public/media/congressional/state activity projection.

Selection/sorting is presentation.

It is not a canonical IssueImportance or NationalAgenda.

A condition omitted from one overview remains reachable through its legitimate discovery path when such a path exists.

---

# 10. Maps and geography

A geographic display preserves:

- geography type;
- time/vintage;
- population/entity universe;
- denominator;
- exact/modelled/bounded/unsupported support.

State-level evidence cannot silently become exact county or district values.

Modeled allocation must remain visibly modeled.

Missing/unsupported geography cannot be painted as zero.

Residence, workplace, district, market, service, media, jurisdictional, network, and event geographies do not substitute for one another.

---

# 11. Evidence and trend legibility

The interface must preserve meaningful states such as:

- preliminary;
- revised;
- corrected;
- withdrawn;
- stale;
- modeled;
- partial coverage;
- methodology break;
- disputed;
- no reliable estimate.

Time-series presentation cannot erase material methodology/source/geography breaks.

A current revised series may coexist with earlier historical vintages used by actual decisions.

---

# 12. Staff assessment remains plural

Staff interpretation must identify its producer or synthesis process where load-bearing.

An integrated White House assessment exists only when a real synthesis product/process creates it.

OMB, Counsel, Legislative Affairs, economic advisers, agencies, and other offices may disagree.

The State-of-the-Nation surface may summarize disagreement.

It may not silently pick one office and label it `White House truth`.

---

# 13. One occurrence, many receipts, many views

One underlying occurrence remains singular.

Several genuine information routes may exist around it.

Example:

```text
Plant P closure                    ← one material occurrence
company filing                     ← evidence
Labor report                       ← evidence
Governor request                   ← political communication
news story                         ← media artifact
Senator call                       ← political communication
```

Those information occurrences remain separate because different offices may learn at different times.

Country Watch, dossier, workstream, briefing, map, and Record may all reference the same closure without cloning it.

Cross-view deduplication may not erase receipt history.

---

# 14. UI/navigation actions versus governing actions

The following remain distinct:

- **Pin/Watch** — monitoring/presentation preference or legitimate monitoring instruction;
- **Request analysis/retrieval** — administration information assignment/action;
- **Create/elevate workstream** — administration objective/priority action;
- **Open dossier/evidence/history** — navigation within received content or legitimate retrieval when needed;
- **Contact/negotiate/direct/communicate** — canonical actor/presidential action;
- **Hide/collapse** — presentation preference only.

Hiding an item cannot suppress a later valid Presidential Attention escalation, legal duty, or deadline.

UI filtering is not presidential inaction.

---

# 15. Briefing versus State of the Nation

A **Briefing** is a dated administration communication product emphasizing:

- since-last-attention changes;
- newly received evidence;
- workstream progress;
- delegated results;
- deadlines;
- actor/institutional developments;
- matters now requiring review.

A **State-of-the-Nation** view/report is broader orientation and exploration.

It can show the country moving while Attention is empty.

Briefing is not the whole country.

State of the Nation is not an inbox.

---

# 16. Opening presidency

The opening briefing should derive from generated/current history and accessible evidence under five broad questions:

1. How did you get here?
2. What government did you inherit?
3. What country did you inherit, according to available evidence?
4. What is already moving?
5. What actually requires early presidential choice?

The opening may have no immediate crisis.

It may contain uncertainty and information gaps.

It cannot use authored prose to create history or omniscient explanation.

# 17. Accepted adversarial legibility proofs

At contract level the composite supports:

1. a preliminary recession estimate later revised without rewriting material/history;
2. a severe but weakly observed condition remaining outside Attention while still potentially investigable;
3. a politically huge education controversy over materially coarse Education state;
4. Housing appearing differently under material, public, media, congressional, governor, White House, and presidential lenses;
5. a pivotal senator dossier with known acts plus uncertain staff assessment but no hidden motive;
6. state-supported labor evidence plus modeled local allocation without fake local precision;
7. one plant closure referenced through many views while preserving several legitimate receipts;
8. twenty quiet days with empty Attention and continued country movement;
9. an agency-only record remaining invisible in substance until White House retrieval/receipt;
10. a public report retrieved today remaining historically unknown to the administration before today's retrieval.

These are information-contract proofs, not UI usability proof.

# 18. Accepted anti-cheat requirements

Step 15 rejects:

- raw developer/canonical truth as player dashboard;
- treating all executive agencies as one mind;
- agency possession becoming White House knowledge automatically;
- drill-down widening access without receipt/retrieval;
- public availability becoming historical notice automatically;
- one Top Issues / National Importance ranking;
- one generic confidence score replacing evidence semantics;
- one staff voice when offices actually disagree;
- Workstream status becoming national severity;
- Country Watch containing only administration priorities;
- State of the Nation becoming an inbox;
- Briefing becoming the whole country;
- hidden-truth semantic search;
- instant debug-truth investigation;
- unsupported local heatmaps;
- missing data shown as zero;
- revised evidence rewriting evidence-at-time;
- hidden actor motives in dossiers;
- screen prominence causing political/material depth;
- cross-view duplicate occurrences/evidence/people/pressure;
- over-deduplication that erases legitimate separate receipts;
- hiding a card suppressing mandatory escalation;
- UI filters becoming governing decisions;
- forced presidential work because Attention would otherwise be empty.

# 20. Step 15 verdict

## **ACCEPTED**

The Step 15 question is answered at design-contract level:

> **The President can understand a deep Living Country through bounded semantic surfaces that distinguish the world being discussed, evidence actually available, staff interpretation, political activity, administration priority, and presidential requirement. Country Watch preserves observable neglected state without becoming omniscient; Workstreams preserve administration intent without becoming national importance; drill-down increases explanation without expanding knowledge rights; and the Historical Record preserves what was known when.**

This establishes the **State-of-the-Nation legibility constitution**.

It does not prove the final user experience.
