# 1. Proof question

The proof must answer:

> **Can a player direct a bounded United States presidential administration through several concurrent, causally honest situations—using imperfect information, identifiable advisers, proactive investigation, delegation, autonomous institutions, dated time, delayed consequences, and a legible historical record—and want to advance the calendar again?**

This is broader than a static information prototype and narrower than the complete Living Country.

The proof is not allowed to pass merely because:

- cards render;
- a scripted sequence completes;
- one Housing route is easier to read;
- many systems possess state;
- all automated tests are green.

It must demonstrate an actual presidential operating loop.

---

# 2. Product doctrine retained

The proof remains governed by:

> **The country keeps moving. You decide what deserves the President.**

The recurring player loop is:

```text
receive a bounded briefing
→ orient to what changed
→ inspect evidence, actors, places, workstreams, and history as needed
→ intervene, delegate, investigate, communicate, wait, or deliberately decline
→ issue visible typed presidential acts
→ advance dated time
→ autonomous owners continue
→ later information and consequences return
→ reprioritize
```

The proof must preserve:

- bounded executive control;
- autonomous non-player initiative;
- office-specific information and work;
- daily dated continuity with event-driven review;
- explicit uncertainty and evidence vintage;
- concrete owner-specific opportunity cost;
- deliberate inaction/default;
- typed causal handoffs;
- one canonical world;
- persistent history;
- no universal Political Capital, Issue Importance, Media Attention, Staff Quality, Crisis, or Presidential Capacity meter.

The proof composition is built beside I10 for safety, but it is not designed as a permanent sidecar.

## 3.2 Proof composition status

The proof may expose a separate bounded factory, conceptually:

```text

```

The exact symbol name may differ.

Its production-intended operating interfaces must not contain proof-thread IDs, fixed actor names, or route-stage assumptions.

Proof-specific content belongs in configuration/content packages or bounded owner adapters—not in the general coordinator.

## 3.3 One-world invariant

Within the proof there is exactly one canonical state for every fact.

Forbidden:

```text
real Employment state
+ briefing-only Employment state
```

```text
real agency receipt
+ UI fake knowledge flag
```

```text
real congressional initiative
+ card-owned initiative status
```

```text
real historical occurrence
+ separate UI decision receipt pretending to be history
```

Player projections may cache navigation state, but they may not own substantive country, actor, office, knowledge, workstream, or consequence state.

## 3.4 Passing graduation

If the proof passes:

- its presidential operating composition becomes the candidate basis for the future production session;
- its permanent interfaces remain and are deepened rather than replaced;
- I10 remains available as regression evidence;
- a later controlled migration may place the operating composition behind the normal production factory;
- the next product proof deepens one material chain without removing the broad operating environment.

Passing does not automatically merge the branch into `main` or define Early Access.

## 3.5 Failing discard

If the proof fails:

- its coordinator, projections, UI, and configured proof package may be discarded;
- independently valuable owner extensions may survive only if their tests and semantics stand on their own;
- the existing accepted engines and I10 regression route remain intact;
- failure may not be hidden by declaring the proof to be the new product anyway.

# 6. Permanent operating backbone

The following capabilities are intended to graduate if the proof passes.

Exact TypeScript names and file layout remain implementation choices, but their semantic responsibilities are fixed.

## 6.1 Operating composition

A product-level coordinator may:

- reference and advance canonical owner states;
- order same-instant owner work deterministically;
- route artifacts, communications, duties, and instruments;
- permit office assignments and staff products;
- derive bounded player projections;
- serialize the operating world.

It may not decide:

- congressional votes or scheduling owned by Congress;
- governor/state choices;
- agency compliance or material success;
- journalistic publication;
- audience belief;
- court outcomes;
- election outcomes;
- material-domain outcomes.

## 6.2 Persistent administration offices

A retained office state minimally supports:

- office identity and jurisdiction;
- current officeholder/acting holder;
- role-based access;
- office-owned records;
- incoming and outgoing communications;
- assignments and queue order;
- delegated authority;
- deadlines/defaults;
- current analysis products;
- turnover-safe institutional records.

The office persists; the officeholder interprets and acts.

## 6.3 Assignment and queue state

An administration assignment records:

- requesting person/office;
- lead office;
- question/objective;
- source references;
- required consultation;
- authority;
- queue position or scheduled work period;
- deadline;
- expected product;
- reserved decisions;
- completion, delay, refusal, cancellation, or supersession;
- resulting product/occurrence references.

Assignments are owner-specific. There is no administration-wide `capacity` number.

## 6.4 Knowledge and receipt ledger

The proof supports, without collapsing:

```text
artifact exists
→ institution possesses it
→ White House index/notice exists or does not
→ office may retrieve it
→ office retrieves/receives it
→ staff analyzes it
→ President receives a bounded presentation
```

Required records include:

- institution possession;
- metadata notice/index;
- retrieval attempt and result;
- office receipt;
- staff assessment/synthesis;
- presidential presentation scope;
- evidence vintage and revision lineage;
- current access/classification;
- historical receipt/presentation time.

UI `read/unread` state cannot substitute for presentation history.

## 6.5 Staff assessment artifacts

A staff assessment records:

- producing office/team;
- source evidence and dependency lineage;
- as-of time;
- propositions/conclusions;
- confidence/support;
- unresolved uncertainty;
- dissent or alternative assessment where present;
- requested decision or monitoring recommendation;
- revision/supersession status.

A wrong, biased, or weakly supported assessment is permitted.

Claimed confidence remains distinct from methodological support.

## 6.6 Presidential escalation and Attention

The canonical load-bearing object is an escalation/review record, not a UI card.

A valid escalation identifies:

- initiating receipt, duty, deadline, or unresolved office conflict;
- escalating office/person;
- why presidential authority or judgment is requested;
- known facts;
- uncertainty;
- options and provenance;
- expiration/deadline;
- default/delegated consequence;
- downstream resolvers.

`Presidential Attention` is a projection over valid active escalations and reserved reviews.

It may be empty.

No action-ID prefix or global severity threshold may create it.

## 6.7 Administration workstreams

A workstream persists the administration’s adopted objective and coordination state while referencing, rather than owning:

- country conditions;
- evidence;
- responsible offices;
- actors/institutions;
- presidential acts;
- commitments;
- legal/fiscal state;
- implementation;
- deadlines;
- historical occurrences.

A workstream may be:

- active;
- delegated;
- monitored;
- paused;
- deprioritized;
- completed;
- abandoned;
- blocked;
- inherited.

It does not mean the subject is objectively important.

## 6.8 Typed presidential decision and instrument state

A presidential choice records the selected intent and every knowingly authorized instrument.

Initial proof instrument families may include:

- request analysis;
- create/reprioritize/close administration assignment;
- delegate bounded authority;
- reserve a decision;
- authorize a legislative position or negotiated offer;
- request/issue an agency instruction where lawful;
- request governor/organization contact;
- schedule a presidential meeting or call;
- authorize a public statement/interview/briefing;
- approve deliberate inaction or allow default.

A choice may authorize a bundle only when each included act is visible before confirmation.

Staff may not add an undisclosed promise, agency order, fiscal commitment, or public statement.

## 6.9 Dispatch and recipient receipt

For every outgoing act:

```text
presidential decision
→ typed instrument
→ dispatch attempt
→ actual delivery/receipt or failure
→ recipient interpretation
→ recipient-owned action, delay, refusal, modification, or no action
```

The President’s choice never directly writes the recipient’s result.

## 6.10 Cross-owner Historical Record projection

The proof retains one canonical occurrence identity per underlying occurrence and provides a player-safe index showing:

- what happened;
- what office/institution possessed or received;
- what the President was shown;
- what decision/instrument was authorized;
- what other owners did;
- what later evidence revised;
- what consequences followed;
- what remains uncertain.

The index references owner records. It does not duplicate them as a second historical truth.

## 6.11 Versioned operating save

The proof uses one versioned save envelope for its operating world.

Rules:

- canonical owner states serialize once;
- derived projections are regenerated;
- navigation state may serialize separately and cannot become truth;
- same-history load is idempotent;
- loading cannot rerun searches, retrievals, deliveries, presentations, autonomous choices, or random draws;
- save restoration verifies configuration/artifact identity;
- existing I10 format 11 remains a frozen regression format, not a synchronized second world;
- the proof format is intended to become a future production successor if the proof passes.

Rollback UI is not required in POP-0, but state identity may not make branch-bounded knowledge impossible later.

# 8. Concurrent proof threads

All four threads coexist in one dated world.

They are proof fixtures. Their count and subject matter do not define final product or Early Access breadth.

## 8.1 Thread A — regional employment deterioration

### Canonical material scope

A bounded Labor owner represents:

- selected state/industry employment stocks;
- hires and separations as gross flows;
- labor-force/employment classifications necessary for the proof;
- one named plant overlay reconciled with its aggregate state/industry cell;
- a rest-of-nation aggregate sufficient for national context.

Primary affected states:

- Ohio;
- Michigan;
- Pennsylvania as a supplier/logistics spillover region.

### Initiating state

A fictional named manufacturing plant announces closure and begins a dated separation schedule.

A second regional contraction may arise through an aggregate supplier mechanism.

The exact named-plant contribution is counted once in aggregate Labor totals.

### Evidence path

The proof includes:

- company/public notice;
- state unemployment-claims or workforce reports;
- a preliminary Labor Department estimate;
- a later revised estimate;
- office-specific assessments with differing confidence;
- local versus national media selection.

### Allowed depth

Labor state is canonical and real within the bounded cells.

Household-income, healthcare-coverage, and federal-receipt consequences may initially be represented only as explicitly modeled/bounded analytical artifacts when the receiving material owners are not yet implemented.

Those estimates may affect staff recommendations and political actors’ beliefs.

They may not mutate canonical household resources, coverage, or fiscal receipts as though those downstream systems already existed.

### Presidential relevance

Potential routes include:

- request deeper analysis;
- convene governors;
- assign an administrative response review;
- authorize a legislative position;
- make or decline public remarks;
- wait for stronger evidence;
- deliberately treat the matter as non-presidential for now.

No route directly improves employment.

## 8.2 Thread B — inherited Housing implementation

### Reused owners

Use the accepted public-finance, program-administration, recipient, intergovernmental, BABA/waiver, Housing-material, and information primitives where applicable.

The proof starts after legal authority and some implementation records already exist.

Housing is an inherited workstream, not the player’s only initiative.

### Background progression

- agency and recipient work continues while the player focuses elsewhere;
- projects progress or delay on dated material and administrative state;
- payments do not directly create Housing outcomes;
- states/recipients retain independent action;
- material consequences remain Housing-owned.

### Hidden-but-discoverable problem

At proof start, one meaningful implementation problem exists in HUD or recipient records but has not been presented to the President.

Possible configured form:

- a domestic-preference waiver/supplemental-record issue;
- recipient readiness/capacity delay;
- implementation evidence inconsistent with the high-level status.

The White House may know only that a monitoring product exists.

The player may:

- retrieve/request review;
- leave it to standing monitoring;
- learn later through a governor, congressional contact, or journalism;
- finish the proof window without personally learning the full fact.

### Presidential relevance

When legitimately surfaced, routes may include:

- request HUD options;
- reprioritize an OMB/DPC review;
- contact a governor/recipient;
- approve one visible bounded administrative direction;
- communicate publicly;
- defer and allow standing procedure/default.

The proof must not recreate the entire original Housing enactment pipeline.

## 8.3 Thread C — autonomous congressional initiative

### Initiative ownership

A group of affected-state lawmakers independently observes/receives employment evidence and pursues a bounded `Regional Employment Stabilization` initiative.

The initiative originates from lawmaker, leadership, and committee state—not a White House workstream and not a dramatic event director.

### Legislative scope

The proof requires:

- one Congress-owned proposal;
- identifiable sponsor(s);
- one relevant committee/procedural opportunity;
- one real deadline or scheduling window;
- actor/organization positions with uncertainty;
- autonomous progression whether or not the President acts.

The proof does not require several simultaneous formal bills.

That broader product requirement remains unresolved rather than rejected.

### Presidential routes

The President may:

- support the proposal as introduced;
- authorize negotiation over specific terms;
- oppose it;
- request analysis before committing;
- make a public statement without a formal commitment;
- remain silent and let Congress proceed;
- prioritize another matter while delegating congressional contact.

Each route becomes typed acts and does not set votes or scheduling.

## 8.4 Thread D — serious politically quiet condition

### Canonical material scope

A bounded healthcare-service-access mechanism represents:

- a small number of rural maternity-service areas;
- facility/service capacity;
- dated closure or service-withdrawal occurrences;
- affected population catchments;
- increased access/travel burden at supported geographic resolution.

This is not a complete healthcare simulation.

### Information state

- state/local records exist;
- federal evidence is partial, stale, or not yet synthesized;
- affected populations may experience the condition directly;
- organization is weak;
- national media does not automatically adopt the story;
- no valid presidential escalation exists at proof start.

### Discovery

A legitimate discovery route may exist through:

- Country Watch metadata that an HHS/state product exists but is unretrieved;
- player-requested service-access review;
- later local journalism;
- a governor or organization communication.

The player may never discover the full condition during the proof.

### Investigation result

At least one valid investigation path must be capable of returning:

- delayed evidence;
- narrower-than-feared scope;
- conflicting reports;
- insufficient national evidence;
- or no useful finding.

It may not return hidden canonical truth merely because the player clicked `Investigate`.

# 10. Required concrete opportunity cost

The proof must contain at least one owner-specific collision that the player can understand before choosing.

Primary collision:

- OMB/economic analysis has two dated deliverables using the same named review team or queue;
- one supports the congressional employment initiative before committee action;
- one supports an urgent Housing implementation review;
- both cannot receive full review by their preferred deadlines under the current assignment order;
- the President/Chief of Staff may reprioritize, accept a narrower product, delegate, or let one deadline slip.

Consequences must trace to:

- the specific assignment/queue;
- evidence quality available at the later decision;
- congressional or implementation timing;
- commitments and actor responses.

Forbidden resolution:

```text
too many priorities
→ generic overextension penalty
```

A second optional collision may use one presidential calendar block requested by Senate leadership and affected governors, with Vice-Presidential or staff delegation as alternatives.

The proof passes opportunity cost only when one player choice measurably weakens, delays, narrows, or closes another real route.

# 11. Attention, briefing, and uncertainty behavior

## 11.1 Attention stop

An automatic stop requires a valid escalation/review record or reserved review threshold.

Every displayed Attention item answers:

- what happened or what duty/deadline exists;
- who received it;
- who escalated it;
- what the President knows;
- what remains uncertain;
- why presidential judgment/authority is requested;
- available routes and their authority;
- expiration/default;
- who resolves the downstream result.

## 11.2 Non-attention information

Country Watch and scheduled briefings may show observable developments that do not require action.

Examples:

- updated employment estimate;
- Housing project progression;
- new congressional hearing date;
- local media coverage;
- a monitoring gap;
- a delegated assignment completion.

Prominence on a screen does not make a fact more causally important.

## 11.3 Uncertainty display

The player-facing representation must distinguish, when material:

- canonical material condition versus available evidence;
- preliminary versus revised;
- official measurement versus staff forecast;
- exact versus modeled/bounded;
- stale/partial/missing;
- staff agreement versus disagreement;
- known cause versus disputed attribution;
- next expected evidence.

The proof must not reduce uncertainty to one decorative percentage.

## 11.4 Empty Attention

At least one scheduled briefing must truthfully display:

```text
Nothing currently requires presidential action.
```

The country, administration, Congress, agencies, states, media, and material domains continue moving.

The player may still investigate, inspect, reprioritize, contact actors, or advance.

- congressional engagement if formally adopted;
- lead offices;
- assignments;

The player can inspect:

- producer/source;
- as-of period;
- release/receipt date;
- vintage/status;
- geography/population scope;
- uncertainty/support;
- revision lineage;
- which office received or assessed it.

## 12.5 Actor/institution dossier

At least one lawmaker, governor, administration official, and media actor has a bounded dossier containing:

- role/office;
- public record;
- known communications/commitments;
- relevant geography/constituency;
- administration relationship history;
- staff assessment with basis and uncertainty.

No hidden motive or exact future choice appears.

## 12.6 Historical Record

The player can follow at least one chain from occurrence through evidence, decision, autonomous response, later revision, and consequence.

The Record distinguishes:

```text
what happened
what was known then
what was decided then
what happened later
what is known now
```

## 12.7 UI implementation limit

The proof may use one shell with tabs, panels, drawers, or linked views.

It does not need final art, final information architecture, animations, full maps, or commercial polish.

It must nevertheless be readable enough for an actual playtest. A console/log-only proof fails.

# 17. Required representative traces

The proof is not accepted from one ideal autoplay path.

At minimum, preserve reproducible traces for:

## Trace A — early intervention

- President treats employment deterioration as an active priority;
- requests faster analysis;
- engages governors/Congress;
- accepts risk from preliminary evidence;
- Housing review loses time or depth;
- later revision changes interpretation without rewriting the decision.

## Trace B — wait for evidence

- President delays major employment commitment;
- Congress and governors continue;
- a congressional window changes or partially closes;
- Housing receives greater attention;
- later evidence may vindicate or undermine the wait.

## Trace C — delegated/legislative route

- President delegates negotiation and focuses elsewhere;
- Legislative Affairs/VP acts within delegated bounds;
- Congress transforms or rejects administration terms independently;
- the President later inherits the resulting bill/coalition state.

## Trace D — quiet condition discovered

- player proactively requests an HHS/service-access review;
- the assignment returns delayed, bounded, or inconclusive evidence;
- discovery time is current, not backdated;
- the player may adopt a workstream or continue to defer.

## Trace E — quiet condition remains quiet

- player never investigates;
- no valid escalation arises;
- the condition and local actors continue coherently;
- no hidden director forces a presidential decision.

The traces may reconnect to shared later dates, but their records, relationships, evidence, opportunities, and workstreams must differ causally.

# 18. Playtest protocol

Automated correctness is necessary and insufficient.

## 18.1 Primary playtest

The project owner plays the proof through at least three materially different approaches without using repository/debug information.

Each session records:

- time to understand the opening;
- number and cause of automatic stops;
- proactive actions taken;
- decisions/defaults;
- times the player felt confused;
- times evidence/uncertainty changed a decision;
- whether advisers/offices felt distinct;
- whether consequences were explainable;
- whether the player wanted to advance again.

## 18.2 Cold legibility test

At least one person without repository knowledge should attempt the proof when reasonably available.

If unavailable, that absence is disclosed and the proof cannot claim cold-user legibility as established.

## 18.3 Qualitative pass questions

The proof should receive affirmative answers to most of the following, with no identity-level failure:

- Did I feel like I directed an administration rather than clicked a policy chain?
- Did the country appear to continue without me?
- Did lawmakers, governors, agencies, organizations, and media appear to have their own agency?
- Could I understand why something reached me?
- Did uncertainty create strategy rather than arbitrary fog?
- Could I act proactively rather than only clear an inbox?
- Did choosing one route cost me another specific opportunity?
- Did Housing feel like one inherited governing problem among several?
- Did advisers provide meaningfully different institutional views?
- Did I care enough to advance the calendar again?

The proof fails product-wise if the owner’s honest reaction remains:

> `I still would not play this.`

# 22. Final acceptance gate

A detached final review must answer:

> **Does the Presidential Operating Proof create a playable, understandable, causally honest experience of directing a United States administration through concurrent conditions and autonomous politics—without duplicate state, scripted sequencing, omniscient information, action-prefix attention, hidden fan-out, universal resources, or a second temporary game architecture—and does the player want to keep governing?**

## PASS requires

- all hard automated invariants pass;
- I10 remains intact;
- all four proof threads exist in one canonical world;
- three or more remain meaningfully concurrent;
- at least one major development originates outside the Presidency;
- at least one condition legitimately remains outside Attention;
- proactive investigation works without revealing truth;
- one evidence revision preserves historical knowledge;
- one specific opportunity-cost collision changes later options;
- recipient-owned responses remain independent;
- the player can explain what happened and what was known;
- the owner honestly wants to advance again.

## REVISE means

Repair only the failed operating, causal, persistence, legibility, or gameplay evidence. Do not respond by adding unrelated policy domains.

## REJECT means

The operating composition or experience is fundamentally wrong enough that continuing it would create product debt. Preserve the accepted engines, discard the failed coordinator/projection/UI as appropriate, and redesign the proof.
