The President/Chief of Staff may use accepted typed assignment/coordination mechanisms to reprioritize, accept a capability-authorized narrower product, delegate permitted work, or allow a deadline/default.

The result must be recorded in the OMB office queue and resulting product scope. There is no administration-wide capacity meter or generic overextension penalty.

The consequence is bounded:

- delayed/narrow employment analysis changes what Legislative Affairs can know/use before the Congress window but does not command Congress;
- delayed/narrow Housing analysis changes the administration/recipient route but does not directly mutate implementation or Housing state.

## 11.3 Same-instant ordering

`advanceTo(target)` processes every crossed boundary exactly once in this order at each instant:

1. accepted I3 escalation, reserved-review, assignment, recipient-response, and other deadline/default closures;
2. Congress/editorial/external-actor opportunity expiration, where expiration wins at the exact deadline;
3. material owner transitions for Employment, maternity service access, implementation, and Housing;
4. observation, evidence-version creation, and scheduled institutional release;
5. dispatch/delivery attempts and recipient-owned receipt admissions;
6. bounded actor/outlet assessments and autonomous decisions whose complete inputs are now effective;
7. resulting publication/external communication dispatch and exact recipient exposure/receipt;
8. office queue work that becomes eligible after prior phases.

An artifact released at the exact instant an opportunity expires is too late for that expired opportunity unless it was received in an earlier phase/instant explicitly permitted by configuration.

Coarse advancement, fine advancement, and save-before-boundary/load/continue must converge byte-for-byte for identical choices and inputs.
