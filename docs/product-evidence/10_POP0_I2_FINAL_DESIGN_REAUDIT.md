# 2. R2-01 is closed: every individualized human has an honest Population relationship

The repaired contract declares exactly seven individualized humans and assigns every one the same bounded I2 linkage status:

```text
OUTSIDE_MODELED_ORDINARY_POPULATION_SCOPE
```

The declaration is explicitly limited to the I2 proof because I2 composes no canonical Population owner.

Every declaration is authenticated configuration content containing:

- actor identity;
- effective interval;
- declared proof population scope;
- bounded proof support;
- configured synthetic provenance;
- zero Population weight.

The contract permits only the relationships I2 actually uses:

- actor identity;
- officeholder assignment;
- assessment authorship;
- office/institution information routing;
- presidential-recipient binding.

It prohibits unsupported I2 joins to:

- residence;
- household;
- demographic state;
- material exposure;
- ordinary-population receipt or belief;
- electorate/voter state;
- eligibility;
- personal lifecycle state.

Validation must reject missing, duplicate, overlapping, gapped, temporally invalid, nonzero-weight, unknown-status, or join-expanding declarations.

The contract also prohibits a later implementation from silently changing these actors into ordinary Population members. A later linkage requires separately reviewed transition and reconciliation semantics, preserves actor identity, adds no duplicate person weight, and cannot fabricate earlier residence, household, material, belief, eligibility, or lifecycle history.

The original free-floating-special-person exploit is therefore closed.

The declaration does not claim that the fictional officials are literally outside the United States. It states honestly that their ordinary-population identities are not represented in this bounded implementation increment.

# 3. C2-02 is closed: federal institution possession is not leadership-office knowledge

The repaired contract now distinguishes:

```text
pop0.institution.department-of-labor
→ source-institution possession

pop0.office.secretary-of-labor
→ leadership-office receipt and assessment
```

and separately:

```text
pop0.institution.department-of-housing-and-urban-development

pop0.office.secretary-of-hud
```

The institution identities are not operating offices and do not receive officeholder assignments or queues.

Department of Labor possession does not grant the Secretary office or Naomi Mercer substantive knowledge. The leadership office must receive the permitted sections through an explicit institution-to-office route before its holder may author the assessment.

The deterministic worked path and acceptance tests now require:

- Department possession without Secretary receipt;
- separate metadata notice and access;
- separate technical retrieval;
- separate substantive office receipt;
- effective officeholder authorship afterward.

This prevents a department-sized shared mind while remaining appropriately narrow for I2.

# 4. C2-03 is closed: the route ledger is infrastructure, not cognition

The repaired contract explicitly defines the information-route ledger as:

- noncognitive;
- append-only storage and occurrence infrastructure;
- not an actor, office, possessor, recipient, analyst, or White House memory;
- incapable of authoring assessments, syntheses, priorities, or presentations.

Semantic possession belongs to the named institution.

Semantic receipt belongs to one named office and exact content scope.

Every load-bearing transition and query must provide an explicit institution or office/officeholder-assignment context and permitted scope.

Global serialization does not create global accessibility.

Recipient-local projections may derive only from records addressed to that recipient. No actor, office, future UI, or assessment process may enumerate every stored artifact merely because the ledger contains them.

The acceptance tests now require recipient-scoped query isolation and reject background preload, global serialization, and UI flags as knowledge.

The central storage shape therefore does not become a hidden shared White House mind.

# 5. C2-04 is closed: role timing and cross-role access are explicit

The repaired contract uses start-inclusive/end-exclusive role intervals.

An assessment, synthesis, or presidential presentation is valid only when:

- the cited officeholder assignment is effective at the event time;
- the assignment belongs to the producing or presenting office;
- the presidential recipient binding is effective at the presentation time.

The presidential binding identifies:

- one binding identity;
- constitutional office identity;
- actor identity;
- effective interval;
- provenance.

It explicitly does not create:

- `ControlBinding`;
- player authority;
- executive entitlement;
- office information access;
- general presidential runtime state.

The configured proof uses seven distinct actors. An alternate multi-role fixture must prove that one actor holding more than one office does not merge office entitlements, receipts, records, queues, assignments, or authorship authority.

Because I2 has no person-memory owner, it may not infer personal cross-role knowledge.

This closes the timing and role-context ambiguity without importing later control, succession, or memory systems.

# 6. C2-05 is closed: disagreement is causal proof content, not office-ID behavior

The repaired contract explicitly prohibits general runtime logic equivalent to:

```text
if Labor office → skeptical
if NEC office → concerned
if OMB office → blocked
```

The configured judgments remain bounded proof-fixture content carried by typed office-owned assessment acts.

Their validity and support derive from:

- the exact receipt scope;
- declared analytical assumptions;
- current entitlement and route state;
- effective author role;
- typed act provenance.

The implementation must prove the path with counterfactuals:

1. remove or expire NEC access/receipt → NEC cannot author the same evidence-grounded assessment;
2. remove the supplier-spillover assumption → the same NEC conclusion cannot remain supported by office identity;
3. withhold a source assessment from Chief of Staff → synthesis cannot cite or imply it;
4. give OMB metadata only → OMB cannot claim substantive evidence analysis;
5. reorder independent notice/queue operations → deterministic state remains and no replacement assessment or dramatic event appears.

These tests establish route and provenance integrity without requiring a general staff-AI system in I2.

# 7. Unchanged full-gate review

## 7.1 One canonical world

The contract adds four direct owner-state families beside the accepted calendar owner:

- administration directory;
- partitioned office operations;
- information-route ledger;
- presidential presentation history.

Configuration owns immutable proof identities and actor linkage declarations.

No second Population, I10 session, UI-owned inbox, parallel knowledge set, or duplicate historical truth is introduced.

## 7.2 Offices remain independent

Assignments and queues are keyed to offices.

Each assignment has one lead office and one canonical record.

One office's queue mutation cannot reorder another's queue.

No administration-wide workload or capacity truth exists.

## 7.3 Knowledge ladder remains closed

The contract distinguishes:

```text
artifact existence
≠ institution possession
≠ White House index entry
≠ office metadata notice
≠ access entitlement
≠ technical retrieval
≠ substantive office receipt
≠ assessment
≠ synthesis
≠ presidential presentation
```

No earlier state automatically creates a later one.

## 7.4 Assessment and synthesis remain bounded

Assessments retain producing office, effective author assignment, received evidence scope, proposition judgments, claimed confidence, evidentiary support, assumptions, limitations, recommendation, and provenance.

Synthesis requires separate Chief-of-Staff receipts and preserves the source assessments and disagreement instead of overwriting them.

## 7.5 Presidential presentation remains bounded

The President receives only explicitly identified portions of the Chief-of-Staff synthesis.

Referenced attachments and source assessments remain unseen unless separately presented later.

Presentation history is append-only and distinct from read/unread UI state.

## 7.6 Persistence remains atomic

The contract requires all new owner state and configuration relationships to enter atomically through:

- types;
- factory;
- validation;
- deep-copy surface;
- save envelope;
- exact-shape parser;
- restoration;
- round-trip and tamper tests.

Five nontrivial save checkpoints must preserve route, queue, assessment, synthesis, and presentation distinctions exactly.

I1 saves may not silently acquire invented I2 history.

## 7.7 I3+ remains excluded

Still absent from I2 design scope:

- Presidential Attention and escalation;
- player decisions or commands;
- administration workstreams;
- presidential instruments and dispatch;
- Housing integration;
- employment simulation;
- Congress behavior;
- governors, organizations, media, public belief, or healthcare conditions;
- recipient response behavior;
- playable UI;
- final staff personalities or general staff AI;
- production migration or `main` changes.

# 8. Nonblocking implementation watchpoints

These do not block coding, but they become implementation-review targets.

## 8.1 Outside-scope linkage must remain genuinely restrictive

The implementation must not add convenience getters or joins that let the seven actors acquire residence, voter, material, public-belief, or other ordinary-population facts while still claiming outside-scope status.

## 8.2 Recipient scoping must be enforced by APIs and tests

The ledger contract cannot rely only on caller discipline. Production-shaped route/query operations and structural tests must make cross-office enumeration or access impossible without an explicit permitted route.

## 8.3 Typed assessment acts are not accepted staff AI

I2 proves ownership, provenance, support, disagreement, and information routing. Configured typed assessment acts must not later be advertised as autonomous adviser reasoning or allowed to become permanent office-ID story scripting.

## 8.4 Full canonical state remains non-player-facing

I1's full-state inspection remains shell/test evidence only. I2 must not introduce UI or another production-facing full-state consumer.
