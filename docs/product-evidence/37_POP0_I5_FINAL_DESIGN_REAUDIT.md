# 2. Findings closed

## D5-01 — presidential act origination and local option model — CLOSED

The accepted I3/I4 `REQUEST_SCOPED_ANALYSIS_AND_COORDINATION` option remains exact and unchanged: two previews in order, analysis then coordination.

I5 adds exactly three single-preview escalation-local option kinds for legislative position, intergovernmental contact, and public statement. Rule-local option sets and permitted instrument kinds are explicitly closed; no arbitrary payload array or global action catalogue is authorized.

The proactive HHS path no longer requires a fabricated quiet-condition escalation. `PresidentialInquiryOwnerState` provides a narrowly scoped, persisted HHS-only inquiry opportunity and one-preview `REQUEST_OFFICE_ANALYSIS` authorization path. It requires active ControlBinding and exact preview/hash equality, creates no Attention or HHS result directly, and contains no hidden facility/catchment/service facts.

## D5-02 — OMB queue collision ownership/capacity — CLOSED

The contract now defines one OMB-only canonical review-capacity state under the OMB office, one named team, nine exact nonoverlapping work periods, exact full/narrow product consumption requirements, immutable bookings, deterministic booking rules, deadline/default behavior, and restore validation.

Direct queue reordering is explicitly internal plumbing only. Product-authorized reprioritization requires either an OMB-received presidential coordination instrument or a typed Chief-of-Staff-to-OMB request under one exact standing authority. OMB remains the owner that records the immutable queue-reprioritization occurrence.

Narrowing is no longer an in-place mutation of a full assignment. It requires an exact scope-contained replacement assignment and immutable supersession occurrence while the original remains immutable and `SUPERSEDED`.

## D5-03 — administration, human linkage, and capability expansion — CLOSED

The administration expansion is exact and closed at:

- 3 institutions;
- 9 administration offices;
- 10 administration actor IDs;
- 9 officeholder assignments;
- 10 administration Population-linkage declarations.

Accepted I4 identities and meanings are retained. The I5 capability union and exact nine-record configured set are explicit, including HHS analysis, Legislative Affairs position, Intergovernmental Affairs contact, Communications statement, and OMB queue coordination.

A single authenticated configuration-level external-human identity/linkage registry owns the identity/linkage declaration source for congressional, gubernatorial, and supporting lower-legislative humans outside the administration directory. It is not a Population owner and rejects duplicates, mismatches, and incompatible linkages.

## D5-04 — Congress adapter ownership seam — CLOSED

The contract now chooses the gate-only seam.

Before runtime creation, the outer `congressionalInitiative` owner owns formation opportunity/decision only. After runtime creation, `LegislativeRuntimeState` alone owns sponsorship acceptance, proposal state, lower political actor decisions, consideration gates, amendments/votes if reached, native deadlines, and terminal disposition.

Outer receipts/assessments may only determine whether a lower transition may be attempted. They do not store a second sponsorship/procedure result. The outer `CongressionalProcedureOpportunity` owns only availability/deadline, and exact deadline expiration prevents a lower call without rewriting lower procedure truth.
