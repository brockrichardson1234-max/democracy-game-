# 1. Accepted I2 question

POP0-I2 is authorized to answer only:

> **Can different administration offices possess, retrieve, assess, disagree about, synthesize, and present information without becoming one mind?**

The implementation candidate must prove this in one direct POP operating world while retaining the accepted I1 shell and I10 regression boundary.

Passing I2 does not establish playable presidential gameplay. It establishes the administration-information substrate later Attention and decision systems may consume.

# 2. Authorized proof identities

I2 may configure exactly the bounded proof identities established by the repaired contract.

## 2.1 Operating offices

- Chief of Staff / Presidential Operations;
- National Economic Council;
- Office of Management and Budget;
- Legislative Affairs;
- Office of the Secretary of Labor;
- Office of the Secretary of HUD.

Each office retains independent identity, records, assignments, queue state, access, receipts, and authorship context.

## 2.2 Institution identities

- Department of Labor;
- Department of Housing and Urban Development.

These are institution identities, not operating-office aliases.

Department possession may not become leadership-office knowledge without an explicit route and receipt.

## 2.3 Individualized humans

```text

```

for the I2 effective interval.

Every actor contributes zero Population weight and has no I2 residence, household, demographic, material-exposure, ordinary-population belief, electorate, eligibility, or personal-lifecycle joins.

No Population owner may be introduced merely to satisfy these declarations.

# 3. Authorized canonical owner state

I2 may extend the I1 operating world with exactly four bounded owner-state families:

1. **Administration directory**
   - officeholder assignments;
   - effective intervals;
   - presidential recipient binding;
   - no office records, queues, assessments, or presentation content.

2. **Partitioned office operations**
   - office-owned assignments;
   - independent ordered queues;
   - deadlines/default records;
   - no global administration queue or capacity truth.

3. **Information-route ledger**
   - immutable evidence and assessment artifacts;
   - institution-possession occurrences;
   - index/metadata notices;
   - access entitlements;
   - retrieval requests/results;
   - recipient-scoped substantive office receipts;
   - revision/supersession lineage.

   The ledger is noncognitive infrastructure. It is not an actor, possessor, analyst, shared inbox, or White House mind.

4. **Presidential presentation history**
   - immutable records of exactly which configured portions were presented;
   - presenter and recipient role validity;
   - referenced-but-unseen attachments;
   - presentation provenance and deduplication;
   - no Attention, read/unread truth, decision, action, or workstream.

These owner families serialize directly inside the POP save. No opaque I10 save, synchronized second world, or UI-owned substantive state is permitted.

# 4. Authorized information and office operations

I2 may implement typed operations only for:

- office and officeholder configuration/validation;
- institution identity and source possession;
- office assignments and independent queue operations;
- metadata indexing and notice;
- office-scoped access entitlement;
- technical retrieval and explicit failure;
- separate substantive office receipt;
- immutable assessment authoring from valid receipt scope;
- office-to-office assessment transfer;
- provenance-preserving Chief-of-Staff synthesis;
- one bounded presidential presentation;
- revision/supersession links;
- direct save, parse, copy, validate, and restore behavior.

The canonical information sequence remains:

```text
artifact exists
≠ institution possesses it
≠ White House metadata/index exists
≠ office receives notice
≠ office has access
≠ technical retrieval succeeds
≠ substantive office receipt occurs
≠ assessment is authored
≠ synthesis exists
≠ President receives a bounded presentation
```

No step automatically creates the next.

# 5. Required I2 evidence path

The candidate implementation must demonstrate the repaired contract's bounded trace:

1. Department of Labor possesses one configured synthetic evidence artifact.
2. The Secretary of Labor office, NEC, and OMB receive separate metadata notices.
3. Secretary of Labor and NEC possess valid scoped access; OMB does not.
4. Secretary and NEC technical retrievals remain distinct from later substantive receipts.
5. OMB's attempted retrieval is denied and its independent assignment may become blocked from that actual route state.
6. Secretary of Labor and NEC author materially different assessments over the same evidence version, with the NEC conclusion additionally requiring a declared supplier-spillover assumption.
7. Chief of Staff separately receives both assessment artifacts.
8. Chief of Staff authors a synthesis that preserves both source judgments and their limitations.
9. The configured President receives only the exact presentation portions authorized by the contract; source attachments remain unseen.

This is a typed proof trace. It is not autonomous politics, staff AI, a player interaction, a dramatic schedule, or a final briefing system.

# 6. Required counterfactual and hostile tests

The implementation must prove that the trace arises from owner state, role, access, and typed operations rather than office-ID scripting.

At minimum:

- missing or expired NEC access/receipt prevents the same evidence-grounded NEC assessment;
- removing the supplier-spillover assumption prevents the same NEC conclusion from remaining supported;
- withholding either assessment from Chief of Staff prevents synthesis from citing or implying it;
- OMB metadata without substantive receipt permits no substantive evidence assessment;
- Department possession alone creates no Secretary-office receipt or officeholder knowledge;
- a multi-role actor fixture does not merge office records, queues, entitlements, or receipts;
- invalid officeholder or presidential-role intervals reject authorship/presentation;
- recipient-scoped queries cannot enumerate another office's records or the entire ledger;
- global serialization, preload, and UI-like flags create no knowledge;
- reordered independent operations remain deterministic and create no replacement assessment or story event.

Configured assessment content may not be implemented through general logic equivalent to:

```text
if office == Labor → skeptical
if office == NEC → concerned
if office == OMB → blocked
```

# 7. Persistence and restoration gate

I2 must advance the POP runtime/save version and add all new state atomically to:

- runtime and configuration types;
- factory construction;
- validation and referential checks;
- defensive/deep-copy surface;
- save envelope;
- exact-shape parser;
- restoration;
- round-trip tests;
- tamper tests.

The implementation must prove at least these nontrivial checkpoints:

1. metadata notice exists before retrieval;
2. NEC technical retrieval exists before substantive receipt;
3. NEC and OMB have different independent queue states;
4. disagreement exists before synthesis;
5. bounded presidential presentation exists afterward.

At each checkpoint:

- restored state equals uninterrupted state;
- save → load → save is byte-stable;
- loading appends no occurrence;
- loading reruns no retrieval, assessment, synthesis, presentation, or random choice;
- returned state is defensively copied;
- identical subsequent operations produce identical state.

Unless a separately reviewed deterministic migration is included within the I2 candidate, I1-format saves must be rejected rather than silently acquiring invented I2 history.
