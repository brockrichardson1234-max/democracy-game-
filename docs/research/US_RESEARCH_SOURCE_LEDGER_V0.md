## Excluded or bounded source observations

- The current House explanatory page includes a stale sentence about adoption of House rules. Current-rule claims therefore rely on HMAN-119, not that sentence.
- The Senate classes page includes stale example expiration years. Structural class claims rely on the Constitution and only use the page's enduring class explanation.
- National Archives and USAGov sometimes call Maine and Nebraska's method “proportional.” Packet A records the actual district-plus-two-at-large structure and does not infer proportional allocation in the mathematical sense.
- Preliminary U.S. Code pages carry explicit “laws in effect through” dates. Those dates are retained above; mutable P0 legal dependencies must be revalidated at the later evidence freeze under the accepted program.
