## Geography and identifier facts carried by the claims

The following details summarize USR-CLM-0073–0078 and their dependencies without creating configuration values:

- The contemporary union contains 50 states; the Census state-code reference provides separate stable identifiers for each and a separate entry for the District of Columbia (USR-SRC-0028).
- Census 119th-congressional-district products represent state-submitted district plans, identify the congressional-session vintage, and distinguish whole-block tabulation representations from boundary geometry where a plan splits a Census block (USR-SRC-0029).
- A boundary used for a future election can differ from the boundary governing current representation until the next Congress begins (USR-SRC-0007). Geography therefore requires an effective-time/vintage association rather than a timeless district label.
- Senate representation is state-based, while House representation is district-based and electoral-vote allocation combines state/DC allocation with congressional apportionment. These relationships do not make a jurisdiction and its geometry the same fact.
